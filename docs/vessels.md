# Vessels and pouring

Vessels hold something to drink, or carry it: a tankard, a cup or a waterskin. Like a [cask](casks), each holds one liquid at a time, such as water, oil, juice or cordial, and what is in it adds to its weight, about a kilogram a litre. A vessel's popup says how much it holds.

## Kinds

| Vessel | Holds | Empty | Takes up | Sold by |
| ------ | ----- | ----- | -------- | ------- |
| Goblet | 0.2 litres | about 250 g | 1 cell | Jewellers |
| Cup | 0.25 litres | about 200 g | 1 cell | Carpenters |
| Drinking Horn | 0.3 litres | about 250 g | 1 × 2 cells | Leatherworkers |
| Tankard | 0.5 litres | about 450 g | 1 cell | Carpenters |
| Flask | 0.5 litres | about 300 g | 1 cell | Leatherworkers |
| Wine Bottle | 0.75 litres | about 500 g | 1 × 2 cells | Herbalists |
| Jug | 1.5 litres | about 1.1 kg | 2 × 2 cells | Herbalists |
| Waterskin | 2 litres | about 300 g | 2 × 2 cells | Leatherworkers |

A goblet is pewter on a stem; a cup and a jug are earthenware; a tankard is pewter with a lid; a drinking horn is a cow's horn with a metal rim; a flask is leather over pewter; a waterskin is sewn leather. A wine bottle is tall, dark green glass with a cork, much bigger than a potion's bottle.

## The tap

A **Tap** is a brass spigot, knocked into a cask to draw from it. Carpenters sell them. You need one with you to pour anything out of a cask; pouring into a cask needs none.

## Pouring

Pick up a cask, vessel or bottle and let go of it on another one to pour into it. As much goes as there is, up to what the other takes. Either one can be in your inventory, or the one you pour from can be on the ground beside you, such as a cask too heavy to carry.

- A liquid only goes into something empty or holding the same: water won't go into a jug of oil. Juice and water are the exception: they make cordial (below).
- Potions stay in their bottles and can't be poured.
- A bottle is filled or emptied whole: it takes a bottle's worth, about 0.1 litres, or nothing.
- Something red under your clothes says why when a pour can't be done.

## Sealed and opened

A new cask is **sealed**. It is opened once anything is poured out of it or into it, or water is collected into it. A wine bottle or a bottle filled straight from a cask starts sealed too, and opens when poured from. The popup says which. A sealed cask never spoils; an opened drink turns to vinegar in time (see [Drinks](drinks)).

## Juice and cordial

**Press Juice** presses fruit into juice with an oil press, about two kilograms at a time, into a cask or vessel you carry that is empty or already holds that juice. It keeps pressing until the fruit runs out, the cask or vessel is full, or you move. Juicy fruit gives about 0.7 litres a kilogram; dry fruit such as dates, and nuts, can't be juiced. It practises Alchemy.

Pour water into juice, or juice into water, to make **cordial**: never more water than juice, so a cup of juice makes at most two cups of cordial. Salt water won't mix.

## Drinking

Let go of a vessel on yourself to drink from it: a cup's worth at a time, or what is left. The vessel stays where it was. A bottle of water, juice or cordial is drunk whole, and you get the empty bottle back.

| Drink | A cup's worth gives |
| ----- | ------------------- |
| Water | Nothing yet |
| Juice | 1 Mana a second for 30 seconds |
| Cordial | 1 Mana a second for 20 seconds |

A smaller drink lasts less long. Only your latest juice or cordial counts. Oil can't be drunk.
