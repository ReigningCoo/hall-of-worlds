# Brewing

Brewing turns [herbs](herbs) into potions, in four steps: fill a bottle with water, press an oil, grind herbs and the oil into an ointment, then brew the ointment with the water. Each step is an ability on the Abilities page (B), and the last three need a tool carried. The **Alchemy** skill makes every step better, and **Herbalism** helps with the herbs.

## Tools

| Tool | Used for | Sold by |
| ---- | -------- | ------- |
| Oil Press | Press Oil | Carpenters |
| Mortar and Pestle | Grind | Herbalists and potion shops |
| Cauldron | Brew | Blacksmiths |

An ability whose tool you don't carry is greyed out. A tool in a bag counts.

## Waters

Herbalists and potion shops sell bottles of water. The first time you have an empty bottle, you learn **Collect Water**: face water close by (a pond, a river, the sea) and use it to fill your empty bottles one after another, and then any [cask](casks) you carry, until you move or are hurt. Practice in the **Water** skill makes it quicker.

Where you fill a bottle decides which water you get:

| Water | Where | What it does to a brew |
| ----- | ----- | ---------------------- |
| Water | Anywhere else | Nothing |
| Cave Water | Underground: caves and cellars | Makes harmful effects stronger |
| Moon Water | Outside at night, with a moon in the sky | Makes helpful effects stronger |
| Salt Water | The sea | Takes away the strongest helpful effect |

## Oils

**Press Oil** fills empty bottles with oil from what you carry, about ten handfuls a bottle.

| Oil | Pressed from | Strengthens |
| --- | ------------ | ----------- |
| Vegetable Oil | Vegetables and grain | Strength, Toughness, Agility and Health |
| Nut Oil | Nuts | Intellect, Perception, Will and Mana |
| Seed Oil | Seeds, beans and herbs used for their seeds | Presence, Empathy and Aura |

Herbalists and potion shops sell oils as well.

## Grinding an ointment

**Grind** opens a panel listing the herbs you carry. Tick the herbs to use (one of each kind counts), choose a bottle of oil and how many doses to make. Each dose uses one of every herb ticked; one bottle of oil makes the whole batch, and the bottle comes back empty.

- A herb's effects add up, and opposite effects cancel out.
- How strong each herb's effects are depends on its quality and your Herbalism.
- Alchemy lets you mix more kinds of herb, make more doses at once and reach stronger effects.
- Herbalism trims away effects you don't want.

The panel tells you what the ointment will hold once you know every effect of the herbs ticked. The mixes you have made before are listed below, to make again with one press. A mix that cancels out to nothing still uses up its herbs and oil.

You can rub an ointment on yourself, as you eat food, for a quarter of its strength.

## Brewing a potion

**Brew** opens a panel to choose an ointment and a bottle of water, and says what they would make. Brewing uses one ointment and turns the water in that bottle into the potion. A potion forms only when one of its effects is strong enough; weak effects are left out. If no potion forms, the ointment and the water are both lost, and the bottle comes back empty.

The purer a potion, the better its quality: one strong effect makes a better potion than several of the same strength. A better potion lasts longer.

Brewing a potion teaches you the effects of the herbs in its ointment on what the potion does, so you learn more about herbs as you brew.

## Drinking a potion

Drink a potion as you eat food: let go of it on yourself in the Character panel, or put it on the action bar and press its key. The bottle comes back empty, into your bags, or onto the ground if there is no room.

Health and Mana come at once. Other effects last a while: 2 minutes for a common potion, longer for a better one and shorter for a poor one. Each thing a potion changes is a separate effect, so a new potion's effect on, say, Strength replaces the old one's, while the old potion's other effects carry on.

After drinking a potion, you must wait 30 seconds before drinking another that changes the same things the same way, however strong it is. A potion that changes something else can be drunk at once.

A slot on the action bar holds one particular potion, and shows how many of it you carry.

## Practice

Every step practises its skill, whatever comes of it: Collect Water the Water skill, Press Oil, Grind and Brew Alchemy, and Grind and Brew Herbalism too.
