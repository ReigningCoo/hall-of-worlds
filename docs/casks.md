# Casks

A cask is a wooden barrel bound with iron hoops. It holds one liquid at a time, by the litre: water, an oil, juice, a drink or vinegar. Carpenters sell them.

## Sizes

Casks come in eight sizes, the old brewers' casks, each about twice the one before. Big casks are very heavy: the larger ones are hard to carry at all.

| Size | Holds | Empty, in oak | Full of water |
| ---- | ----- | ------------- | ------------- |
| Pin | 20 litres | about 8 kg | about 28 kg |
| Firkin | 40 litres | about 14 kg | about 54 kg |
| Kilderkin | 80 litres | about 24 kg | about 104 kg |
| Barrel | 160 litres | about 45 kg | about 205 kg |
| Hogshead | 240 litres | about 60 kg | about 300 kg |
| Puncheon | 320 litres | about 80 kg | about 400 kg |
| Butt | 480 litres | about 105 kg | about 585 kg |
| Tun | 960 litres | about 150 kg | about 1,110 kg |

A litre of anything in a cask weighs about a kilogram.

## Woods

| Wood | Looks | Weight |
| ---- | ----- | ------ |
| Oak | Mid brown | As above |
| Chestnut | Red-brown | A little lighter |
| Acacia | Yellowish brown | A little heavier |
| Cherry | Deep red-brown | A little lighter |
| Pine | Pale | Lightest |

Oak is the commonest. The wood matters for drinks ageing in the cask: see [Drinks](drinks).

## Filling a cask

**Collect Water** (see [Brewing](brewing)) fills your empty bottles first. Once none are left, it pours into a cask you carry, about 1 to 2 litres a second as your **Water** skill grows, until the cask is full or you move or are hurt. A cask takes only one liquid: water from the sea won't go into a cask of moon water.

A cask's popup says how much it holds, and whether it is still sealed. A cask on the ground shows from afar, standing where it was left.

## Drawing from a cask

To pour out of a cask you need a **Tap** with you; then let go of the cask on a vessel or bottle, or pick it up from the ground beside you and let go of it on one you carry. A cask is sealed until something is poured into or out of it. See [Vessels and pouring](vessels).
