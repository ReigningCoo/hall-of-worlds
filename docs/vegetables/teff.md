## Flour

![Sack of Teff Flour]({{ '/assets/flour/teff.png' | relative_url }})

A sheaf of teff mills into a sack of teff flour weighing 29 g: 95% of the sheaf, ground whole between millstones. Sacks of the same flour stack, and flour never goes stale. Town millers sell flour of the grains grown near them; milling your own sheaves is planned.
