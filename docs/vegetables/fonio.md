## Flour

![Sack of Fonio Flour]({{ '/assets/flour/fonio.png' | relative_url }})

A sheaf of fonio mills into a sack of fonio flour weighing 21 g: 70% of the sheaf, ground whole between millstones. Sacks of the same flour stack, and flour never goes stale. Town millers sell flour of the grains grown near them; milling your own sheaves is planned.
