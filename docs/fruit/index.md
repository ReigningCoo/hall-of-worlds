# Fruit and nuts

Fruit and nuts are food you pick from plants: berries from low bushes, apples and pears from trees, dates from palms, melons from vines along the ground. Each kind has its own page, with its picture, where it grows and what eating it does. Back to [Items](items).

## Finding and picking

Fruit plants grow wild only very rarely, in lands that suit them: a fruit of the tundra won't grow in the desert. Farms grow them in orchards, in rows, choosing whatever suits the warmth of the land; some fruit grows only in orchards and never wild.

A plant starts young, and then goes round and round: leafy, flowering, then fruiting, the fruit green at first and then ripe. After many rounds it dies and a young one takes its place. Click or tap a plant beside you when its fruit is ripe to pick it. Picking starts the round again from its leaves, so a plant takes time to bear again.

How many you get depends on the plant, and your **Foraging** and **Orchards** skills find more.

Trees, palms, giant herbs and tall cacti have trunks, which stand in your way as wild trees do, whatever stage they are at.

## Eating

Drag fruit onto your clothes, as you would other food, to eat one. It gives back Health a little at a time, until you move or are hurt: the more filling it is, the longer it lasts, and a few fruits heal for longer than most. Some also raise or lower an attribute, or restore or drain Health or Mana, for as long. The item's popup says what it does, and the effect shows in the bar at the top.

A few fruits and nuts are **harmful raw**: eaten as they are, they give back no Health.

## Big fruits

Small fruits and nuts come by the handful, and fruits about the size of an apple one at a time; the same kind stacks. A big fruit, such as a watermelon, a coconut or a jackfruit, comes whole and takes more room. When you eat it, it is cut into servings: you eat one, and the rest go back into your bags as slices (or onto the ground, if there is no room).

## Herbs that bear fruit
