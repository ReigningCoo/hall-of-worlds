# Spears

A short blade or point on the end of a long shaft, for thrusting. Back to [Weapons](../).

## Plain names

A weapon of this family that matches none of the named ones below goes by a plain name for its lengths.

| Name | Blade | Handle |
| ---- | ----- | ------ |
| Stabbing Spear | 20 to 30 cm | 60 to 70 cm |
| Short Spear | 10 to 35 cm | 100 to 170 cm |
| Spear | 5 to 35 cm | 170 to 280 cm |
| Pike | 8 to 35 cm | 280 to 680 cm |
| Polearm | 35 to 60 cm | 100 to 280 cm |

## Named spears

Each has its own page. Lengths are those it can be made in.

| Name | Blade | Handle | What sets it apart |
| ---- | ----- | ------ | ------------------ |
| [Assegai](assegai) | 20 to 30 cm | 60 to 70 cm | A short stabbing spear with a long head. |
| [Harpoon](harpoon) | 10 to 20 cm | 120 to 200 cm | A spear with a barbed head. |
| [Javelin](javelin) | 5 to 15 cm | 170 to 220 cm | A light spear made to be thrown. |
| [Lance](lance) | 8 to 15 cm | 250 to 390 cm | A very long spear with a guard for the hand, made for charging. |
| [Pike](pike) | 15 to 25 cm | 280 to 680 cm | A very long spear, held in two hands. |
| [Pilum](pilum) | 25 to 35 cm | 100 to 140 cm | A throwing spear with a small head on a long iron shank. |
| [Sarissa](sarissa) | 20 to 35 cm | 450 to 680 cm | The longest spear of all, held in two hands. |
| [Trident](trident) | 15 to 25 cm | 120 to 200 cm | A spear with three tines. |
| [Yari](yari) | 15 to 60 cm | 100 to 600 cm | A straight-headed spear, sometimes with blades at the side. |
