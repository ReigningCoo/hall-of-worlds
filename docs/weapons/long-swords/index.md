# Long swords

Long and great blades on a long grip, held in both hands or either. Back to [Weapons](../).

## Plain names

A weapon of this family that matches none of the named ones below goes by a plain name for its lengths.

| Name | Blade | Handle |
| ---- | ----- | ------ |
| Longsword | 75 to 100 cm | 20 to 40 cm |
| Greatsword | 100 to 150 cm | 20 to 40 cm |

## Named long swords

Each has its own page. Lengths are those it can be made in.

| Name | Blade | Handle | What sets it apart |
| ---- | ----- | ------ | ------------------ |
| [Bastard Sword](bastard-sword) | 75 to 95 cm | 20 to 28 cm | A long sword held in one hand or two. |
| [Claymore](claymore) | 100 to 115 cm | 30 to 36 cm | A great two-handed sword whose guard slopes towards the blade. |
| [Odachi](odachi) | 90 to 120 cm | 30 to 40 cm | A very long, curved blade with one edge, for two hands. |
| [Zweihänder](zweihander) | 100 to 150 cm | 30 to 40 cm | A huge two-handed sword with a blunt stretch above the guard and hooks to parry with. |
