# Knives

Short blades on a grip for one hand, mostly with one edge: tools as much as weapons. Back to [Weapons](../).

## Plain names

A weapon of this family that matches none of the named ones below goes by a plain name for its lengths. A short blade on a grip is a Knife if it has one edge, and a Dagger if it has two or only a point.

| Name | Blade | Handle |
| ---- | ----- | ------ |
| Knife or Dagger | 8 to 35 cm | 8 to 20 cm |
| Short Sword | 35 to 60 cm | 10 to 20 cm |
| Sword | 60 to 91 cm | 10 to 20 cm |

## Named knives

Each has its own page. Lengths are those it can be made in.

| Name | Blade | Handle | What sets it apart |
| ---- | ----- | ------ | ------------------ |
| [Bowie Knife](bowie-knife) | 24 to 30 cm | 10 to 15 cm | A large knife with a clipped point and a cross guard. |
| [Combat Knife](combat-knife) | 15 to 20 cm | 10 to 13 cm | A soldier's straight knife with a cross guard. |
| [Fighting Knife](fighting-knife) | 15 to 25 cm | 10 to 13 cm | A straight, tapering knife with two edges and a cross guard, made for fighting. |
| [Hunting Knife](hunting-knife) | 10 to 20 cm | 10 to 13 cm | A short, slightly curved knife for skinning and cutting up game. |

| [Machete](machete) | 30 to 66 cm | 10 to 15 cm | A wide, heavy blade for chopping through growth, with no guard. |

| [Throwing Knife](throwing-knife) | 15 to 30 cm | 8 to 12 cm | A plain, balanced blade with two edges and hardly a handle, made to be thrown. |
