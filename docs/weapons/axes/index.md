# Axes

A head at the side of a haft, cutting with its edge. Their blade is the edge of the head, measured along it. Back to [Weapons](../).

## Plain names

A weapon of this family that matches none of the named ones below goes by a plain name for its lengths.

| Name | Edge of the head | Handle |
| ---- | ----- | ------ |
| Hatchet | 6 to 15 cm | 25 to 60 cm |
| Axe | 6 to 15 cm | 60 to 100 cm |
| Battle Axe | 15 to 30 cm | 60 to 120 cm |
| Great Axe | 20 to 30 cm | 120 to 170 cm |
