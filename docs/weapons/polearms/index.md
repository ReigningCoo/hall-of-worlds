# Polearms

A long blade, or an axe's head, on a pole, held in both hands. Back to [Weapons](../).

## Plain names

A weapon of this family that matches none of the named ones below goes by a plain name for its lengths.

| Name | Blade or edge | Handle |
| ---- | ----- | ------ |
| Short Spear | 15 to 35 cm | 100 to 170 cm |
| Spear | 15 to 35 cm | 170 to 240 cm |
| Polearm | 35 to 60 cm | 100 to 250 cm |
| Great Axe | 15 to 35 cm | 120 to 180 cm |
| Polearm | 15 to 70 cm | 120 to 270 cm |
