# Swords

Long blades on a grip, mostly for one hand, straight or curved, with one edge or two. Back to [Weapons](../).

## Plain names

A weapon of this family that matches none of the named ones below goes by a plain name for its lengths.

| Name | Blade | Handle |
| ---- | ----- | ------ |
| Short Sword | 45 to 60 cm | 15 to 20 cm |
| Sword | 60 to 100 cm | 10 to 20 cm |
| Longsword | 60 to 80 cm | 20 to 30 cm |

## Named swords

Each has its own page. Lengths are those it can be made in.

| Name | Blade | Handle | What sets it apart |
| ---- | ----- | ------ | ------------------ |
| [Arming Sword](arming-sword) | 69 to 81 cm | 10 to 20 cm | A straight blade with two edges and a cross guard: the plain knight's sword. |
| [Backsword](backsword) | 75 to 90 cm | 12 to 15 cm | A straight blade with one edge and a guard round the hand. |
| [Broadsword](broadsword) | 75 to 90 cm | 12 to 15 cm | A broad, straight blade with two edges and a basket guarding the hand. |
| [Dao](dao) | 65 to 90 cm | 15 to 20 cm | A broad, curved blade with one edge. |
| [Falchion](falchion) | 70 to 80 cm | 12 to 15 cm | A broad blade with one edge, heavier towards the tip. |
| [Jian](jian) | 45 to 80 cm | 15 to 25 cm | A straight blade with two edges and a tassel on the pommel. |
| [Katana](katana) | 60 to 80 cm | 25 to 30 cm | A slightly curved blade with one edge, a round or square guard and a long grip for two hands. |
| [Rapier](rapier) | 100 to 130 cm | 12 to 15 cm | A long, narrow blade for thrusting, with a cup or a bow guarding the hand. |
| [Sabre](sabre) | 70 to 90 cm | 12 to 15 cm | A curved blade with one edge and a bow guarding the knuckles. |
| [Scimitar](scimitar) | 75 to 90 cm | 12 to 15 cm | A broad blade, strongly curved, with one edge. |
| [Shamshir](shamshir) | 75 to 90 cm | 10 to 13 cm | A slim blade, strongly curved, with one edge. |

| [Spatha](spatha) | 60 to 75 cm | 18 to 20 cm | A long, straight blade with two edges, for cutting. |
| [Tulwar](tulwar) | 70 to 85 cm | 10 to 13 cm | A curved blade with a disc for a pommel. |
