# Parrying Dagger

![Parrying Dagger]({{ '/assets/weapons/parrying-dagger.png' | relative_url }})

A dagger with a large guard, held in the off hand to turn blows aside. One of the [daggers](./). No two are quite alike: this is what one usually is.
