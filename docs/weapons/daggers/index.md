# Daggers

Short blades on a grip for one hand, made for stabbing, mostly with two edges or only a point. Back to [Weapons](../).

## Plain names

A weapon of this family that matches none of the named ones below goes by a plain name for its lengths. A short blade on a grip is a Knife if it has one edge, and a Dagger if it has two or only a point.

| Name | Blade | Handle |
| ---- | ----- | ------ |
| Knife or Dagger | 10 to 35 cm | 8 to 20 cm |
| Short Sword | 35 to 60 cm | 8 to 13 cm |
