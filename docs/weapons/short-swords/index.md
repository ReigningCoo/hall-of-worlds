# Short swords

Blades longer than a dagger's and shorter than a sword's, on a grip for one hand. Back to [Weapons](../).

## Plain names

A weapon of this family that matches none of the named ones below goes by a plain name for its lengths.

| Name | Blade | Handle |
| ---- | ----- | ------ |
| Knife or Dagger | 30 to 35 cm | 15 to 20 cm |
| Short Sword | 35 to 60 cm | 10 to 20 cm |
| Sword | 60 to 76 cm | 10 to 17 cm |

## Named short swords

Each has its own page. Lengths are those it can be made in.

| Name | Blade | Handle | What sets it apart |
| ---- | ----- | ------ | ------------------ |
| [Cutlass](cutlass) | 61 to 76 cm | 10 to 15 cm | A short, slightly curved blade with a cup or shell guard. |
| [Falcata](falcata) | 35 to 55 cm | 10 to 13 cm | A blade curved forward, with a hooked handle that guards the hand. |
| [Gladius](gladius) | 45 to 68 cm | 13 to 17 cm | A short, broad blade with two edges and a sharp point, for stabbing. |
| [Khopesh](khopesh) | 35 to 45 cm | 12 to 15 cm | A blade bent like a sickle, used as a sword. |
| [Kopis](kopis) | 48 to 65 cm | 10 to 13 cm | A heavy blade curved forward, for cutting blows. |
| [Wakizashi](wakizashi) | 30 to 60 cm | 15 to 20 cm | A short curved blade with one edge, a round guard and a long grip. |
| [Xiphos](xiphos) | 45 to 60 cm | 10 to 13 cm | A leaf-shaped blade with two edges. |
| [Yataghan](yataghan) | 55 to 65 cm | 13 to 17 cm | A blade curved forward, with no guard and a flared pommel. |
