# Woodcutting

Any tree can be felled with an axe: the trees of woods and forests, the palms of an oasis, and the fruit and nut trees of orchards. A felled tree leaves a stump and a pile of [logs](logs), and grows back in time. Logs can be split into [kindling](logs#kindling). Back to [Gameplay](gameplay).

## Felling a tree

The first time you hold a one-handed axe, you learn **Fell Tree** and **Cut Kindling**. Put them on your action bar from the Abilities page (B). Both need a one-handed axe held in your hand: carried in your pack it doesn't count.

Stand next to a tree and use Fell Tree. If you aren't facing a tree, you turn to the nearest one in reach. You chop at it until it falls, or until you move, press Esc or are hurt. Every blow makes the tree flash red, for you and anyone else who can see it.

| How long it takes | |
| ----------------- | - |
| Untrained, with a plain hatchet | About 60 seconds |
| With practice and a fine axe | Down to about 30 seconds |
| With any axe but a hatchet | Half as long again |

The **Woodcutting** skill grows with every tree felled, and a better axe cuts faster. If you stop, the tree keeps the damage you did for a day, so you or someone else can finish it. Several people chopping one tree finish it sooner.

When the tree falls, its logs drop by your feet: up to 12 for the tallest forest trees, two or three for most fruit trees, one for the smallest. A young tree gives a quarter of a grown one's logs, a half-grown one half. Bananas, papayas and saguaros have no true wood: they give 5 to 15 pieces of kindling instead. A ripe fruit tree also drops its fruit, though fewer than picking it would give.

## Splitting logs

**Cut Kindling** splits a log into kindling, one piece for each kilogram of the log. It splits the first log you carry; with none in your pack, it splits the first log on the ground in reach while your inventory is open and shows the ground. It takes about 20 seconds a log, a little less with practice in Woodcutting.

## Growing back

All of this follows the game's own clock, so it waits while the game is paused.

| After felling | What is there | Walking over it |
| ------------- | ------------- | --------------- |
| The first day | A stump | Slower than open ground |
| Days 1 to 5 | Open ground | As open ground |
| From day 5 | A sapling | As open ground |
| A week later | A young tree, which can be felled | Slower than open ground |
| A week after that | A half-grown tree, which can be felled | Much slower |
| A week after that | A grown tree | Blocks the way, as any tree |

A fruit tree grows back the same way, and bears fruit again once grown.
