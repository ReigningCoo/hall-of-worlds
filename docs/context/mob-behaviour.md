# Mob behaviour plan

How mobs and NPCs decide what to do, in 9 PRs. Planned with the owner on 2026-11-27, starting from RogueBasin's "A Better Monster AI": drives with weights (aggression, fear, desire, wander, misc), the strongest score winning; courage from allies seen and fear from enemies' strength; targets plus pathfinding rather than its 8-direction vectors; remembering the last strong desire; every mob's scores worked out before any moves.

## Before the plan

`Temper` and `Senses` were matches in `shared/src/creature/senses.rs`; `notice` (server `entities/notice.rs`) only sees players; memory is per mob per character (`shared::mob::Memory`, `provoked` meaning self-defence for alignment); `listen` turns idle mobs to fights heard (4, 8 or 14 cells); Sneak only slows the pace; no groups beyond a wild herd's den.

## Owner's decisions

- Behaviour is configuration per race (`behaviours.ron`), **moddable** like herbs: `HOW_MODS/behaviours/*.ron`, the same race replacing; server-only, never sent to clients.
- **Bands** within a race, each with its own friends and enemies (a band variant rolled per band). **Families** within bands: a field reserved, nothing built yet; for the more intelligent races, not animals.
- **Mobs fight mobs.** Wolves hunt wild prey (rabbits) and **always** go after prey they see (no hunger clock). A kill is eaten: no items left (a blood mark on the ground later). Prey running far enough (past the leash from the den) makes the hunters give up and go home. Fleeing is straight for some, **zigzag** for prey animals, by race.
- Hunting is **wild only** for now: never penned livestock or escortees. For wolves and the like, **fences and gates are nearly impossible to cross**, and they are **afraid of open fires** (campfires, lit fireplaces).
- **Sneak attacks:** a blow on an Unaware mob is a guaranteed critical (double harm for now), kept as its own rule for future critical hits.
- **No markers over mobs** (they would be confused with quest markers). Instead, **while Sneak is on only**, an eye over the player, closed to open by how noticeable they are.
- **Desire** (going for things on the ground): basic support now, improved later.

## PRs

1 first, then 2; 3 to 5 and 6 to 8 are two lines that may interleave (8's alarm calls best after 3); 9 any time after 2.

1. **Settings, bands, mods** (built: `shared::behaviour`, server `behaviours.rs`): drives, defends, alarm call, `hunts`, flee style, fence crossing, fears fire, desires; `Temper` moved in; a band on each `Spawn` (a wild pack or herd; per race per site), band variants with relations, `relation(a, b)` (Kin, Ally, Neutral, Rival, Enemy); family reserved; the server loads mods; no change in play.
2. **Scorer** (built: server `entities/choice`) replacing `notice`, `after_hit`, territory and timid with today's results, plus morale (fleeing when hurt by courage, braver with the band in sight, afraid when outnumbered), a margin against flip-flopping, straight or zigzag fleeing, fear of open fire, fences for wolves; scores in Inspect. Numbers chosen (Claude's, to check in play): attack 50 + aggression ÷ 2, guard 45 + guarding ÷ 4, approach 40, keep away 60, fear hurt × (100 − courage) ÷ 40 + 20 per extra enemy − 10 per friend seen (three at most), margin 10, fires kept 3 cells off; `Seldom` fences give way only to a mob fleeing.
3. **Pack defence** (built: server `entities/defend.rs`): mobs see mobs in their cone; seeing one of its band or an ally harmed by a player, it remembers the attacker (provoked) and joins.
4. **Mobs fighting mobs** (built: server `entities/foes.rs`, `mob_blows.rs`): mobs see other mobs as they see players and strike them by the same combat rules and wind-ups; harm, death and memory by the attacker's id (a mob remembered as a `CharacterId` of its own id); rival bands and enemies start unfriendly and fight when they meet, the rest are nothing to each other; pack defence works against mob attackers, never against the defender's own band or allies; penned animals, escortees and still mobs neither fight mobs nor are fought.
5. **Hunting** (built: server `entities/hunt.rs`, shared `mob/wild_prey.rs`): hunters always go after wild prey of their `hunts` they see or smell (a fresh mark on or beside their cell), never penned animals, escortees or still mobs; their band in sight joins the chase; prey of any temper keeps away from hunters within 10 cells, zigzagging by its race; a kill is eaten (no items, no kill counted), the killer and hunters within 4 cells eating 30 s, then home; prey known past 32 cells from the den (`HUNT_LEASH`) ends the hunt, home. Packs that hunt often have their prey's herd 10 to 16 cells from their den. Numbers Claude's, to check in play: a wolf (140) seldom catches a zigzagging rabbit (130) in the open, so most rabbits get away past the leash, and cornered ones are caught.
6. **Noise** (built: `shared::noise`, server `entities/noise.rs`, client `sneak_eye`): a shared rule (place, loudness, walls and doors muffling, hearing and distance, a hashed roll); footsteps by pace, armour weight, Sneak and Stealth; blows, spells, doors, calls; Stealth practice; the sneaking eye (the client from the shared rule, wide when a mob is Alert to them, told by the server: `common::MobAlert`, set from PR 7). Numbers Claude's, to check in play: walking heard 5 cells by normal hearing (Poor ½, Keen 1¾), +1 a 5 kg of armour, sneaking ½ at 0% Sneaking to ⅛ at 100%; blows 8, spells 6, doors 5, kicked 12, calls by race; certain within half the reach, then down to nothing at its end; each wall cell between leaves ⅓, a closed door ½; footsteps rolled per cell stepped into; a sneaker's step missed by a mob in reach practises Sneaking.
7. **Alertness** (built: `shared::alertness`, `combat::Critical`, server `entities/alert.rs`): Unaware, Alert (heard something, turning to it at rest once a reaction delay by its Mind row is over), Aware (sees a player, or a mob it is against; or was harmed); a blow on one caught off guard (Unaware, or Alert and still reacting, so someone heard creeping up gets that moment) is a critical, double harm, as its own rule; players told `ServerMessage::MobAlert` while a mob is Alert to or Aware of them, opening the sneaking eye wide. Numbers Claude's, to check in play: reaction 1.5 s at a Mind row averaging 100 (½ s to 3 s), Alert for 10 s after the last noise or sight; one stage a mob, so Aware of anyone means no critical from anyone.
8. **Curiosity and alarm calls** (built: server `entities/curious.rs`): at rest, turned to a noise it can't see, a mob goes to look by its curiosity (`Doing::Investigating`: walking until it can see the noise's cell, looking 5 s, then home; anyone seen hands over to the scorer), a timid one steps away (`Doing::Shying`, 6 steps, then home); a hurt mob of a race with an alarm call gives it on every harm, and its band who hear it come over (timid ones shy) unless fighting, following or fleeing. Numbers Claude's, to check in play: curiosity as a percent chance a noise, looking no further than 24 cells from home, giving up the walk after 30 s.
9. **Desire basics** (built: server `entities/desire.rs`, shared `behaviour/desire.rs`): a mob takes wanted things from the ground by its race's `desires` (food for animals, coins and weapons for greedy races): the nearest bag it sees holding one, within 24 cells of home, is one more choice for the scorer (`Choice::Take`, 20 + desire ÷ 2, less a point a cell walked), only while at rest, settling, going home or looking into a noise; in reach it takes the first thing it wants, whole, into what it carries (dropped where it dies), goes home, and may come back for more; a bag it can't get to is passed over a minute. Numbers Claude's, to check in play. Left for later: herbivores wanting only what they eat (Food is the whole category now), eating food rather than carrying it, how much a mob can carry, remembering a strong desire out of sight, wanting better things than it has.
