# client: abilities

What the character can do: the Character panel's Abilities page (B) and Spells page (P, the spellbook), every ability they know (`shared::ability::known`: by the body they are in, from skills, or learned) as ability widgets flowing in rows, Attack first, each used by a click or tap and marked as on the bar (automatic, on, queued); and the action bar, ten slots, five either side of the Health and Mana circle, with dragging between them. Not the panel or its tabs: those are `inventory`'s, which spawns the pages as `common::AbilitiesPage` and `common::SpellsPage` nodes. Native and web.

## Notes

- Items on the bar (owner, 2026-11-15): an item dragged from any grid onto a slot puts its kind there (green or red as for abilities; the item goes back where it was); a kind of item dragged off a slot onto anywhere on the Character panel comes off; slot onto slot swaps. The slot shows the kind's picture and how many are carried (bags too, however deep, never quest items: `Inventory::count_of`), greyed at 0, and greyed with the dull red frame when the player's kind won't eat it (Dwarven Bread for a non-dwarf). Its key, a click or a tap eats one at once (smallest stack in the own grid first, then in bags), as dropping it on the clothes does; never automatic.
- The page as the bar (owner, 2026-11-22): a click or tap on an ability on the page uses it as its slot would (queued, a toggle at once, a panel opened), without putting it on the bar first; a press becomes a drag only once it moves. Each ability there shows its state as on the bar, from the same `Marks`: the metal edge if automatic on the bar, the green edge for Sneak on or a panel open (Alchemy, Cook, Press Oil, Press Juice, Ferment), the stripes while queued; greyed when it can't be used now. A panel ability used again, from the page, the bar or its key, closes its panel.
- Owner's design: from the page onto a slot puts an ability there (replacing what was there; one already on the bar moves, with its automatic use); slot onto slot swaps; slot onto the page takes it off and forgets its automatic use; dropped anywhere else nothing changes, so nothing is lost by accident. Abilities can't be sorted on the page; filtering and searching come later.
- Mouse: press and drag to move; a click on a slot uses it; right-click on a slot turns automatic use on or off. Touch: drag to move; a tap uses; a finger held still on a slot turns automatic use on or off (a drag starts only when the finger moves). Keys: `Action::UseSlot(0..9)`, 1 to 0 by default, in Settings' Action Bar group. Using one asks the server for it at the target; an automatic one is asked for whenever it can be done (owner: target, in reach, an enemy for Attack; a quarter of the player's Health for Heal), so it can be left on. Automatic slots take turns in bar order, keys 1 to 0: the first whose time has come is asked for, so their order is the player's choice of how to fight (owner, 2026-11-07). The next is asked a quarter of a second before the last's recovery ends (`OwnAbility::is_busy`), as the server keeps it until then; otherwise slow frames would slow attacks. Only abilities that may be made automatic can be.
- Clicks come first (owner, 2026-11-07): while the player moves (a journey with way to go, steps waiting, a movement key held) or abilities chosen by hand are queued, asked for or winding up, automatic ones wait, starting again once it is over. Presses queue in order (owner: a second press of a queued one takes it out; pressing the one winding up queues it again), and moving empties the queue while Esc only calls off the wind-up; one asked for early and still waiting on the server is taken back (`WithdrawAbility`, at no cost) so the press or the move goes in its place. One already winding up finishes, unless Esc or a move calls it off. The Update systems run after `PlayerSystems::Travel`, so they know whether the player is moving this frame.
- Walking into reach (owner, 2026-11-27): Attack, Kick or a spell with a where used by hand at a target out of reach walks the player there first (`player::Approach`, following the target, round others), then is asked for as usual; moving, a click elsewhere or Esc calls it off, and it is dropped if the target goes or can't be got nearer. Automatic ones never walk: they wait for the target to be in reach.
- The bar takes presses (each side's row is `dom::Opaque`), so clicks on it never walk the player; right-clicks on a slot never target.
- Each ability stands on the colour of what it leans on (`widgets::attribute_colour` of `Ability::leans`), dark when active and pastel when passive, on the page, the bar and while dragged.
- Spells on the bar (magic plan PR 5): the Heal spell is where the Heal ability was (new bars, and old saves' Heal loads as it). A spell slot queues like an ability (`Manual` holds `BarThing`s: abilities and spells) and is cast with `SpellbookAsk::Cast` when its turn comes, if `can_cast`; the server answers as Magic (`AbilityStarted(Magic)`), which `Manual::begun` takes for the spell sent. Greyed without the Mana, out of the book, or with a where in The Way Inn. Its tip is its name over "A spell from your spellbook.". Spells are dragged onto the bar from the Spells page (`spellbook/`, `BarChange::PlaceSpell`) as abilities are from the Abilities page, by mouse or finger, and back onto it to come off; a click or tap on one there casts it as its slot would.
- Passive abilities are drawn round and never greyed; active ones the player can't use now are greyed with a dull red frame (also on the bar, after a shapeshift, without its tool carried, and while there isn't the Mana). Ferment (greyed without a usable yeast carried) is never queued: using it opens its panel (`ferment/`). Alchemy (greyed without an apparatus carried, or on the bench the player is at, `common::BenchHere`; `use_slots` checks the same) is never queued: using it opens or closes the Alchemy panel (`alchemy::OpenAlchemy`, the `alchemy` module), which asks the server itself. Cook (cooking plan PR 10; everyone has it, no tool) the same way: using it opens or closes the Cooking panel (`cooking::OpenCooking`). Magic (everyone's, magic plan PR 4) is never sent: using it opens or closes the spell design panel (`spell_design::OpenSpellDesign`). Press Oil (greyed without an oil press) is never queued: using it opens its requester (`press_oil.rs`), and Press asks the server with the chosen stack as the target; the server then presses bottle after bottle, as for Collect Water. Press Juice (greyed without an oil press) the same way (`press_juice.rs`), the server pressing up to 2 kg at a time into a carried cask or vessel. Collect Water (learned on a first empty bottle, greyed with none carried) is asked for with no target, and only while `potion::water_faced` finds water ahead of the player on `CurrentDungeon`, or, facing none, `potion::catches_rain` says rain is caught where they stand (`rain_here.rs`: `WaterHere`, the rain at `WorldReturn`'s world map point as `dungeon_map` draws it) (`ask_wanted`); the server then fills bottle after bottle, each its own `AbilityStarted`, until Esc, a move or harm calls it off. Fell Tree and Cut Kindling (learned on first holding a one-handed axe, greyed without one held) are queued like any other by hand (woodcutting PR 4, `woodcutting.rs`): Fell Tree with no target while a tree is faced (`woodcutting::tree_faced`), or, with one in reach beside or behind, turning to it first (`ClientMessage::Face` sent just before the ask, and `common::FaceTowards`); the server then lands a blow every 2 s, each its own `AbilityStarted` with its wind-up bar, until the tree falls or Esc, a move or harm stops it. Cut Kindling with no target for a log carried, else the first log on the ground while the inventory shows it (`inventory::GroundShown`) as its target. The tip names the ability (on the bar with its key in full) over its `description`.
- Ids: `abilities/<ability>` (`attack`, `heal`, `night_vision`, ...), `bar/slot.<0..9>`; the Spells tab's `spellbook/design`, `spellbook/<name>.<row>` (`favourite`, `rename`, `remove`), `spellbook/new_name`, `spellbook/really_rename`, `spellbook/really_remove`.
- Narrow screens will show only the first few slots and may move the circle: a later mobile task (owner).
