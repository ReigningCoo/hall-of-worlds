# client: music

Music played by the client (never game state): a SoundFont's instruments rendered by `rustysynth` (MIT) inside a custom Bevy audio source (PR 2), and tunes from MIDI files (`midly`, Unlicense) named by playlists, played by a sequencer inside the stream (PR 3; `docs/context/research-music.md`), each place with its own tunes and players, changing at the next bar line (PR 4), and combat parts joining when the player is attacked (PR 5). The debug menu's Music panel (Play Tune) follows the tune playing, plays one alone, forces combat music on and goes back to music by place; the menu also plays every tune, or the test.

## Files

- `crates/client/src/music/mod.rs`: `MusicPlugin`: the SoundFont asset and its loader, the stream as an audio source (`add_audio_source`), the messages, and the systems in one chain in `Update`.
- `crates/client/src/music/font.rs`: `SoundFontAsset` (an `Arc<rustysynth::SoundFont>`) and its `.sf2` loader; `PATH` (`music/music.sf2`).
- `crates/client/src/music/stream.rs`: `MusicEvent` (a MIDI channel message), `Command` (Play a `Program`, Change, Attacked, Stop), `Shared` (commands and events queued for the next block, the target gain as `f32` bits, the tune playing as an atomic the decoder writes after each block, combat forced as an atomic flag), `MusicStream` (the asset Bevy plays: `Decodable`, handing its one synthesizer to the decoder).
- `crates/client/src/music/decoder.rs`: `MusicDecoder` (rodio `Source`, stereo at 44.1 kHz, endless): at each 64-sample block takes the commands (to its `Sequencer`), then queued events, and renders the block in pieces between the sequencer's events, so each lands on its sample (moving the sequencer on without rendering while the gain and target are 0); while combat is forced, the sequencer is told it was attacked every block; after each block, the tune playing (`Sequencer::tune_now`) goes into `Shared`; the gain eased towards the target per sample (`eased`, about 30 ms).
- `crates/client/src/music/start.rs`: `Music` (Waiting, Loading, Playing, Failed), `start_music` (loads the SoundFont only once `SoundSwitches::music_on`, `sound::MusicVolume` above 0 and `MayPlay`; then a synthesizer, 64 voices natively, 24 and no reverb or chorus in a browser; natively an `AudioPlayer` entity, `MusicPlayer`, in a browser the decoder handed to `web_out`), `sink_at_full`, `follow_volume` (target = `MusicVolume` × `GlobalVolume`), `send_events`.
- `crates/client/src/music/web_out.rs` (wasm32 only): the music's own Web Audio output: an `AudioContext` made once music starts, the decoder's samples in chunks of 2048 frames scheduled `LEAD` (0.4 s) ahead by `AudioBufferSourceNode`s, topped up each frame (`top_up_each_frame`) and as each chunk ends (so in hidden tabs too); fallen behind, it starts again `MARGIN` after now with the first chunk faded in, counting it in the log ("Music fell behind").
- `crates/client/src/music/gesture.rs`: `MayPlay`: true natively; in a browser after the first click, tap or key released.
- `crates/client/src/music/tune.rs`: `Tune` (title, notes in ticks of `PPQ` = 960 a quarter, `length` once round, tempos in quarters a minute, `Signature`s, `final_class`), `Signature` (`unit`, `bar`, `beat`: three units in compound metres, `in_bar`), `nearest_of_class`.
- `crates/client/src/music/midi.rs`: `read_tune`: a standard MIDI file into a `Tune` (notes off the drum channel, any number of tracks, ticks rescaled; track name, tempo and time signature changes, "mode: ... on X" text for the final, "loop" marker for the length; notes cut at it), `final_of`.
- `crates/client/src/music/playlist.rs`: `PlaylistFile` (`players`, `tunes`, `ensembles`), `TuneEntry` (id, file from the assets folder, title, source, read_from, licence, `Rhythm` Written or Ours, `places` (none: everywhere), `after`), `Ensemble` (places and their `Players`), `Players` (melody, second, drone as `Instrument` bank and program, `Percussion` strong and light drum keys, `link_seconds`, `Combat`: a kit drum and a `Stinger` instrument `above` the final, none for no combat music; a recorder alone by default), `merge` (later files' players win, ensembles added after, a known id replaced in place), `in_order` (an `after` tune moved just after the one it follows).
- `crates/client/src/music/place.rs`: `Place` (`Wilderness(Band, Daylight)`, WorldMap, Hall, Inn, Village, Town, City, Castle, Tower, Cave, Farm, Herbalist, Oasis), `Band` (Any, Cold, Temperate, Dry, Hot: `Band::of(Biome)`), `Daylight` (Any, Day, Night); `within` (a playlist's `Any` taking in every band or time), `of_level` (by the level's kind; basements and caves' depths are Cave; huts, ruins and the like the wilderness round them).
- `crates/client/src/music/place_now.rs`: `PlaceNow`, `follow_place`: the place from `GameView`, the level's `LevelInfo` and the world map point (the player's on the world map, else `WorldReturn`) with its biome and the suns (day while any is up), taken once it has held 1.5 s.
- `crates/client/src/music/sets.rs`: `tunes_for` (the tunes naming a place, with those naming none; a wilderness no tune names borrows any band's at that time, then any; elsewhere every tune), `players_for` (the last ensemble naming the place, else `players`).
- `crates/client/src/music/loaders.rs`: `TuneAsset` (`.mid`, `.midi`) and `PlaylistAsset` (`.playlist.ron`) with their loaders.
- `crates/client/src/music/library.rs`: `Library` loading once music plays: natively every playlist in `music/playlists/` (`load_folder`; the game's first, mods' by name), in a browser only `game.playlist.ron`; merged, ordered, each tune's file loaded, unreadable ones left out with a warning; `Ready` (tunes, each one's places, players, ensembles); `TuneList` of `ListedTune`s (title, places as words).
- `crates/client/src/music/place_words.rs`: `place_words`: a tune's places as words ("Wilderness: cold, night · Village · Town", "Everywhere" for none).
- `crates/client/src/music/now.rs`: `PlayingTune`, `ForcedCombat`, `follow_now` (the stream's tune out, the forcing in).
- `crates/client/src/music/play_tunes.rs`: `PlayTunes` (ByPlace, All, One), `Asked` (what was asked and told; each place's tunes start one further on each visit), `play_tunes` (tells the stream `Command::Play` at first and on a debug ask, `Command::Change` when the place changes; every tune until a place is known).
- `crates/client/src/music/sequencer.rs`: `Sequencer` (run by the decoder: `play`, `stop`, `samples_to_next`, `advance`; a queue of what is due by tick, a clock turning ticks to samples at the tempo of the moment), `change` (another program at the next bar line, at once if nothing plays), `attacked`, `Program` (tunes, players, `listed`: each tune's place in `TuneList`), `tune_now`, `LOOPS`; channels melody 0, second 1, drone 2, stinger 3, drums 9; the tempo played is the tune's times combat's `factor`. `sequencer/due.rs`: `Due` (MIDI, Tempo, PassEnd, LinkEnd, Factor and FightBar, numbered by fight so stale ones are dropped) and `Queue` (by tick; offs, then controls, then markers, then ons). `sequencer/pass.rs`: a pass of a tune queued at once: melody (2% shorter), second player on notes starting on a beat an octave down at 70%, held to the next beat with a note (at most 4), drums a unit at a time by `drum_level` (the first rhythmic mode), fading in over 2 bars after a link; `end_pass` (again, or a link after `LOOPS`). `sequencer/link.rs`: `begin_link` (1 to 3 bars by `link_seconds`, the tempo gliding a unit at a time to the next tune's, the drone moved half a bar in, a bridging note on the next final near its first note), `link_into` (a link in a given signature into a given tune, also for changes), `end_link`, `move_drone` (final and fifth in the third octave). `sequencer/change.rs`: `change` (what was coming from the next bar line taken out (`Queue::cut_from`), notes sounding let go on it, the new players' instruments, the drone let go if they have none, then a link into the new program's first tune), `next_bar` (the end counts; in a link, bars go on from the end); combat's tempo steps carry on through it. `sequencer/combat.rs`: `Fight` (`State` Calm, Fighting, Easing), `attacked` (on the next beat: unless calm for under `RECENT`, 30 s, a stinger, a roll through the beat and the accent on the next; the tempo `RISE`, 1.2, over a bar a unit at a time; the drone's volume, CC 7, up with it), `bar` (the ostinato a bar at a time to the next bar line by `ostinato`, fills at every 4th bar's end; at a bar line after `HOLD`, 10 s, quiet, two bars easing back fading), `set_factor`, `changed` (a change of place: the fight goes on with the new combat parts, or calms over a bar with none).
- `crates/client/src/music/combat.rs`: `follow_attacks`: each `common::Attacked` told to the stream as `Command::Attacked`.
- `crates/client/src/music/test_tune.rs`: `PlayTestMusic`, `INSTRUMENTS`, `test_tune` (a D Dorian phrase on each instrument, bank and program, then drums), `play_test_tune` (starts once the music plays, sends events by `Time`).

## Public API

- `MusicPlugin`.
- `MusicEvent` (message): a MIDI channel message for the synthesizer (`note_on`, `note_off`, `bank`, `program`); dropped while no music plays.
- `PlayTestMusic` (message): stops the tunes and plays the test from the start (debug menu's Play Test Music).
- `PlayTunes` (message): `ByPlace` (as at the start), `All` (every tune in turn) or `One(n)` (the `n`th in `TuneList`, round and round): the Music panel's Reset and dropdown, and the debug menu's Play All Tunes.
- `TuneList` (resource): every tune in the order they play (`ListedTune`: `title`, `used`, its places as words), once loaded (empty until music starts).
- `PlayingTune` (resource): the tune playing, by its place in `TuneList`, as the stream reports it (by place, during Play All Tunes, after links and changes; none while no music plays).
- `ForcedCombat` (resource): while true, combat music holds as if attacked all the time; false, it eases back after `HOLD`. Real attacks work alongside.

## Depends on

- `common`: `Attacked`, `SoundSwitches`, `GameView`, `CurrentDungeon`, `CurrentWorld` (climate, surface, suns), `WorldReturn`, `ServerClock`, `MapPosition`. `player`: `Player`. `sound`: `MusicVolume`. Bevy's audio (`Decodable`, `GlobalVolume`, `AudioSink`; natively), `web-sys`'s Web Audio (in a browser), `rustysynth`, `midly` (without its threads, for the web).

## Notes

- **The SoundFont:** `crates/client/assets/music/music.sf2`, made by `tools/subset_soundfont.py` from MuseScore General 0.2 (MIT; licence beside it) and Early European Instruments (public domain; a signed-in download from polyphone.io, which the owner did), both credited in `ATTRIBUTION.md` and `docs/credits.md`; downloads kept in `D:\how-downloads\soundfonts\`, never in the repository. Presets keep their General MIDI bank and program, so tunes name instruments as General MIDI does (`PRESETS` in the script says what each stands for: recorder, fiddle, nylon guitar for the lute, harp, dulcimer for the psaltery, shanai for the shawm, trumpet, trombone for the sackbut, bagpipe, church and reed organs, voice oohs for the choir, tubular and tinkle bells, timpani for nakers, taiko). The standard kit (bank 128) keeps only the keys in `DRUM_KEYS`. The hurdy-gurdy and cornemuse are in bank 8 (General MIDI's variations) as programs 110 and 109 (the fiddle's and bagpipe's), one channel at 48 kHz (from 192 kHz stereo); a tune picks them with control change 0 = 8 (`MusicEvent::bank`) before the program. 12.9 MB; `--sizes` prints what each preset would bring (Choir Aahs alone 4.8 MB, left out). Tests check the test tune's instruments are all there, and that the two from bank 8 sound.
- **Browsers:** the SoundFont is fetched only when music first starts, so never with music muted or off. The music plays through `web_out`, not Bevy's audio: cpal's Web Audio output keeps only about 0.09 s queued (so long frames stuttered) and bursts after a freeze (see `decisions.md`). Its context is made after the first click, tap or key (`MayPlay`), so browsers let it run; `web_out` resumes it if not, and `index.html` still resumes Bevy's own, opened at startup. Volume and combat changes reach the ear up to the lead (0.4 s) later than natively.
- **Volumes:** Bevy applies `GlobalVolume` only when a sound starts, so the music's sink is set to full once (`sink_at_full`) and General is folded into the stream's own eased gain.
- **Events** are applied at the start of the next block (64 samples, about 1.5 ms), not to the sample; the sequencer runs inside the stream, so its own are exact to the sample.
- **Tunes** (`assets/music/tunes/`, 23 from the transcription task: `~/.claude/research-tools/music/tunes/`, whose `tunes.toml` the game's playlist was made from): single-melody MIDI files, format 0, 480 ticks a quarter, melody on channel 0, no program change (the players are the playlist's), a "loop" marker where each goes round seamlessly. Credited in `docs/credits.md` (one line per manuscript or edition) and `assets/ATTRIBUTION.md` (the one CC BY edition). La Rotta follows the Lamento (`after`).
- **Mods** add a `<name>.playlist.ron` in `music/playlists/` (natively only, as a browser can't list a folder), with their tunes (paths from the assets folder, places as `Place` values, none for everywhere) and, if they like, players and ensembles (theirs win for the places they name); an id already known replaces that tune.
- **By place:** every place has at least 3 tunes of its own and an ensemble (tests check, and that every instrument named is in the SoundFont): the wilderness a pipe and drone (cornemuse in the cold, shawm and frame drum in deserts, hand drums where hot), sparser at night; more players the larger the town; links short in towns and the inn, long in the wilderness, caves and the Hall. The new players' `link_seconds` sets a change of place's link too. Combat parts by place in the ensembles too (none in The Way Inn and on the world map; tests check every other place has them and their drum is in the kit).
- **Never silent:** one tune plays round for good; more are each played twice and linked (owner's decision: no rests). Muted music keeps time without rendering.
- **Combat** (owner's model, `music-sketchbook.html`): the key never changes, so it sounds like the same players speeding up; the parts play through links and changes, so a fight never goes quiet. Hostile actions come from `target`'s guesses at what the server tells (see `target.md`); without a hit the music only knows of a mob going after the player once it is shown unfriendly or starts an attack.
