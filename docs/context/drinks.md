# Drinks and fermentation: plan

Owner's request and decisions (2026-11-18). Bottles, waters and oils (`shared::potion`), the oil press and Collect Water (`brewing.md`), fruit, vegetables and grains with their kcal (`shared::fruit`, `shared::vegetable`) and bakers already exist. This adds casks, vessels and pouring, juice and cordial, yeast, fermentation, vinegar, drinking alcohol and casks in cellars (the alembic moved to the Alchemy plan). Research: `research-drinks.md`.

## Owner's decisions

### Clocks
1. One real hour is one game day.
2. Casks and drinks age on **world time** while on the ground, and on **game time while logged in** while in someone's pack.
3. Yeast goes by **24 real hours of playtime** in the pack (paused while logged out), and by real time on the ground.
4. Durations are real ones, in game days: ale about 24 game days (about a real day), cider and perry about 1.5 real days, wine about 4.
5. Ageing in oak raises quality, up to a cap per drink.

### Casks
6. Eight sizes, doubling from the English casks: Pin 20 L, Firkin 40, Kilderkin 80, Barrel 160, Hogshead 240, Puncheon 320, Butt 480, Tun 960.
7. Woods: oak (improves with age), chestnut and acacia (neutral), cherry (a little flavour), pine (taints).
8. The liquid adds its weight (about a kilogram a litre).
9. Big casks simply exist as items for now; how they are moved or used is settled later.
10. No ownership: anyone may use a cask in the world.

### Vessels and pouring
11. Vessels: tankard, cup, goblet, horn, wine bottle, jug, flask, waterskin.
12. A **tap** item, and one **Pour** rule between any containers.
13. A sealed cask never spoils. Once opened, a **best-before** countdown runs (longer for stronger drinks, none over 20%), shown in the popup, with a notice near the end. Adding vinegar speeds it.
14. Opened juice ferments wild into Poor cider instead of spoiling.
15. A bottle filled from a cask starts sealed.

### Fermentation
16. Strength ≈ kcal per litre ÷ 68, capped by the yeast's tolerance by its quality; leftover sugar makes the drink sweet; under 1% nothing ferments.
17. Grains ferment directly.
18. More yeast starts a cask faster. Time grows with the cask's size and falls with the yeast's quality and amount.

### Juice and cordial
19. Juice is pressed (**Press Juice**, with the oil press): a serving gives 1 Mana a second for 30 s.
20. Cordial (juice and water) makes 2 servings of 1 Mana a second for 20 s each.
21. An alcoholic drink gives twice its juice's effect, plus units of alcohol.

### Alcohol
22. **Units** on the sheet (1 unit = 10 ml of pure alcohol), worn off at **1 unit per 10 real minutes at 70 kg**, scaled by the kind's body mass; thresholds scale with mass too.
23. Stages: **tipsy** −33% speed; **drunk** −66% speed, Mind-row penalties, the odd **stumble** (seeded, so the client predicts it) that stops travel; **very drunk** collapses; **poisoning** harms; a **hangover** after.
24. Stacks fully with load penalties.
25. A **Tolerance** skill raises the thresholds.

### Yeast
26. Bought from bakers, nearly always Common.
27. Each yeast is its own item in a stack, the ones needing feeding shown first; feeding a stack feeds them all.
28. Each yeast can be used in one thing between feeds without being used up.
29. It loses a quality step for each 24 h unfed (Poor stays Poor for ever).
30. An **early feed** (under 12 h) resets its progress and splits it into two identical yeasts; the split uses that use up, so it needs 12+ hours and an on-time feed before it can split again.
31. **On-time feeds** (12 to 24 h) add progress points by the feeder's **Fermenting** skill, kept on the item. Fermenting is a new skill.

### Alembic
32. Heat is assumed to exist for now. The alembic and pure alcohol moved to the Alchemy plan (its PR 5, 2026-11-19), where pure alcohol's use is tinctures.

## As built

- Casks (PR 1, `docs/context/shared/cask.md`; Claude's choices and first guesses for the owner to tune): one `ItemKind::Cask` with its form (size, wood, liquid, litres); the woods are new materials (Oak, Chestnut, Acacia, Cherry, Pine), each cask weighing oak's figure for its size by its wood's density against oak's. Cells lying in the pack grow with size, from 2 × 3 (Pin) to 7 × 8 (Tun). Give Item asks for a size, a wood (as its material) and optionally a water or oil (then full); potions are refused. Carpenters sell them, the small sizes far more often. Collect Water fills empty bottles first, then pours 5 to 10 litres (by the Water skill) every 5 seconds into a carried cask that is empty or holds the same water, the one already started first; each pour practises Water as a bottle does. A pile on the ground holding a cask shows the biggest cask standing there instead of a sack (`ground::Load::Cask`, remembered like bags). The popup says how many litres it holds.
- Vessels, the tap and pouring (PR 2, `docs/context/shared/vessel.md`, `shared/pour.md`; Claude's first guesses for the owner to tune): one `ItemKind::Vessel` whose form says which (Goblet 0.2 L, Cup 0.25, Drinking Horn 0.3, Tankard 0.5, Flask 0.5, Wine Bottle 0.75, Jug 1.5, Waterskin 2), sold by carpenters, leatherworkers, jewellers and herbalists; `ItemKind::Tap` at carpenters. One Pour rule for casks, vessels and bottles: as much as there is up to the room, never two liquids or a potion, a bottle whole (100 ml), a tap carried to draw from a cask; letting go of one container on another pours, from the inventory or from the ground. Casks are now by the millilitre, sealed until poured from or into (Collect Water opens them too); an empty wine bottle or bottle filled from a cask starts sealed, and opens when poured from. Water drunk from a vessel by letting go on yourself, a serving of 250 ml, does nothing yet. Pouring into something on the ground is the server's but not aimed at in the inventory yet.
- Juice and cordial (PR 3, `docs/context/shared/juice.md`; Claude's choices and first guesses for the owner to tune): `Liquid::Juice(fruit)` and `Cordial(fruit)`. Press Juice (Alchemy, with the oil press) presses up to 2 kg of one fruit a pressing (15 to 11 s, as a bottle of oil, 8 Alchemy), one fruit at a time, into a carried cask or vessel (not bottles: pour from a vessel to fill them) empty or holding that juice, until the fruit runs out, it is full, or the player moves or is harmed. A fruit gives four fifths of its water (dry matter taken as kcal ÷ 4 a gram): an apple 141 ml, a kilogram about 0.7 L, so a Pin takes about 28 kg of apples. Fruit over 120 kcal in 100 g (dates and the like), nuts, herbs' fruits and fruit harmful raw can't be juiced. 60% of a fruit's kcal goes into its juice (`kcal_per_litre`, apple 400 a litre, ready for decision 16). Cordial is poured: water into juice up to the juice's amount, juice into water only if at least as much; salt water won't mix. Drinking goes by the share of a 250 ml serving drunk (a bottle's 100 ml of juice: 12 s); the latest juice or cordial replaces any earlier one, nothing ends it early. Bottles of water, juice or cordial are drunk whole and come back empty. In a bottle, juice is its fruit's ripe colour, cordial paler. Not built: opened juice turning into Poor cider (decision 14) waits for PR 6's clocks.
- Yeast (PR 4, `docs/context/shared/yeast.md`; Claude's choices and first guesses for the owner to tune): `ItemKind::Yeast`, a 250 g crock of starter, 1 × 1, with quality, each its own item so a stack keeps every one's `YeastForm` (seconds unfed, progress, used since fed, when it was put on the ground); stacks key on quality alone. It loses a step each 24 h unfed, Poor staying Poor. Its clock: the server's playtime (`Activity::played`, which already stops 5 minutes after the player last did anything, as well as while logged out) while carried, real time on the ground (stamped by `ground::drop_at`, settled for anything in a player's reach and once picked up). The popup lists each yeast in a stack, the one needing feeding soonest first ("Feed in 7 h", "Feed now: weakens in 5 h", "used since fed"), the client told each hour on its clock. Towns have no bakers, so herbalists sell it for now, Common 95% of the time, else the shop's rolled quality. The Fermenting skill (`crafting/fermenting`, Perception) is in the tree; Ferment practises it (PR 6), feeding will (PR 8). Give Item makes a fresh one of the quality chosen.
- Fermentation rules (PR 5, `docs/context/shared/fermentation.md`; Claude's first guesses for the owner to tune, listed there): `shared::fermentation`, pure functions. A `Mash` from juice (its kcal), cordial (as half water), fruit mashed whole in water or grain in water; strength kcal a litre ÷ 68, capped by the best yeast's tolerance (Poor 7%, Common 9%, +2% a step), the rest left as sweetness, nothing under 1%: apple juice is a dry 5.8% cider, 8 apples a litre about 6%, 7 sheaves of barley a litre about 5% ale. Time: ale 24 game days, cider and perry 36, wines 90 in a Pin with one Common yeast, +10% a size, quicker for better yeast and for more of it (yeasts stack, up to 40% of the time). Quality: the yeasts' mean, a step down for cordial, whole fruit or pine, Poor wild; oak ages it up (ale, cider, perry 1 step, fruit wines 2, wine 3), cherry 1, pine down. Opened: 6 game days plus strength² ÷ 4 (5% 12 days), none from 20%, vinegar shortening it, a notice in the last tenth; opened juice turns into a Poor wild cider (up to 6%) after 2 game days.
- Ferment and vinegar (PR 6, `docs/context/shared/ferment.md`; Claude's choices and first guesses for the owner to tune): `Liquid::Drink` (base, strength, sweetness, quality, vinegar added) and `Liquid::Vinegar` (by base: Cider, Malt or Wine Vinegar). **Ferment** (Fermenting, given to everyone for now like Alchemy; needs a usable yeast carried) opens a panel: a cask carried or lying in reach holding juice, cordial or fresh water; for water, a carried fruit or grain stack, all of it mashed in; a yeast stack and how many of its usable yeasts; what it would make. After a 5 s wind-up the cask is sealed holding the drink it will be, fermenting; the yeasts are marked used (not used up); 8 Fermenting practice. A fermenting cask can't be poured from or into. Casks keep clocks (`CaskTime`), worked out when looked at: playtime while carried, world time on the ground (stamped when put down, settled in a player's reach), as yeast. Sealed drinks fermented in the cask age by its wood; opened ones count down to vinegar, the popup saying "Turns to vinegar in N days" and a notice ("<cask> turns to vinegar soon", `ServerMessage::VinegarSoon`) in the last tenth; vinegar poured into a drink speeds it. Opened juice or cordial ferments wild into a Poor drink after 2 game days (decision 14). Give Item can make a drink or vinegar. Not built: clocks on vessels and bottles (a drink poured into one neither ages nor turns); pouring into a container lying on the ground from the inventory (Ferment reaches ground casks itself).
- Drinking alcohol (PR 7, `docs/context/shared/alcohol.md`; thresholds, penalties and timings are Claude's first guesses for the owner to tune): drinks are drunk from vessels a serving at a time and from bottles whole, as juice; each gives 2 Mana a second for 30 s a serving (`Source::Drink`, twice juice's) and its units (ml × strength ÷ 1000 ÷ 10 ml) on `Sheet::alcohol`, worn off at 1 a 10 real minutes at 70 kg by the server's action clock, faster for heavier bodies. Tipsy from 3 units (two thirds speed), drunk from 7 (a third speed, Intellect, Perception and Will −20%, one move in 10 stumbles), very drunk from 12 (worse, one in 5), poisoned from 20 (draining 1 Health a second, never below 1); reaching very drunk or poisoned by drinking collapses for a minute, unable to act (the energy spent on both sides); having been drunk brings an hour or so of hangover (Mind row −10%). Thresholds scale with mass and up to twice by Tolerance (`survival/tolerance`, Toughness), practised by drinking while tipsy or worse. The stage is one effect on the effect bar (icons by stage), swapped by the server each tick as it changes; speed stacks with load. Stumbles turn a move an eighth or a quarter of a turn (shorter, or nowhere, if in the way) by a hash of the character's id, map and move number: the client predicts it, stopping travel there, and the server veers whatever it is sent. No sway is drawn.
- Feeding starters (PR 8, `docs/context/shared/yeast.md`; amounts, points and chances are Claude's first guesses for the owner to tune): flour let go of on a carried yeast stack feeds it, every yeast that can be fed, the longest unfed first, each taking a sack of flour and 100 ml of fresh water (any but salt) from carried bottles (whole) or vessels (casks need a tap, so not those), until they run out. Early (under 12 h) splits it into two alike, progress reset and its use spent, taking flour and water twice and an empty bottle for the second crock; one already used can't split until fed on time. On time (12 to 24 h) adds 10 progress points plus one per whole point of Fermenting, and a step up comes with a chance in 100 of the progress at Poor, half at Common, a third at Uncommon and so on, up to Legendary (a beginner's Common yeast about 5 feeds on average); late only feeds. Every feed resets the clock and the use. Flour let go of on a bottle of fresh water begins a starter: a Poor yeast in place of the bottle, developing for 24 h of its clock ("Starter: ready in N h"), neither usable nor fed until then. 4 Fermenting practice a yeast fed or starter begun; no energy cost, answered at once.
- Casks in cellars (PR 9, `docs/context/shared/cellar.md`; chances, counts, sizes and contents are Claude's first guesses for the owner to tune): castles (80%), stone towers (50%) and wooden towers (40%) may have one room of 3 to 8 larger casks (castles' Barrel to Tun, towers' Kilderkin to Butt, mostly oak) against its walls, a cell apart, never by a door, stairs or the way in, nor under furniture, all from hashes of the site's seed after furniture (no generator moved). Neither has a basement, so the room is on the ground floor (the keep's or the tower's own; the ground floor has no windows, like an undercroft), which is played set into the wilderness: the server puts the casks on the wilderness's ground at the cutout's cells when it first works out that chunk (a developer's site on its own level), once, remembered on the site's level save (`cellar_stocked`). They are ordinary casks lying on the ground: drawn standing as any cask on the ground, blocking no one, for anyone to tap, fill, ferment in or carry off, their clocks by world time from then. A fifth are empty, about a seventh vinegar, the rest ale (a third) or a fruit's drink, Poor to Rare, aged up to 120 game days in their wood; three in ten drinks opened and partly drunk, on their way to vinegar. Spirits wait for the alembic (Alchemy plan).
