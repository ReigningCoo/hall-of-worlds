# shared: cooking

Cooking (plan: `docs/context/cooking.md`, research: `docs/context/research-cooking.md`). The culinary data of every raw food: its roles in a dish, how it is used and cooked, texture, functions, methods, flavours, strength, max share and diet flags. And a process's rules (plan PR 4): the grid of rows and tool steps (a tall step joining rows), tools and their sub-skills, chance, balance, least quality with an intermediate's history, heat and the Rare cap away from a hearth; pure functions on whole numbers, so the client's preview matches the server. And what an output is (PR 5): named after the closest real dish (a table of 219 from the research) or generically, its diet labels, Well Fed, and the poisons fungi carry into it. Its items are `shared::food` (PR 6).

## Public API

- `IngredientDef { source, key, usage, roles, main, cooking, texture, functions, methods, flavours, strength, max_share, meat, animal_product, gluten, dairy, nut_allergen }`; `check()` (strength 1 to 5, share at most 100%, the main role among the roles, meat an animal product).
- `Source`: Herb, Fruit, Vegetable, Fungus (by their tables' keys), Meat, Milk, Egg, Honey (by `livestock`'s animal key), Part (by `parts.ron`'s key), Pantry (kitchen staples made rather than grown, by `PANTRY`'s keys: salt, soy sauce; cooking PR 12).
- `Ingredients::built_in()`, `read(text)` (checked, each source and key once), `new`, `get(source, key)`, `of_form(&ItemForm)` (herb, fruit, vegetable, fungus by key, flour as its grain, an egg by its animal, a bottle, vessel or cask by its liquid; `None` otherwise, and for a server's modded herbs), `of_liquid(&Liquid)` (milk and honey by animal, soy sauce), `of_item(&Item)` (Salt by its kind, else `of_form`, never a container: what the order and the panel take as food), `from_source`, `iter`, `len`.

- `plan(process, ingredients, dishes, skills) -> Result<Planned { chance, outputs, practised, heat }, Refused>`. `Process { rows (1 to MOST_ROWS, 2), sources: Vec<Option<Carried>> by row, steps, heat }`; `Step { tool, column (below MOST_COLUMNS, 12), row (its top), height, portions }` (`Step::at` one row; `is_join` when taller; `halves` 2, or 3 for a join). Each step takes, from every row it covers, the latest step before it in that row, else the row's source; a join needs something in every row (`JoinMissing`); an output goes on to at most one step (`Split`); every source used (`UnusedSource`); something in every step (`NothingIn`); no two steps on one place (`Overlap`). Steps nothing takes are the outputs (`Output { step, portions, history, dish, diet, balance, quality, water }`, `water` the millilitres still in it, by column then row): a process can give several (rows left apart, an intermediate stopped early). Heat steps (Pan, Cauldron, Oven) refused with `Heat::Cold` (`NoHeat`); a Cauldron or Sieve needs water or milk in or before it (`NoLiquid`); foods with no culinary data refused (`UnknownIngredient`, `UnknownInSource`).
- `Portion::Food { source, key, grams, quality }` (none counts as Common) or `Water { ml }` (any source; no role or quality). `Carried { portions, history }`: an intermediate in a source space; `History { halves, roots, tools, rests }` (its steps in halves, a join 3; each tool's sub-skill root once, the lower kept on `merge` / `add_root`; its tools in order, a source's first; the kinds of intermediate that went in as sources, `food::FoodForm::carried`, for the long processes; `joins`, `cooked`: any heat step).
- `chance(cooking%, halves)`: Alchemy's (100% + Cooking%, halved per step over `alchemy::steps_allowed`), the part over counted in halves and rounded up to whole halvings; this session's steps only.
- `balance(portions, ingredients, wants)`: roles counted (main role full, others half; a role from 10% of the weight, seasonings Herb, Spice and Aromatic from 1%; medicinal herbs add to none), at most `ROLES_COUNTED` (4), less 1 per role the dish wants and lacks (`Wants { roles, exempt }`, `Dish::wants`: a real dish's needs, a generic one's `Generic::wants`), less ⌈points past max share ÷ `EXCESS_STEP` (20)⌉ per food unless the real dish names it (`exempt`: a fried egg is all egg; at least 1 for an inedible one, max share 0), less 1 per medicinal herb; water left out.
- `Shape::of(history, portions, water, ingredients)`: tools, joins, `Wetness` (Dry under 5% liquid, Moist under 25, Wet under 50, Soupy) from milk and the water still in it (`plan`: water taken on from a step to anything but a Cauldron is drained; a step's own water stays; milk through a Sieve loses its whey, neither wetting nor weighing: recipes plan PR 1); `distance` between tool lists, by uses whatever the order.
- `classify(portions, shape, ingredients, dishes) -> Dish { name: DishName::{Real(key), Generic { kind, foods }}, form: DishForm }` (`Dish::name(dishes)` in English, `wants`). Real when close: the same last tool, joining or not, at most one tool use more or less, wetness a band apart at most, every food it names (`specific`, groups of `Wanted::{Any(source), One(source, key)}`), its needs all there but a fat, a liquid if it wants one, the biggest role one of its needs, nothing it forbids beyond the leeway, two or more foods if it names none, and no foreign food (one it doesn't name whose main role it doesn't need, other than seasonings and fats); closest by tool distance, wetness, needs missing, its usual foods (`with`) there less missing, how well known, then table order. Else `Generic::of` by the last tool, wetness, dough (`DOUGH_PERCENT` of foods that make dough), grain and the biggest food (Soup, Stew, Porridge, Boiled, Fried, Omelette, Cake, Pie, Bread, Roast, Baked, Dough, Salad, Mixed, Noodles, Chopped, Curd, Strained, Paste), named with its biggest `NAMED_FOODS` (2) foods (`food_name`: fungi always "Mushroom", a hen's egg "Egg", cow's milk "Milk", meat by `livestock`'s meat name).
- `Diet::of(portions, ingredients)`: `vegan`, `vegetarian`, `gluten_free`, `lactose_free`, `nut_free` from the flags; `labels()`.
- `WellFed::of(portions, cooked, ingredients) -> WellFed { health, mana, bonuses }`: `WELL_FED_RESTORE` (250 thousandths a second, a first guess) split by role weight, Protein, Fat and Nut to Health, Starch and Sweet to Mana, the rest halves; bonuses from vegetables' cooked effects (raw ones if nothing was heated), fruits', fungi's food effects and each herb's first, added by stat, at most `MOST_CHANGE` (3) either way. `effect(quality, now)`: `bonus::Source::WellFed` for `well_fed_ticks` (20 minutes at Poor, 10 more a quality step).
- `poisons(portions, cooked, fungi)`: the fungi whose poison eating the dish brings on (eaten, or the ink cap's window), each once, less raw-only ones when cooked; for the server only (`fungus::poisoning` when eaten, PR 6), never in a preview.
- `least_quality(ingredients, balance, history, chance, heat)`: `ingredient_quality` (mean rank by grams, rounded down) moved by balance − `BALANCED_ROLES` (2), plus `skill_steps` (halves × Σroots ÷ (tools × 20), Alchemy's steps × mean ∛ ÷ 10) only when certain; Poor to Legendary, then `capped` at `AWAY_CAP` (Rare) unless `Heat::Hearth`. `outcome(planned, chance_roll, luck_roll) -> Outcome::{Failed, Made { qualities }}`: Alchemy's luck, still capped.
- The order (PR 9): `CookOrder { bench, rows, sources, steps, culture, count }`: `bench` a hearth's cell to work at (faced); `sources` by row, a carried intermediate or one in the hearth's storage, used whole; `OrderStep { tool, column, row, height, inputs }` with `OrderInput::{Food { stack, count }, Liquid { stack, ml }, WaterFaced { ml }}` (food each its own weight, `Item::own_weight`; water, milk or honey from a bottle in whole 100 ml, a vessel or a cask; water faced); `culture` a carried Yeast item, used only where an output ferments; `count` 1 to `MOST_REPEATS` (Alchemy's 99). `Kitchen { heat, oven, tools, storage, water_faced }`, found by the server (a lit hearth or a town: `Hearth` and the Oven; a lit campfire faced: `Fire`; else `Cold`). `Inventory::check_cooking(order, kitchen, ingredients, dishes, skills) -> Result<Checked { planned, fungi, culture }, OrderRefused>`: tools on the hearth or in the town before carried ones (`has_cooking_tool`; the Oven only from the kitchen), every stack carried and not worn with enough for one run, then `plan` (`OrderRefused::Rules`). `run_seconds` (each step's `Tool::first_seconds`, Knife, Bowl, Sieve and Mortar 10, Pan 20, Cauldron 30, Oven 40, less the sub-skill's ∛, a join half again), `heat_seconds` (the heat steps', for a hearth's Ash), `drawn(item, ml)` (a bottle emptied, a vessel or cask holding less, opened), `COOK_PRACTICE` (8 a step's sub-skill a run).
- Recipes (recipes plan, `docs/context/recipes.md`): `Recipe { rows, sources: Vec<Option<food::Rest>>, steps }` with `RecipeStep { tool, column, row, height, inputs }` and `RecipeInput::{Food { foods, count }, Liquid { foods, ml }, Water { ml }}` (`foods` any of these `Wanted`, the first the usual one): `normalised` (steps by column then row), `halves`, `chance(skills)`, `steps_beyond(skills)` (steps, or part of one, past `alchemy::steps_allowed`), `beyond_skill(skills)` (chance under 100%: the panels' note), `process(ingredients, quality, heat)` (the usual foods at a quality, `None` with an intermediate source). `unit_grams(source, key)` (one item: a herb, fruit, vegetable or fungus by its table, a grain as a sack of its flour, an egg by its animal, meat as Raw Meat, parts `PART_GRAMS` 100, salt; `None` for liquids), `is_liquid_food` (milk, honey, soy sauce), `usual_food(wanted)` (a hen's egg, cow's milk, bees' honey, beef, an apple, else the first common one). `dish_recipe(key)`: every built-in real dish's, built once; `build_recipe(def, ...)` and `makes(recipe, ...)` (cooked with Common foods at a hearth, its one output's name). `Inventory::cooking_recipe(order, storage, ingredients)`: an order as cooked, for the book (`recipe::RecipeBook::remember_cooking`). `Inventory::has_recipe_input(input, ingredients)`: enough of it carried (items of any of its foods, millilitres of any of its liquids or of water, in bottles, vessels and casks); `has_intermediate(rest)`: one of that kind carried, `intermediate_stack(rest)` which. `RecipeInput::takes(top, ingredients)` (a stack can go in for it), `amount` (items or ml), `is_liquid`; `Inventory::recipe_stacks(input, ingredients)` (every carried stack it takes, own grid before bags, never worn or a quest item): the Recipes tab's Make fills from these. `DishName::name(dishes)`: a real or generic dish's name (`Dish::name` uses it).
- `Tool::of_item`: knives and daggers are a Knife, the Pan, Bowl, Sieve, Cauldron and Mortar & pestle themselves (PR 8); the Oven has no item, it is a lit hearth's (`crafting_bench`); a lit fireplace (`shared::fireplace`) gives `Heat::Hearth` but no Oven and no tools of its own, and `CookOrder::bench` may name one.

## Notes

- A test checks every herb, fruit, vegetable and fungus has a row, and every row names something in its table (livestock and parts included): adding a food means adding its row.
- `animal_product` is true for meat too (the research had it only for what is not meat): vegetarian is no `meat`, vegan no `animal_product`.
- `max_share` 0 means it should never be in a dish (inedible things).
- Claude's choices in the rules (PR 4, for the owner to tune): the Cooking sub-skills are Cutting, Frying, Boiling, Mixing, Baking, Straining and Pounding (the Mortar's, apart from Alchemy's Grinding); the Rare cap holds for everything worked away from a hearth, cold steps too; history keeps a tool's lower root when two cooks used it, so an intermediate never lifts a dish past its weakest hand; an intermediate's portions go into the dish and its quality is worked out again from them (not added), so its steps count once; balance is a first guess: a single food is far past its max share (an egg alone loses 2, unless its real dish names it).
- Every real dish's recipe must cook into it (`recipe_tests.rs`): a dish only reachable by no process fails it, so a new or changed row of `dishes.ron` must stay distinguishable from its neighbours (the recipes plan's PR 1 changed several; `recipes.md`).
- Claude's choices in naming and its results (PR 5, for the owner to tune): only a missing fat is forgiven (the fried egg's); medicinal herbs are a bitter seasoning, adding no role and never naming a dish; the dish table's `with` foods (from the research's steps) break ties between dishes of the same shape (Pita for plain dough, Naan with milk); Well Fed's split and amount, each herb's first effect only, fungi named "Mushroom" so a dish never says which; dish names are English like other items' for now.
