# shared: mob

Who is on a site level to begin with, from the site's secret seed, and what a player is told of anything on the map. Mobs themselves are kept by the server (`server::entities`).

## Files

- `crates/shared/src/mob.rs`: `Spawn`, `spawns`, `EntityLook`. Tests. In `mob/`: `gear.rs` (`gear_of`, `roll_gear`: what a kind may start with), `memory.rs` (`Memory`), `scent.rs` (how strong a trail is, how long a nose follows it), `kept.rs` (`site_spawns`: kept animals; tests in `kept/tests.rs`), `wild.rs` (the wilderness's mobs; tests in `wild_tests.rs`), `wild_shares.rs` (who may live in a block and how likely, from the livestock table), `herd.rs` (herd members' numbers picked for their ages), `wild_looks.rs` (wild forms' own looks: aurochs), `looks.rs` (a mob's rolled looks: even choices, or a kind's weights for a trait: coos' coats 60 red-brown, 22 yellow-brown, 6 silvery brown, 6 black, 3 and 3 brindled; only among the choices its gender may have, `looks(roll, kind, gender)`), `trail.rs` (where they have wandered lately; tests in `trail_tests.rs`).

## Public API

- `spawns(seed, index, site_kind, level, stairs) -> Vec<Spawn>`: the level's mobs, each with its `number`, kind, position, appearance (fitted to its gender: no beard on a female), `gender` (`Gender::rolled` from its own hash, `GENDER_ROLL`: 45% Male, 45% Female, 10% Neutral; `own(61)` in the wilderness), `alignment` (`Alignment::rolled` from its own hash, `ALIGNMENT_ROLL`, `own(62)` in the wilderness: about its kind's leaning, `None` for animals; secret, never in `EntityLook`), `attributes` (a build rolled near the centre, `sheet::Build::rolled`, plus its kind's and gender's bonuses) `penned` (a kept animal: the server never lets it through a gate or over a fence), `band` (`behaviour::Band`: on a site one per race, `Band::of_site(seed, kind)`, the same on every level, kept animals too; in the wilderness one per pack or herd, its block's roll; `spawn_at`'s by its seed and kind), `family` (reserved, always `None`), `gear` (kinds and qualities, rolled by `item::roll_drop`; beggars, kobolds and goblins may carry fruit or a vegetable, which the server makes what they foraged there, `shared::produce`) and `coins` (its money in copper, one roll of its own in `mob/purse.rs`: a chance and a range per kind, first guesses; the server gives it as the fewest coins).
- `site_spawns(seed, index, site_kind, site_level, stairs, land: Habitat) -> Vec<Spawn>` (`mob/kept.rs`, livestock plan PR 8): what the server makes a site level's mobs from (its visit and the wilderness's copy): `spawns`, but a farm's ground floor gets kept animals instead (a ruined one, with no pens, keeps `spawns`' chickens, horses and cattle) and a town's adds them, numbered from 1000. By the livestock table's `kept` biomes (home ×3, else ×1): one grazer kind per pasture field (cattle favoured ×6; 2 to 1 per 16 cells, at most 6), pigs in a sty (goats or sheep where pigs aren't kept), 3 to 6 poultry of one kind in a yard (chickens ×6; a town's yard 25% one or two goats or pigs instead), 2 to 4 pigeons in a yard with a dovecote, 2 to 5 ducks, Muscovies or geese on ground within 2 of a farm's pond. Rolled as `spawn_at` from `derive("kept animals")` (looks, gender 45/45/10, so Neutral castrates), never on a shelter or dovecote cell; ages from their ids (`stage_of`), so mixed. `Spawn::penned` is true for them.
- `who_lives_in(site_kind) -> &[(CreatureKind, weight)]`: who can be found in a kind of place, how many of each in 100 (what `spawns` rolls from; `shared::quest` picks the kinds quests ask to be slain from it).
- `chosen_spawns(seed, kind, count, level, clear_of) -> Vec<Spawn>` (`mob/chosen.rs`): developer tools' mobs of one kind (a Dev Area), on any walkable cell at least `FROM_STAIRS` from each of `clear_of`, one to a cell, rolled as a level's own; fewer if no room is found in 64 tries each. `spawn_at(seed, kind, number, position)`: one rolled the same way at a place the caller chose (quest dungeons, `quest::QuestDungeon::spawns`).
- `Habitat { biome, metres, celsius }` (whole metres and °C at a block's middle: `Wilderness::habitat_of`; `Habitat::typical(biome)` for the development area). `wild_spawns(seed, block, level, land: Habitat) -> Vec<Spawn>`: a wilderness block's creatures from the secret seed and its position alone: nothing in 45% of blocks (75% in desert), else a pack or herd of one kind round a den on open ground (`Ground` or `Grass`; water birds' within 3 cells of water), 1 to 3 grown and, for kinds in the livestock table, in 40% of herds 1 or 2 young too, led by their mother (female). Who (`wild_shares::shares`, weights): every table kind's wild form in its wild biomes by rarity (very rare 1, rare 3, uncommon 8, common 16, very common 32, × 4) and feral herd in its kept biomes (× 1), each by `Wild::place` (mountains: a quarter at the sea to full at 1500 m; high: none at the sea; by water, near people (roads) and cliffs (water, roads or 1000 m up): only there, at half) and `Wild::warmth` (cold up to 14 °C, hot from 15 °C); wolves by biome (48 in forests and tundra, 24 woodland, 4 grassland and savanna, none in desert); coos (highland cattle) a share of the whole in tundra, taiga, woodland and grassland, never where hot (`coo_percent`): 15% in low mild land, rising with cold (full at 0 °C, none from 15 °C, counting twice) and height (full at 1500 m) to 40%. Wild forms are never Neutral, and wild cattle are aurochs (their coat, bulls an eel stripe). Herd ages (`herd.rs`): numbers are tried (256) until their ids' ages (`livestock::age_hours`) put the young within their young stage of the herd's season and the grown a growing up and more older, so the mother is grown while they are small and a herd is never only young; nothing saved. Not on farms. Each number is a hash, its own across the wilderness. `wild_mob_id(seed, number)`: their ids (UUID v5 of a hash), always the same, so nothing needs saving for one nobody has touched.
- `wild_trail(seed, number, home, level) -> Vec<cell>`: where a wild mob has wandered lately, `home` first: `TRAIL_SHORTEST` (24) to `TRAIL_LONGEST` (96) legal steps out (`movement::mob_step_on_level`, never onto a World Gate), keeping a heading that turns an eighth one step in four, going round what is in the way, stopping at the level's edge or where it can't go on.
- `Memory` (`set_standing` (dev tools: Create Mob's attitude), `remember_gift`, `remember_harm(who, harm, start)`, `remember_attacking(who)`, `remember_band_harmed(who)` (it saw them harm its band or an ally: unfriendly, and provoked unless they had already struck it), `remember_trespass`, `forget_old`, `standing(who, start)`, `opinion(who)`, `provoked(who)`), `Event` (`Gave` from older saves only, `Trespassed(tick)`, `Regard`), `Regard { standing, opinion, harmed, provoked }` (`provoked`: it attacked them, or was unfriendly to them (a trespass too), before their first blow, so killing it is self-defence and costs no alignment; false in older saves), `GIVE_REACH`, `GIFT_OPINION`, `FIRST_HARM_OPINION`, `TRESPASS_FORGOTTEN_AFTER` (3 minutes of action ticks): what a mob remembers about characters (owner, 2026-11-07). Two things per character, kept apart: its **standing** (`Feeling`: what it does and the token's colour; its kind's `feeling_about` until something changes it) and its **opinion** (a number in thousandths of a Health point, starting at 0, which never sets the standing by itself). A first blow lowers the standing a step (friendly to neutral with the opinion lowered by `max(harm, 10 points)`; neutral to unfriendly with the opinion set to minus that); any later blow makes it unfriendly and lowers the opinion by the harm. Gifts only raise the opinion (10 points). A trespass makes it unfriendly while remembered: only the latest per character, forgotten after that time. A regard is never forgotten or pushed out by the 32-thing limit; older saves' gifts are counted into the opinion when read.
- Scent: `scent_strength(inventory)` (percent: `BASE_SCENT` 100 carrying nothing, plus 4 per kg worn and 1 per kg carried, up to `MOST_SCENT` 200; weight stands in for metal), `smells_for(acuity)` (action ticks on the weakest trail: poor 30 s, normal 2 min, keen 5 min, none 0), `can_smell(acuity, strength, laid, now)` (the nose's time × strength), `SCENT_KEPT_FOR` (keenest × strongest: 10 min; older marks can go).
- `EntityLook { id, kind, gender, name, appearance, feeling, x, y, worn, boss, stage, light, never_trades }`: all a player learns of a mob or another player. `boss`: a quest boss's `BossLook { boss, tier }` (its story `Boss`, named on hover, and its tier, a `Quality` it glows in), `None` for anyone else. Never stats. `gender` is for words about it later (pronouns). `name` is another player's character's name (`None` for mobs, which go by their kind). `worn` is what can be seen of what it wears (`Inventory::shown`), for its picture; `heading` the way it faces (`facing::Heading`); `stage` its stage of life (`livestock::Stage`: a mob's from `livestock::stage_of`, players and escortees grown), for its body; `light` how far its light reaches in cells (a player's `Inventory::light_reach`, from `shared::lantern`; 0 for mobs and escortees, which carry none: mobs have no darkness rule yet); `never_trades` a player set never to trade (`trade::TradingSetting::Never`), greyed in the trade window's list.

## Notes

- Nobody starts on furniture (`spawns`, `chosen_spawns`; quest dungeons' `QuestDungeon::spawns` too).

- Worked out apart from the level's shape, so changing them moves no walls and needs no generator version. Only the server calls it: the seed is secret.
- Which kinds live where, and how many, are first guesses: a few beggars in towns, animals on farms (chickens, horses and cattle), mostly kobolds elsewhere, with goblins, orcs, a few chickens and wolves, the odd giant and some lizardfolk (kobolds start without boots, as they stand on their toes). A number stays with its mob, even if a place couldn't be found for an earlier one. What each kind may start with (`gear_of`) is too, and a blade is then made for what the mob can lift and suits its size, on the server, or left out if none can be; what is worn is made at the mob's size and put straight on (armour made again, a few times, when it would work at under half for the mob: server `entities/suited.rs`), other things are `Medium` and go in its pockets, and a test checks that whoever can have something can wear or carry it; animals have nothing. Nobody starts within `FROM_STAIRS` (6) cells of stairs, so waiting for a reset can't put one beside the way in.
