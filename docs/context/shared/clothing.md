# shared: clothing

Clothes on the Middle layer: everything worn over underclothes and under cloaks and armour, footwear apart. So far tunics, robes, doublets, bodices and vests on the torso, trousers and skirts on the legs, belts and gloves, hoods, caps, headcloths, headdresses, neckwear and shawls, and hats on the Outer layer (PRs 1 to 3 of 6; models follow). Medieval and earlier first, later pieces (to 1700) rarer. What one is like is rolled once and saved, with its names, cells and weight. Not the rules for wearing (`wear`), nor underclothes (`underclothes`), nor models (client `item_art`, still to come).

## Files

- `crates/shared/src/clothing/mod.rs`: `ClothingForm` (`piece`), the module list and re-exports.
- `piece.rs`: `ClothingPiece` (one for each item kind, `of`, `kind`; in `ItemKind::ALL_CLOTHING`'s order).
- `garment.rs`: `Garment` (which garment and how it is cut: `piece`, `bare`), passing each question to the garment's own file.
- One file per garment, each with its options, grid, roll, names by signature and base weight: `tunic/` (`Tunic`, `Length`, `Cut`, `Sleeves`, `Closure`, `Neck`, `TunicDecoration`; tables in `tables.rs`, names and weight in `names.rs`), `robe.rs` (`Robe`, `RobeLength`, `Front`, `Fastening`, `RobeSleeves`, `Collar`, `Lining`, `RobeDecoration`), `doublet.rs` (`Doublet`, `DoubletLength`, `Padding`, `DoubletFastening`, `DoubletCollar`, `DoubletSleeves`, `DoubletDecoration`; `arming`), `bodice.rs` (`Bodice`, `BodiceLength`, `Lacing`, `BodiceNeck`, `BodiceSleeves`, `Stiffening`), `vest.rs` (`Vest`, `VestLength`, `VestMake`, `VestFastening`, `VestDecoration`), `trousers.rs` (`Trousers`, `TrouserLength`, `Fullness`, `TrouserWaist`, `LegEnds`, `TrouserFront`; leg wraps a flag), `skirt.rs` (`Skirt`, `SkirtLength`, `SkirtMake`, `SkirtWaist`, `SkirtFront`), `belt.rs` (`Belt`, `BeltForm`, `BeltWidth`, `BeltEnd`, `Fittings`, `BeltFastening`; pleated sashes a flag), `gloves.rs` (`Gloves`, `Fingers`, `Cuff`, `GloveMake`, `GloveDecoration`; fur lining a flag), `hood.rs` (`Hood`, `HoodCape`, `Tail`, `HoodFace`, `Edge`; lappets and chaperon flags), `cap.rs` (`Cap`, `CapShape`, `CapMake`, `CapBrim`, `CapTop`; ear flaps a flag), `headcloth.rs` (`Headcloth`, `HeadclothForm`, `Reach`, `TurbanSize`; face covered and lace flags), `headdress.rs` (`Headdress`, `HeaddressForm`, `HeaddressVeil`, `HeaddressDecoration`), `neckwear.rs` (`Neckwear`, `NeckwearForm`, `RuffSize`), `shawl.rs` (`Shawl`, `ShawlForm`, `ShawlLength`, `ShawlCollar`), `hat.rs` (`Hat`, `Crown`, `HatBrim`, `HatMake`, `HatDecoration`; chin strap a flag). The new garments keep their own materials lists (`materials`), which `cloth.rs` asks for.
- `cloth.rs`: `Weave` (plain, velvet, brocade, damask), `Pattern` (plain, striped, checked, tartan); materials, weaves, patterns, dyes and second colours by garment.
- `roll.rs`: `roll`. `grid.rs`: `allowed`. `names.rs`: `specific_name`, `name`. `weight.rs`: `grams`, `grams_at`, `shape` (fur linings, belts' metal and headdresses' pearls added after the cloth). The weighted tables use `underclothes::table` (`has`, `when`).

## Public API

- `roll(rng, piece, quality)`: a `ClothingForm` for a `ClothingPiece::{Tunic, Robe, Doublet, Bodice, Vest, Trousers, Skirt, Belt, Gloves, Hood, Cap, Headcloth, Headdress, Neckwear, Shawl, Hat}` (the item kinds of the same names; `ClothingPiece::of(kind)`).
- `ClothingForm { garment, material, weave, pattern, dye, contrast }`: `piece`, `allowed`, `name` (a `String`), `specific_name`, `shape`, `grams`, `grams_at(size)`.
- `Garment::{Tunic(Tunic), Robe(..), Doublet(..), Bodice(..), Vest(..), Trousers(..), Skirt(..), Belt(..), Gloves(..), Hood(..), Cap(..), Headcloth(..), Headdress(..), Neckwear(..), Shawl(..), Hat(..)}`; `Garment::bare()`: the arms of a sleeveless (or one-shouldered) tunic or bodice, fingerless gloves' Fingers (so rings stay free), the Neck of a headcloth on the head only or of a shawl without a collar, read through `Item::bare` / `Item::worn_locations`. `Doublet::arming()`.

## Notes

- Every choice is rolled from what the earlier ones allow (garment, material, weave, pattern, dye, second colour); `allowed` is that grid, checked by a test, as are every name turning up, bare arms and weights.
- Owner's decisions (2026-11-15, the research's review): one garment on the torso on Middle, over-garments (gowns, jerkins over doublets, haori) left for an Outer research; tunics and robes list only the Torso and Arms, their length a property, so trousers, skirts and belts go with any; a doublet may be made for arming (quilted, with points) and goes under unlined plate as an arming vest does, which the layers already give (lined armour takes Middle and clashes); regional names kept as specific names, up to 1700 with later ones rarer; the dalmatic as the Byzantine court tunic, no liturgical names; velvet, brocade and damask are kinds of silk (velvet of wool too) and tartan a pattern like stripes; Middle clothes drop on death on Hard.
- PR 2 (owner, 2026-11-15): skirts, kilts, wraps and the dhoti are a Skirt on the Legs (Middle), clashing with trousers, not belts, leg armour over them; leg wraps are a trousers option, no item; one Belt kind rolled into strap, cord girdle, sash, obi and plates, every one with the belt's bag slots (`item::Inventory`, by kind and quality); one Gloves kind rolled into gloves, mittens and fingerless (Hands only); padded gloves stay armour; the baldric waits for sheaths. Trousers, Belt and Gloves were placeholder kinds and now roll forms (old items without one load as before); the old `FingerlessGloves` kind stays for saves but is no longer made or sold. Plated belts, elbow-length and perfumed gloves are rolled only at Rare and better. Kilts are mostly tartan wool, tube skirts often checked (the Longyi).
- PR 3 (owner, 2026-11-15): hoods list the Head and Neck, their cape a property, so a hood and a collarless shawl (Shoulders, its Neck bare) go together; caps, headcloths and headdresses are Middle on the Head, headcloths to the shoulders the Neck too; neckwear is Middle on the Neck, over a necklace (Skin). Brimmed and stiff hats are Outer on the Head, over all of them: the old placeholder Hat kind now rolls a form (old items without one load as before), and clashes with helmets. Skullcaps (Skullcap, Taqiyah, Kufi) are Linen Coif forms in `underclothes`, so a turban goes over them; felt, fur and knitted caps stay here. No Jewish hat; turbans and veils keep plain names (never dastar, niqab or burqa); the square cloth with a cord is a Headcloth, often checked, not a keffiyeh. A hood's tail to the ankle and a cartwheel ruff come only at Rare and better. Hats and caps say their material unless felt or wool, or their name says it (Straw Hat, Ushanka, Fez).
- Velvet, brocade and damask are a `Weave` on the form, not materials, so a velvet doublet is still silk or wool; Grass is a new `Material` (Plant) for the grass skirt to come. Buff is the existing Buff Leather.
- A tunic trailing to the floor (rank only) is rolled only at Rare and better; the grid allows it at any quality.
- A name comes from its garment's signature (a curved flap is a Deel); a pattern, a weave or an unusual material goes first ("Striped Silk Robe", "Velvet Doublet"), except where the name says it (a Jerkin and Lederhosen are leather, a Grass Skirt grass, a Kaunakes sheepskin (Fur), tartan is wool).
- Weights are worked out from cloth areas in plain linen, changed by material (buff leather 4.5 ×) and weave (velvet 1.6 ×), plus fur linings; by the square of the size, as underclothes.
- Research: `docs/context/research-clothing.md`. Players' page: `docs/clothing.md`, kept in step with this module.
