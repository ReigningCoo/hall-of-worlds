# shared: quest

Quests generated from a town's seed, the same for everyone: who gives them (town buildings, by slots), what they ask (a step graph on hooks of nearby pois or the wilds round the town), what they need, what they pay, how they move alignment, and their story through `shared::story`. Only the server calls the generator: pois' seeds are secret. Offering, accepting, progress and saves are the server's (`server/server.md`, `quests`). Plan: `quests.md`.

## Files

- `crates/shared/src/quest.rs`: `mod` lines and re-exports.
- Model: `quest/kind.rs` (`QuestKind`), `id.rs` (`QuestId`, `Slot`, `earlier`), `requirement.rs` (`Requirement`, `Need`, `Standing`), `step.rs` (`Step`, `Target`, `Alternative`, `Way`), `hook.rs` (`Hook`, what a poi offers, the kind its kills are of), `model.rs` (`Quest`, open steps).
- Generator: `world.rs` (`QuestWorld`, `radius`, `is_larger`, distances, pois near a poi), `owner.rs` (who owns an exclusive hook), `area.rs` (a town's candidates by weight), `givers.rs` (`Giver`, `givers`, the quest seed, each building's household), `template.rs` (a hook's steps, a second step at the same poi, a poi's boss and unique item), `herb_ask.rs` (`HerbAsk`: a gathering step's herb, chosen from those growing wild at its place (the town's own for the wilds, a herbalist's hut's) that the giver's town's shops couldn't stock (`shop::could_stock_herb`); `RARER_PERCENT` (25%) ask a rarer herb at its own quality; counting what is carried, bags at any depth, and taking exactly the count, the poorest first; tests in `herb_ask/tests.rs`), `remains.rs` (`natural_parts(kind)`: the parts a kind has that a quest may ask for, by its body (`body::Parts::of`, `has_hands`): fingernails with hands, toenails on a person's feet, fangs on a muzzle, claws, a tail, horns, tusks, ears, feathers, hooves, scales on kobolds and lizardfolk, hearts and livers on all; `remains_of(poi, kind)`, the poi's own, from its seed), `plan.rs` (a plan's steps and the facts for its story), `reward.rs`, `telling.rs` (the `story::Request`), `make.rs` (a plan into a `Quest`), `slot.rs` (a slot's chain, its needs), `moon.rs` (`moon_need`: a hidden wait for one of the world's moons), `neighbour.rs` (`add_neighbour_needs`: givers' first quests needing another giver's), `opening.rs` (`add_standing_needs`: standing for givers' chains), `town.rs` (`town_quests`), `feeder.rs` and `feeder_quest.rs` (chains across towns), `daily.rs`, `dungeon.rs` (a quest's own dungeon: `QuestDungeon`, `DungeonWay`, `DUNGEON_KIND`, `HIDDEN_KIND`, `plan_cellar`), `hidden.rs` (`plan_hidden`: a larger dungeon by a hidden entrance near a town, or `large` for the artifact line's end), `artifact_line.rs` (a city's artifact line), `prize.rs` (`PRIZE_KINDS`, `set_item_of`, `artifact_kind`), `stage.rs` (stages: `QuestDungeon::stage_door`, `key_holder`).
- Tests: `herb_tests.rs` (gathering's herbs over the test world's towns and a month of dailies: growing at the place, named as an item in the words, rarer ones at their own quality paying more, shops' stock kept off, none where no herb grows), `artifact_tests.rs` (cities' lines: needs, one giver, the prize at the end, the large dungeon peopled), `prize/tests.rs`, `tests.rs` (the rules over a few dozen towns of the test world), `dungeon_tests.rs` (cellars among normal quests and dailies, and hidden ways down near towns and cities, in empty chunks on open land; all generated and peopled enough for their counts; stage doors cutting the way down off, with keys on the near side), `neighbour_tests.rs` (needs between givers, moons, dark deeds), `golden_tests.rs`, `print_tests.rs` (`cargo test -p shared print_quests -- --ignored --nocapture` prints three towns' quests and dailies as a player reads them, with what the server keeps), `test_world.rs`.

## Public API

- `QuestWorld { pois, roads, name_of, moons, herbs, habitat_of }`: what the generator reads; `name_of(&Poi) -> Option<String>` is the server's `place_name_of` (towns, castles, forts); `moons` the world's moon count (`Celestial::moon_count`); `herbs` the server's table (mods too), `habitat_of(&Poi) -> Habitat` the land there (`ServerWorld::habitat_of_poi`), for gathering's herbs.
- `town_quests(world, town, buildings) -> Vec<Quest>`: a town's quests but its dailies (`buildings`: its site's `BuildingLook`s): every giver's slots, each a chain, then its feeder, then in a city its artifact line. Empty for anything but a town.
- `PRIZE_KINDS` (weapons, armour pieces, jewellery), `set_item_of(planet seed value) -> (ItemKind, Quality)` (the planet's set item, Common to Legendary), `artifact_kind(seed)`: what the artifact line's end is made as (the server makes it, from a seed of the character and quest so it is the same on every try).
- `daily_givers(town, buildings)`, `daily_quest(world, town, buildings, building, day) -> Option<Quest>`, `utc_day(unix_seconds)`, `SECONDS_PER_DAY`.
- `givers(town, buildings) -> Vec<Giver { building, kind, slots, daily }>`.
- `feeder_destination(world, town) -> Option<Poi>` (the larger road neighbour its feeder goes to), `feeds_into(world, town)` (smaller neighbours whose feeders come here), `is_larger(a, b)` (size, then chunk), `radius(kind, daily)` (chunks: hamlet 3, village 4, town 6, city 7; dailies half).
- `owner(world, poi, hook) -> Option<ChunkPos>`: the town whose quests may use an exclusive hook (`None` for shared ones).
- `Quest { id, kind, building, member, requirements, steps, reward, deeds, story, ending, dungeon }`; `open_steps(done)`, `ready_to_turn_in(done)`, `is_hidden_by_alignment()`. `story` goes with the offer; `ending` stays on the server until turn-in.
- `QuestId { town, slot }`, `Slot::{Giver { building, slot, chain }, Feeder, Daily { building, day }, Artifact { step }}`, `earlier()`. Serde: the id is all a save or a message keeps; the rest is made again.
- `QuestKind::{Normal, Daily, Artifact}`.
- `HerbAsk { herb, least }` (`herb` a herb's key; `least` Poor for any, or a rarer herb's own quality): `counts(top)`, `carried(&Inventory)` (every stack of it at `least` or better, own grid, worn bags and bags in bags, never a quest item), `take(&mut Inventory, count, new_id) -> bool` (exactly `count`, poorest then smallest stacks first; nothing if too few), `steps_above_common()`. `RARER_PERCENT`.
- `Requirement { need, hidden }` (`shown`, `hidden`, `hides_quest`), `Need::{Standing { town, least }, Done(QuestId), Skill { skill, least }, Alignment(Band), Moon { moon }}` (`Moon`: that moon, by number among the world's moons, in the sky when taken; always hidden), `Standing::{Stranger, Known, Trusted, Honoured, Champion}`.
- `Step { goal, target, count, after, alternatives, herb }` (`is_open(done)`; `herb`: a herb-gathering step's `HerbAsk`, `None` for every other step, a creature part's Collect included), `Target::{Poi { chunk, hook }, Wilds { chunk }, Town { chunk }, Dungeon { chunk, hook, way }}` (`Dungeon`: in the quest's own dungeon, entered in the giver's town `chunk`; hooks `Kills(kind)` and `Explore`), `Alternative { way, skill }`, `Way::{Carry, Tame, Intimidate}`.
- `Hook::{Boss, Item, Escortee, Kills(kind), Explore, Visit, Gather, Collect(kind)}` (`Collect`: a part of the poi's creatures of its counted kills' kind brought back, worded as a Gather step, "Gather kobold scales 2/5"; shared, weight 2), `is_exclusive`, `of_poi(&Poi)`.
- `QuestDungeon { way, poi, levels, size, creatures, per_level }` (`Quest::dungeon`): what a quest's own dungeon is made from; `poi` is made up (`DUNGEON_KIND`, `Barrels`, a dug cellar, at the giver's town's chunk and point, with the dungeon's own seed); `generator()` (the cave generator, a hole in, doors, levels exactly `levels`), `spawns(index, level, stairs)` (`per_level` of `creatures` on walkable cells, the furthest from the stairs first, one to a cell, by `mob::spawn_at`), `stage_door(index, site_level)` (on every level but the deepest, the door that, shut, cuts the stairs down off from where the level is come into, nearest those stairs; none where every way down has more than one door: secret doors never can, as the generator keeps everything reachable without them), `key_holder(index, site_level, door, holders)` (the first of the level's creatures reachable with it shut). `DungeonWay::Cellar` (by the giver's own door, "Enter Cellar"), `Hidden` (its `poi` is `HIDDEN_KIND`, `Boulders`, at its entrance in a chunk of its own with no poi, `Target::Dungeon::chunk` that chunk; the server shows it to the quester alone).
- `boss_of(&Poi) -> story::Boss`: a poi's quest boss (kind, gender, name, epithet) from its own seed: the server spawns it as the story names it. `boss_tier(&Poi) -> Quality`: the boss's tier (owner: a better version of its kind; Claude's weights: Uncommon 50, Rare 30, Epic 15, Legendary 5), from the poi's seed. `item_of(&Poi) -> story::ItemCategory`: its unique item, a keepsake, which the server makes as the story names it.

## Notes

- Seeds: everything from `poi.seed.derive("quests")` (per giver, slot, chain or day by `at`), drawing nothing from the site's sequences. A poi's quest boss, unique item and kills kind come from that poi's own seed ("quest boss", "quest item", "quest kills"), so every town's quest and the site agree. Story and household seeds are derived, never the poi seed itself. Changing a list, weight or draw order changes every town's quests (golden test).
- Not overlapping (owner's rule): a boss, an item and a lost one (exclusive hooks) belong to one of the towns reaching the poi, by `hash(poi, hook)` (`owner`), and within a town to one quest. Kills, exploring, visiting and gathering are shared; each use within a town halves a hook's chances. Dailies only use shared hooks.
- Givers (Claude's figures, *to balance*): 30% of houses one slot; other buildings one, plus a second by town size (10 to 40%); inns and a quarter of other shops give dailies. Slot `n`'s giver is the building's household's member `n`; townsfolk are human until towns have peoples. Chains are 1 to 3 quests (2 in hamlets): each needs the one before, and keeps its reason, giver and relatives (`Story::carry`). 8% of chains outside hamlets need an Evil alignment (hidden entirely) and are told dark; handing one in is `Deed::DarkQuest` instead of help. 30% of gathering quests have a hidden herbalism need. 6% of givers' quests outside hamlets wait for a moon (`MOON_PERCENT`, hidden, by its own seed so nothing else moves; none on a world without moons).
- Standing (owner: a stranger sees only a few quests in a town, more as they become known; Claude's figures, *to balance*): once a town's other needs are set (`opening`), `OPEN` first quests (hamlet 2, village and town 3, city 4), one a building, chosen by hash from those needing no other quest, alignment or moon, need no standing; the other first quests need Honoured (15%, towns and cities), else Known (half) or Trusted; each later part of a chain one tier more than the one before, at most Honoured. The server hides a quest needing two tiers or more beyond a character's standing. Each quest of a chain longer than one has `Story::part` (its number and the chain's length; the artifact line's too).
- Herb gathering (owner, 2026-11-27): a gathering step (`Hook::Gather`, in the wilds round the town or at a herbalist's hut) names one herb (`HerbAsk`), drawn after its count from those growing wild at the step's place (biome and temperature, `HerbDef::suits`), commoner ones more often, never one the giver's town's shops could stock (`shop::could_stock_herb`: none sells herbs yet, so this rules nothing out). A place where no herb fits makes no gathering candidate (`town_candidates`), so another hook is chosen. 25% ask a rarer herb (Uncommon or Rare, where one grows) at its own quality, as a herb's quality is its plant's rarity; the step then pays half as much again per quality step above Common (`reward.rs`; *to follow item values once they exist*). The story names the herb (`Facts::herb`), its items' words written `${kind:herb:<key>}`. Shops checked are the giver's town's (an artifact line's neighbours use the city's).
- Templates: one step on a hook, sometimes (35%) a second at the same poi: kills before a boss or exploring before a find (in series), or kills alongside a find or exploring (in parallel). Escorts are of a pet (other ways: carry it, or tame it with Animals) or of a relative (Rescued). Gathering is herbs, at a herbalist's hut or in the wilds round the town. Turning in isn't a step: it follows them all.
- Across towns (owner): only smaller road neighbours are read, never their quests. A link counts whether or not water cuts its road. Each town's feeder (a delivery) goes to one larger neighbour by its own hash; that town gives one first quest of a chain (not a dark one) a need for it. Within a town (owner: "I heard you helped X"), 25% of givers' first quests (`NEIGHBOUR_PERCENT`, each by its own seed) need another giver's first quest; only quests needing nothing done are used either way, so needs go one deep and never round.
- Cellars (owner: some quests make a dungeon of their own, commoner for dailies; Claude's figures, *to balance*): 10% of normal quests and 60% of dailies (owner: dailies mostly in quest dungeons) are a cellar of the giver's own instead of a hook nearby, rolled before choosing the hook. Levels 1 in hamlets and villages, up to 2 in towns and 3 in cities (dailies always 1); levels 28–36 cells a side in a hamlet, 4 to 6 more per town size; kobolds, goblins, lizardfolk or orcs (`CELLAR_CREATURES`, nothing too big for the passages); a Slay step of 3 + size to 5 + 2 × size, and on more than one level an Explore step alongside; one creature spare per level. Told as a whole `PlaceKind::Cellar` "in town", near. Hidden ways down (owner: larger ones by a hidden entrance near the town; Claude's figures): 8% more of the normal quests of towns and cities (`HIDDEN_PERCENT`, on the same roll after the cellars' share), at a point within 3 chunks of the town in a chunk with no poi, on open land (`PoiMap::is_open_land`), 24 tries else an ordinary quest; 2 levels up to size + 1, 40–52 cells a side plus 4 to 6 per size, any of the wild places' creatures (`Ruins`' table), a Slay step of 5 + 2 × size to 9 + 3 × size and an Explore step alongside; told as the poi's place (a ruin) by direction and distance, and paid by distance.
- Rewards: copper by town size, work, distance and chain place, rounded to 5, dailies half; 20% of normal quests add an item (a weapon, ring, tool, remedy, book or recipe: `story::ItemCategory::Recipe`, a recipe scroll of a real dish, the only one the server gives yet, `quests/prize.rs`), training or a favour (the story then may tell season and weather, which aren't set yet).
- Generating a town's quests takes milliseconds (the tests make a few dozen towns' quests in about 0.15 s); the server should still keep them per town.
- Artifact line (owner: cities only, Honoured or more, a long chain across the city's road neighbours, ending in an artifact or the planet's set item by the city's seed, the last a large quest dungeon; Claude's figures, *to balance*): from its own seed (`"artifact line"`), so nothing else moves. One giver (a non-house giver, else any), the household member after its slots'; 5 to 7 quests (`Slot::Artifact { step }`), each needing Honoured standing and the one before (shown, so a grey "!" and hints until then), told as one chain; every one but the last is a whole quest round one of the city's road neighbours in turn, on shared hooks only (as dailies), so the neighbour's own quests keep theirs; the last is a hidden way down near the city, 4 or 5 levels of 56–68 cells plus 4 to 6 per size, 12 + 3 × size to 18 + 4 × size to slay. Its reward adds `RewardPart::SetItem` (half of cities) or `RewardPart::Artifact`. A city with no road neighbour or no room for the entrance has no line.
