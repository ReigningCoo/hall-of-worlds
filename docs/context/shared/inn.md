# shared: inn

The Way Inn's fixed layout (The Way Inn plan, `docs/context/the-way-inn.md`): its two floors as site levels, written in code like `shared::hall`, with no generator and no seed. Only shapes (cells, rooms, doors, stairs), players' own rooms with their furniture, and the places kept for the rest of the furniture (PR 5).

## Files

- `crates/shared/src/inn/mod.rs`: `FLOORS`, `GROUND`, `UPSTAIRS`, `WIDTH` (41), `DEPTH` (50), `level`, `rooms`. In `inn/`: `room.rs` (`RoomKind`, `InnRoom`, `CORNER_TIER`), `builder.rs` (`Built`: carving rooms out of solid wall, doors closed or locked, windows; `span`), `ground.rs` (the ground floor), `upstairs.rs` (the H, the bedrooms' rooms and doors, `own_rooms`), `upstairs/bedrooms.rs` (where every bedroom is and its tier: the crossbar's rows, the strips, the joined room; `lengths`), `spots.rs` (fixed places, public), `site.rs` (`KIND`, `site`, `is_way_out`, `look_only`), `own_room.rs` (`OwnRoom`, `own_rooms`, `own_room_at`). `furnish.rs` (PR 5: every piece and decor, placed in code) and in `furnish/`: `bar.rs` (storage behind the bar, the back fireplace, the counter with stools and candles, the front fireplaces with armchairs, the foyer), `rows.rs` (private rooms' tables, booths), `tables.rs` (the scattered groups), `back_rooms.rs` (kitchen, treasury, brewing, ageing), `bedroom.rs` (every bedroom and own room by tier), `decor.rs` (hangings, sconces, chandeliers, rugs).

## Public API

- `level(index) -> Option<SiteLevel>`: `GROUND` (0) or `UPSTAIRS` (1); one staircase each on `spots::STAIRS` (Up to level 1, Down to level 0); the ground floor's `entrance` is `spots::ARRIVAL` (inside the entrance door). Doors start closed; the treasury's, every plain bedroom's and the own rooms' above `OPEN_TIER` are locked (`Level::is_locked`). Furnished and decorated (`furnish`), the same every time. Cells `0..WIDTH` × `0..DEPTH`, the outer wall all round, no windows out.
- `rooms(index) -> Vec<InnRoom>`: `InnRoom { id, kind, rects, door }` in id order (ids from 1), `contains`, `area`. `RoomKind::is_public` (shared by everyone in an instance: all but bedrooms and own rooms; things left lying in these are cleared; a private room downstairs is shared). `RoomKind`: `Foyer` (with the archway's cell), `Main` (the bar, its gap and storage too), `PrivateRoom` (doored), `Booth` (open), `Kitchen`, `Brewing`, `Ageing`, `Treasury`, `Corridor`, `Bedroom { tier }` (1 beside the stairs up to `CORNER_TIER` 4, the largest; locked for now), `OwnRoom { tier }` (one a tier, a strip's bedrooms: each player's own copy).
- `OPEN_TIER` (1: the own room every character has; larger tiers bought later, locked until then). `own_rooms() -> Vec<OwnRoom { tier, rect, door }>` smallest first (worked out without building the floor, so cheap to call), `own_room_at(index, cell) -> Option<tier>` (upstairs only; the door is not in it). Every bedroom and own room has, by its tier, a bed against the north wall (the south one when the door is in the north wall) in the corner away from its door, its chests beside it along that wall, its wardrobe on the side wall past the bed's foot (tier 1: single bed, small chest, narrow wardrobe; 2: a large chest; 3 and 4: a double bed, two chests, a wide wardrobe).
- `KIND` (`PoiKind::Castle`: what the inn counts as where a site's kind is asked, its stairs' styles and look), `site()` (the inn as a `site::Site`, `Generator::Inn`, `INN_VERSION`), `is_way_out(index, cell)` (the entrance door on the ground floor: stepping on it leaves), `look_only(index, piece)` (chests and wardrobes anywhere but own rooms: the foyer's, the treasury's, locked bedrooms'; never opened, client and server).
- `spots`: `MIDDLE_X`, `STAIRS`, `ENTRANCE`, `ARRIVAL`, `FOYER`, `ARCHWAY`, `FOYER_WEST` / `FOYER_EAST` (its furniture), `ROW_FIRST_Y`, `ROW_PITCH`, `ROW_COUNT` (private rooms and booths), `STORAGE`, `BAR_GAP`, `BAR`, `BAR_FLAP`, `BACK_FIREPLACE`, `FRONT_FIREPLACES`, `TABLES` (where scattered tables go), `KITCHEN_DOOR`, `BREWING_DOOR`, `AGEING_DOOR`, `STAIR_DOWN_CORNER`, `TREASURY`, `TREASURY_DOOR`, `TREASURY_WINDOWS`. Rectangles are `site::Rect`.

## Used by

## Notes

- Golden test: both floors' fingerprints (cells, rooms, doors' state, stairs); changing the layout changes them, only on purpose. Print both floors: `cargo test -p shared print_inn -- --ignored --nocapture`.
- Upstairs (owner's layout, 2026-11-23): between the legs, a row of 4 small rooms along the crossbar's north side (3, 4, 4, 3 wide) and 3 along its south (5 each), 3 deep, doors on the crossbar, tier 1. Behind them, and outside each leg, strips run to an outer wall, doors on the legs; bedroom lengths 3, 4, 5... while one a row longer still fits after, the last taking the rest (`lengths`). Tiers: outer strips 1 upwards, strips between the legs 2, 3, then 4, the last of each `CORNER_TIER`. The two far-north rooms between the legs are one (17 × 7, across the middle wall), its one door on the east leg.
- Own rooms: tier 1 the crossbar's north room nearest the stairs on the west; tiers 2 and 3 the first two behind it on the west; tier 4 the joined far-north room. The other bedrooms are locked, for later.
- Furnishing (PR 5): placed after the shape, so the golden fingerprints (cells, rooms, doors) don't move. Downstairs: wine racks, spirit racks on tables, Pin, Firkin and Kilderkin racks either side of the back fireplace; the counter wall to wall with its flap, stools before it; a fireplace with two armchairs either side of the entrance; each private room a 2 × 2 table with four chairs, each booth two settles across a 3 × 2 table; up to 30 groups of tables (1 × 1 to 2 × 2 with chairs, benches, games tables, standing barrels) rolled from a fixed seed into `TABLES` with a cell between groups and a ring 2 wide round the stairs; the kitchen's Ranges, Hearths and Stoves (stone) and worktables, its back-left corner empty; the treasury's chests; the brewing room's Alchemy bench and empty casks' cradles and racks; the ageing room's Tun stands, Butt, Puncheon and Hogshead cradles, Firkin racks and wine racks. Upstairs: every bedroom by tier, runners down the corridors. Decor: a sconce or a hanging every third cell along most walls (none behind racks, benches, fireplaces, wardrobes or the bar), chandeliers, rugs. Tests: nothing overlaps, decor fits, the kept places clear, paths from the arrival to the stairs, every door, behind the bar and beside every piece.
- The foyer's "1 × 3 chest" is a chest and a small chest end to end (chests are 1 or 2 long). Flagstones by the bar and kitchen, and an inlay, are not done: floors are one picture a level (the inn's are planks).
