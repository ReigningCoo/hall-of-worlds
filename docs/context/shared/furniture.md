# shared: furniture

Tables, chairs and benches in sites' rooms, of wood or stone, and where they go. Pieces are kept on `dungeon::Level` (as doors are) and sent with its cells; they never block, but moves over them cost more, so routes go round. Material and style are for models only and are never named to players. Drawing is client `dungeon_map` (tables, chairs and benches as models).

## Files

- `crates/shared/src/furniture.rs`: the module, re-exports. Tests in `furniture/tests.rs` (never cuts anything off across kinds, seeds and ruins; only on room floor away from doors, stairs and the way in; seats (chairs, benches, crafting benches) face a table or have a wall behind; seats match their room's table; none in unfurnished kinds; the same every time; golden test of a stone cottage's ground floor).
- `furniture/piece.rs`: `Piece`, `Furniture`, `TABLE_COST_PER_MILLE`, `SEAT_COST_PER_MILLE`.
- `furniture/material.rs`: `Material`, `Wood`, `Stone` with their weights.
- `furniture/style.rs`: `TableStyle`, `ChairStyle`, `BenchStyle` (each with `ALL`), and which seat styles go with each table style.
- `furniture/candles.rs`: `Candles` (tavern furniture plan PR 5).
- `furniture/booth.rs`: `booth` (PR 5).
- `furniture/clutter.rs`: `Barrel`, `Crates`, `Sacks` (PR 6).
- `furniture/bed.rs`: `Bed` (PR 7).
- `furniture/seated.rs`: `games_table`, `barrel_table` (PR 6; tests in `seated/tests.rs`).
- `furniture/places.rs`: furniture with places (tavern furniture plan PR 1): `Holder`, `Held`.
- `furniture/free.rs`: `takes_furniture(cell)` (floor or open ground, not a doorway, gate, water or bridge), `free_for_bench(level, cell)` (on the level, takes furniture, no other piece): what the server checks Place Furniture's and Create Crafting Bench's pieces by (stairs left to each side), and the client's bench ghost.
- `furniture/shape.rs`: `footprint`, `Furniture::is_stone`, `Piece::is_well_made` (tests in `shape/tests.rs`).
- `furniture/place.rs`: `furnish`, the entry point. In `place/`: `setting.rs` (`Setting::for_kind`), `palette.rs` (a site's woods and stone, a room's material), `rooms.rs` (rooms' floor cells, the cells that may be furnished; `pub(crate)` for `shared::cellar`), `reach.rs` (the never-cut-off check), `placer.rs` (`Placer`: what a level's placing keeps track of, a room's matching set, ruin's losses), `tables.rs`, `seats.rs`, `loose.rs` (seats against walls).

## Public API

- `Piece { at, size, facing, material, furniture }` (serde): its north-west cell, its cells east–west and north–south, which way it faces (`door::Side`: a seat the way its sitter looks; a table east or south, the way its length runs), what it is made of and what it is. `cells()`, `covers(cell)`, `moved(offset)`.
- `Furniture::{Table { style }, Chair { style, arms }, Bench { style }, CraftingBench { craft }, CaskRack { rack }, BottleRack { rack }, BarCounter { counter }, Fireplace { fireplace }, Candles { candles }, GamesTable { game }, Barrel { barrel }, Crates { crates }, Sacks { sacks }, Bed { bed }, Chest { chest }, Wardrobe { wardrobe }}`: `is_table()` (a `Table` only: not a crafting bench, games table or barrel), `cost_per_mille()` (a crafting bench, cask or bottle rack, bar counter, fireplace, games table, barrel, crates, bed, chest or wardrobe as a table (a bed's a first guess); sacks as a seat; a bar flap as open floor), `stands_on_furniture()` (a spirit rack or candles: kept on top, `dungeon::Level::furniture_on_top`), `holds_furniture()` (what one may stand on: a table, games table, standing barrel, or a bar counter but not its flap). A bottle rack (`shared::bottle_rack::BottleRack`: a wine rack against a wall, or a spirit rack on a table) is 1 deep and its length, of wood, facing away from its wall (a spirit rack the way its lowest shelf faces). A cask rack (`shared::cask_rack::CaskRack`) is 1 deep and its length, of wood, facing away from the wall as a bench; placed under castles' and towers' cellar casks by `shared::furnishings`.
- Furniture with places (owner's design, 2026-11-22: pieces holding things in fixed places, each taking only the right item, like a bench's tools): `Holder::{Bench(Craft), Casks(CaskRack), Bottles(BottleRack), Bar(BarCounter), Fire(Fireplace), Store(chest::Storage)}` (`of(furniture)`, a bar flap none; `name()`: its panel's title and hover name; `is_drink_storage()`: all but benches, fireplaces, chests and wardrobes, what a bar pours from; `per_place()`: how many of a carried stack the server puts in one place at once, a log basket's `fireplace::KINDLING_PER_PLACE`, a chest's or wardrobe's the whole stack (`u32::MAX`), else 1); `Held::{Bench(BenchTools), Casks(RackCasks), Bottles(RackBottles), Bar(BarVessels), Fire(fireplace::Fireside), Store(chest::Stored)}` (serde: what lies on one, the server's to keep; `new(holder)`, `holder()`, `bench()`, `is_lit(now)` (a hearth's or fireplace's fire, what `SeenBench::lit` tells), `items()`, `put(item) -> Result<Option<Item>, NotTaken>` (a bench's swap comes back), `take(id)`, `replace(item)`: put back in its place, changed by pouring at a bar; `hide_secrets()`: a chest's or wardrobe's items as a client may see them, for the server to send). One pair of messages (`protocol::BenchAsk::{Put, Take}`), one panel (client `crafting_bench`), one picking box (client `dungeon_map`) for every kind; later storage pieces (wine racks, bars, chests) add a variant to each. A fireplace (`shared::fireplace`) is 1 deep and its length, of stone, facing away from the wall (its chimney breast) as a bench. A crafting bench (`shared::crafting_bench`) is 1 deep and its craft's length, of wood, facing away from the wall behind it as a seat does; placed by `crafting_bench::place_benches`, not `furnish`.
- Candles (`Candles::{Candlestick, Pair, Candelabrum}`, `ALL`, `candles()`): 1 × 1, of wood, standing on a table or bar counter as a spirit rack does, always lit, a fixed light (`decor::CANDLE_LIGHT_CELLS`); a pair side by side across the way it faces.
- Games tables, barrels, crates and sacks (tavern furniture plan PR 6), all 1 × 1 and of wood: `GamesTable { game }` a table whose top is a board for a `shared::board_game::BoardGame` (chess or Hnefatafl), facing the way the light player looks across it, its men drawn where they start (the board is the game's to keep later); `Barrel { barrel }` (`Barrel::{Bare, Topped}`) a barrel standing as a table, stools round it; `Crates { crates }` (`Crates::{One, Two, Three, Four}`, `count()`) and `Sacks { sacks }` (`Sacks::{One, Two, Heap}`, `count()`) floor clutter, their facing only turning the model. Crates, sacks, barrels and games tables are placed only by code (the Tavern Dev Area) and Place Furniture.
- Beds, chests and wardrobes (tavern furniture plan PR 7), wooden, placed in castles' and towers' bedrooms by `shared::furnishings` (PR 8): `Bed::{Single, Double}` (`ALL`, `LENGTH` 2, `width()` 1 or 2), facing from its head (against a wall) to its foot, its length running the way it faces; its model smaller than its cells (owner's); no sleeping yet. A chest or wardrobe (`shared::chest`: `Chest::{Small, Large}`, `Wardrobe::{Narrow, Wide}`, 1 or 2 long) is 1 deep and its length, facing away from the wall as a bench; a grid inside (`chest::Stored`), shown shut.
- `games_table(at, game, facing, chair_style, wood)` (the table and a chair each side, behind the board as each player sits) and `barrel_table(at, barrel, sides, wood)` (the barrel and a stool on each side given), facing them: for code placing them (Place Furniture's showcase, The Way Inn).
- `booth(at, length, depth, east_west, wood)`: a booth as three pieces from its north-west cell: a settle `length` long, a joined table `length` by `depth` (1 or 2), a settle facing back; east–west settles north and south of the table, else west and east. For code placing booths (Place Furniture, a tavern's layout).
- `TABLE_COST_PER_MILLE` (3000) and `SEAT_COST_PER_MILLE` (1500): how much longer a move over one takes (`dungeon::Level::furniture_cost_per_mille`, used by `movement::LevelWay` and `pathfinding::DistanceMap`).
- `footprint(furniture, (long, short), facing)`: the cells east–west and north–south of a piece (a table's length the way it faces, a seat's across it). `Piece::is_well_made()`: a shape furnishing could make (sizes per kind, a table facing east or south and east when square, a pedestal where it `fits`, a crafting bench its craft's length and 1 deep across the way it faces, a cask rack its length (`CaskRack::is_well_made`) and 1 deep, of wood, a fireplace its length and 1 deep, of stone, a games table, barrel, crates or sacks 1 × 1 of wood, stone styles (`Furniture::is_stone`) of stone, arms only on wooden chairs): what the server checks Place Furniture's pieces by; a test checks furnishing only makes such pieces.
- `Material::{Wood(Wood), Stone(Stone)}` (`Material::ALL`: the woods, then the stones); `Wood` (oak, walnut, ash, pine, elm, beech, cherry, rare ebony) and `Stone` (granite, sandstone, limestone, marble, slate), each with `WEIGHTS`.
- `TableStyle` (trestle, joined, plank legs, pedestal (1 × 1 and 1 × 2 only, `fits`), stone slab, stone block; `WOOD`, `STONE`, `chairs()`, `benches()`), `ChairStyle` (stool, back-stool, ladder-back, panel-back, X-frame, stone block, bar stool: tall, for along a bar counter, never with arms nor among a table's chairs), `BenchStyle` (splayed-leg plank, board-ended, long stool with rail, stone slab, high-backed settle: never among a table's benches, so furnishing never makes one; for booths).
- `furnish(site_level, kind, seed, index, ruin)`: called by `site::Site::level` last (not for a Dev Area, whose building brings its own).
- `Setting { cells_per_table, cells_per_loose, stone_percent }`, `Setting::for_kind(kind)`: wooden buildings all wood; stone and adobe buildings 15% stone; pyramids 80%; crypts, graves and mausoleums sparse (a table per 120 cells) and 85% stone; `None` for natural caves, boulders, dug ruins, barrels, tents, ice shelters, towns and oases.

## Notes

- Placing (owner's design, 2026-11-15): everything from hashes of `seed.derive("furniture")`, the level and the room, never from a generator's sequence, and after the generator and any ruin, so no generator version changed and sites' golden tests didn't move. Only on `Cell::Floor` with a room; never on a cell beside a door (inside or out), within a cell of stairs, or where anyone arrives.
- About one table per `cells_per_table` floor cells of a room; the first table's size by the room's (2 × 3 from 90 cells, down to 1 × 2 or 1 × 1 in small rooms), later ones by the room shared out; nearest the room's middle, with both long sides free and nothing within two cells. Seats always along both long sides (a bench the side's length in rooms that have benches, else a chair a cell), and at the ends 40% of the time. Chairs with arms: wooden only, 60% at a table's end, 15% elsewhere. Loose seats against a plain wall, back to it, about one per `cells_per_loose`; benches 1 to 3 along the wall. A room's pieces share one material and style set; a site uses one or two woods and one stone.
- Never cutting anything off: while placing, every piece counts as wall, and a piece is kept only if a 4-way fill from each way in (entrance and stairs, every door open, no jumps) still reaches all it did without that piece.
- Ruins: each piece is kept at 100 − 15 × ruin %, decided while placing, so a lost table never leaves its seats closing off its empty cells.
