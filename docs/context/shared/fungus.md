# shared: fungus

Mushrooms, fungi and lichens (owner, 2026-11-19): what each is, the table of them (built in), fungi as counted items (one, a handful, or a large piece cut into servings when eaten), and what eating one raw does. Modelled on `shared::vegetable`, with its own kind and table (`decisions.md`, 2026-11-19). The plan, in 9 PRs, and what each builds: `docs/context/fungi.md` (PR 1 built: the table, items, eating raw; PR 2: pictures and wiki; PR 3: where they grow and picking; PR 4: models on the maps; PR 6: poisons). Research and the owner's decisions: `docs/context/research-fungi.md`.

## Public API

- `FungusDef { key, name, latin, invented, group, grows (where in RON), niches, biomes, temperature, abundance, unit, servings, grams, kcal, raw, food, herb, poison, growth, regrow, glow, lookalikes, look }`: `key` its id for good; `group` a `Group` (`Mushroom`, `Bracket`, `Jelly`, `Truffle`, `Sclerotium`, `Cup`, `Crust`, `Lichen`); `grows` a `Where` (`Deep` anywhere underground, `Dim` underground near daylight, `Dug` buried, `Surface` above ground only); `niches` what it grows on or by (`Niche`: wood, dung, soil, remains, water, lava, trees, roots, rock, insects, termites); `biomes` needed above ground, none for invented cave kinds; `temperature` herbs' `herb::Temperature` (a cave is at its land's); `abundance` vegetables' `Abundance` (`Common` to `VeryRare`); `unit` `One`, `Handful` (100 g) or `Piece` (cut into `servings`, about 200 g each); `kcal` and `grams` for one item (`kcal` 0 for what isn't food); `raw` vegetables' `Raw`; `food` 0 to 2 vegetables' `Effect`s (raw, cooked or both); `herb` four `herb::Effect`s or none (Alchemy, PR 8); `poison` an optional `Poison`; `growth` and `regrow` herbs'; `glow` its light's colour (PR 5); `lookalikes` keys of fungi it is mistaken for (PR 7), checked to be in the table by `Fungi::read`; `look` a `FungusLook`.
- `Poison { severity, onset, false_recovery, effects, raw_only, trigger, confuses }`: `Severity` `Mild` to `Deadly`; `Onset` `Soon`, `Delayed`, `Late` (game time, `onset_ticks`); 1 to 4 `herb::Effect`s; `Trigger` `Eaten` (the default), `Old`, `Repeated`, `WithAlcohol`, `Touched`, `Breathed` (`Old` and `Repeated` not brought on yet, `Touched` and `Breathed` PR 9); `when_eaten` (only `Eaten` poisons on eating); `confuses` (fly agaric, panther cap, Dreamcap: stumbles, `Sheet::stumble_one_in`).
- `FungusLook { form, colours, smallest, largest, cap, under, stem, ring, volva, warts }`: `Form` (cap and stem, funnel, bracket, ball, coral, cup, jelly, honeycomb, club, truffle, crust, lichen tufts, beard, plates), 1 to `MOST_COLOURS` (3) colours (its body, underneath, its stem), sizes across in mm; the rest optional in RON, defaults first: `Cap` (`Convex`, `Flat`, `Bell`, `Conical`, `Depressed`; a morel's `Conical` makes it pointed), `Under` (`Gills`, `Pores`, `Spines`, `Ridges`, `Smooth`: under a cap or bracket, a ball's spines, a coral's `Pores` making it a rosette of fans, a funnel's `Smooth` a deep trumpet), `Stem` (`Slender`, `Tall`, `Stout`), `ring`, `volva`, `warts`; for pictures and models (PRs 2 and 4).
- `Fungi`: `built_in()` (`&'static`), `read(ron_text)` (each checked, lookalikes in the same text), `new(defs)`, `get(key)`, `by_tag(tag)`, `iter`, `len`.
- `FungusTag(u64)`, `FungusTag::of(key)`: a key hashed, so `bonus::Source` can name a fungus and stay `Copy`.
- `FungusForm { fungus, serving, look, grams }` (`ItemForm::Fungus`): `new(def, seed)`, `serving(def, seed)`, `is_whole(def)` (a piece of several servings), `name()` ("Giant puffball", "Giant puffball Slice", "Fungus" for a key the table lacks), `shape()` (as fruit's). `new_item(def, count, id, made_at)`; `Item::fungus()`.
- `eating(def, now, eater) -> Vec<Effect>`: one serving eaten raw: healing (`Source::EatenFungus(tag)`, as vegetables': `RAW_SHARE` of a serving's kcal ÷ 4 s, only for `Commonly` or `Sometimes`, ended by moving or being hurt), raw and both food effects (`Source::FungusEffects(tag)`, for `effect_ticks`), a poison whose trigger is `Eaten` (`poisoning`), or for a `WithAlcohol` one (the common ink cap) a window (`Source::FungusWindow(tag)`: `WINDOW_BONUS` Toughness and Will +1 for `WINDOW_TICKS`, 3 game days); nothing ends those early. Herb effects are not eaten. Some effects start after `now`: lay them on with `Sheet::lay_on`.
- `poisoning(def, poison, now, eater)`: `Source::FungusPoison(tag)` from `now + onset_ticks` (soon 0, delayed 10, late 60 game minutes) for `poison_ticks(severity)` (30, 60, 90 or 120 s). Deadly: its Health effect becomes `bonus::Stat::DeadlyDrain` (`deadly_drain(Eater { most_health, easy }, ticks)`: `DEADLY_TIMES` (3) × the most over its time, halved on Easy), draining to 0. A deadly one with `false_recovery` (amatoxins) is two phases: `SICK_TICKS` (30 s) of its plain effects, `EASED_TICKS` (60 game minutes) eased, then the worst. `with_alcohol(effects, now, eater)`: the poisons alcohol brings on now, by the windows lasting (`Sheet::drink_alcohol` lays them on).
- `recognised(knowledge, def, healing, roll)`: a poison eaten is known before it starts if `FungusKnowledge::knows_poison` (the one place PR 7 widens) or `roll` (0 to 99) is under the Healing skill's percent, never over 95. `FungusKnowledge { poisons }` (`Sheet::fungi`, saved): poisons learned by suffering one (`Sheet::start_pending`).
- `cut(whole, new_id) -> Option<(eaten, Option<rest>)>`: as `vegetable::cut`.
- `FungusPatch { fungus, phase, picked }` (a cell's fungus; kept on `dungeon::Level`, sent in `protocol::LevelChunk::fungi`): `stage(def, now)` and `can_pick(def, now)` grow as herbs' (`herb::grown_stage`), picked full grown only, a stage after the last. `harvest(def, foraging, rng)`: as a herb patch without Herbs (`herb::harvest_range`, 1 to 3 untrained), a `Unit::Piece` always 1.
- `Spot { niches, dim, earth, underground, depth, deepest }`: what a cell offers. `FungusDef::suits_spot(spot, celsius, biome)`: `Deep` anywhere underground, `Dim` only where dim, `Dug` in cave earth underground or in its biomes above ground, `Surface` above ground in its biomes; its temperature range always; a niche the spot offers; `DEEPEST_ONLY` (Dreamcap) only on a cave's deepest level. Biomes don't count underground (decision 3). `weight(abundance, depth)`: 9, 3 + depth, 1 + depth, depth (very rare never on the top level). `suited(fungi, spot, habitat)`, `choose(fungi, depth, rng)` (weighted, `None` if nothing can be).
- `grow_underground(site_level, fungi, Underground, habitat, seed)`: a patch on about one `Cell::Floor` in `PATCH_ONE_IN` (60), never on stairs, a fungus suiting it. `Underground::Cave { depth, levels, crypt }`: soil and dung anywhere, rock beside a wall, water within `WATER_REACH` (2) of a pool, lava beside lava, remains in crypts or on the deepest level, earth (dug kinds); dim on the top level within `DIM_REACH` (4) of outside ground or stairs out, offering roots and wood too. `Underground::Basement`: wood and soil, `Deep` kinds only.
- `felled_patch(fungi, habitat, seed, cell)`: for `FELLED_PERCENT` (40) of wild trees' cells, a wood-feeder of the land's biome and warmth, shown only while the tree is felled and cleared (`dungeon::Level::fungus_at`).
- `dev_fungus(key, seed)`: Give Item's fungus, by key or rolled from the seed, the same on both sides; also `item_form::give_form`'s.

## Notes

- Built in only, no mods yet: both sides read the same table, so nothing is sent. Saves and messages name a fungus by key; effects by `FungusTag`.
- No quality, never magical, blessed or cursed. A stack keys on fungus and serving; each keeps its own look seed.
- Poisons (PR 6, decisions 8, 9, 11): a poison starting later waits in `Sheet::later` and is hidden from the client unless recognised on eating (the server's `eat_fungus.rs`); once it starts it is a normal effect on the bar, "Poisoned: Death cap", teaching the poison (so a false recovery's worst is known from then). Never wrong information: hidden is shown as nothing. Deadly ones kill at 0 Health (server `die_of_poison`), dying curing poisons. Harm from handling and breathing comes in PR 9. Item popups leave poisons and the ink cap's window out until knowing fungi comes (PR 7), so they never say a fungus is safe or not.
- Where they grow (PR 3): placing is the server's (`sites/plants/fungi.rs` on site levels and their cutouts, `wilderness::fungus_patches` on blocks), from hashes, not part of any generator's version. Patches never block. Dug kinds are picked as others for now (no digging tool). Picking is the server's (`connections/pick_fungus.rs`); the client draws each patch as a model by its form (PR 4: `plants::FungusModels`, `dungeon_map/fungi.rs`).
