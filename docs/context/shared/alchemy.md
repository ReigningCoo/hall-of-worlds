# shared: alchemy

The Alchemy rules (Alchemy plan PR 2, `docs/context/alchemy.md`): a process of apparatus steps, its chance, what it makes (doses in hundredths at full amount between steps, diluted only at the end), its least quality, the server's rolls, what the preview shows and what a success teaches. Pure functions on whole numbers, so the client's preview matches the server. And what the server runs (PR 3): the order as asked for and checked against what is carried, the Paste item, each run's time and practice, the sub-skills learned on carrying an apparatus. Not the panel (client `alchemy`).

## Public API

- `plan(process, skills) -> Result<Planned { product, chance, quality, practised, contributions, salt }, Refused>`: every step in order. `Product::Nothing`, `Mixture { form, contents }` (a Mortar last: Paste dry, Ointment with an oil; a Crucible's Powder, a Filter keeping the form), `Potion { contents, bottles }` (liquid in it: diluted over its 100 ml units, a bottle each, a potion if a dose reaches `brewing::POTION_AT`, keeping `KEPT_FROM` and up) or `Alcohol { bottles }` (pure alcohol: no doses, distilled and reduced, a bottle per unit left while the alcohol holds `ALCOHOL_A_BOTTLE` ml each); `Product::has_quality` (mixtures and potions only: pure alcohol's quality is `None`). `Planned::salt`: Salt an Alembic left, one per Salt Water unit distilled.
- Steps: `Mortar { herbs, solvent }` (first only, and not after a stored source; `brewing::ground_doses`, so brewing's values; `Solvent::Dry` a Paste, `Oil` an Ointment, `Alcohol` a Tincture: no row boost, every dose × `TINCTURE_PERCENT` (105) in hundredths, already 1 unit (100 ml) of liquid holding 100 ml of alcohol, so a potion when last); `Retort { liquids }` (1 to `RETORT_UNITS` 100 ml units, each applied in order at full amount: waters as `brewing::watered`, oils their row's boost, drinks and pure alcohol nothing to the doses but their alcohol (strength × 100 ml), Salt Water its salt; juice, cordial, vinegar and potions refused `Liquid`); `Alembic { fed }` (needs units before it, else `NothingToDistil`; `fed` a drink or pure alcohol, else `NotAlcohol`, or none: units ÷ `reduced_by(strength)` (1 + strength ÷ `STRENGTH_A_DIVISION`: under 10% 1, wine 2, pure 11), rounded down, at least 1 while there are doses; doses and alcohol kept; distilled only when reduced; Salt Water's units become salt made); `Crucible` (needs something before it, else `NothingToCalcine`: every dose × `POWDER_PERCENT` (75) in hundredths, form Powder, the liquid, alcohol and Salt Water driven off; a potion calcined is a Powder again); `Filter { powder }` (needs something before it, else `NothingToFilter`, and a Powder's doses, else `NoPowder`: each stat in both adds together, either way, one in only one is taken out; form and volume kept); `Pelican` (needs something before it, else `NothingToCirculate`: changes nothing in the batch, which counts the Pelicans, kept through a Crucible; the product's purity score (`brewing::purity`) × (100% + `CIRCULATION_PERCENT` (20) each), rounded down, before `brewing::quality_of_purity` bands it: `circulated_quality`). `Refused::NotBuilt` is left for inputs whose rules come later. `Stored { form, contents }` as a source: `Form::is_old` (Paste, Ointment; never Powder).
- `chance(alchemy%, steps, extra_kinds)`: hundredths of a percent, (100% + Alchemy%) halved per step over `steps_allowed` (⌈√Alchemy%⌉, at least 1) and per herb kind over `brewing::most_kinds`; `succeeds(chance, roll)`.
- `least_quality(purity, chance, skill_steps, old)`: `circulated_quality` of the product (`brewing::quality` without a Pelican), plus `skill_steps` (steps × Σ∛ of the distinct sub-skills used ÷ (their count × 10), rounded down once) only when the chance is at least `CERTAIN`, less 1 when old; Poor to Legendary. `outcome(planned, chance_roll, luck_roll) -> Outcome::{Failed, Made { product, quality }}`, luck +1 at 1%, +2 at 0.1% (`luck_steps`).
- `Planned::practised`: each step's apparatus, but a Retort holding no mixture (liquids alone) gives no Infusing; an Alembic always gives Distilling.
- `AlchemyOrder { source: Option<Id>, steps: Vec<OrderStep>, count }` (`ClientMessage::Alchemy`): `OrderStep::{Mortar { herbs, solvent: Option<Id> }, Retort { liquids }, Alembic { alcohol: Option<Id> }, Crucible, Filter { powder: Option<Id> }, Pelican}` by carried stack ids, a stack listed twice used twice. `Inventory::check_alchemy(order, herbs, skills) -> Result<Checked { planned, herbs }, OrderRefused>`: `count` 1 to `MOST_REPEATS` (99), each step's apparatus carried (`Apparatus::item`: Mortar the `MortarAndPestle`, Retort the `Retort`, Alembic the `Alembic`, Crucible the `Crucible`, Filter the `Filter`, Pelican the `Pelican`), the source a carried Paste, Ointment or Powder, the Filter's a carried Powder (`OrderRefused::Powder`), herbs from `herb_stacks`, the solvent a carried bottle of oil or pure alcohol (`OrderRefused::Solvent`), liquids and the Alembic's alcohol any carried filled bottle (`plan` refuses what doesn't fit), every stack holding what one run uses (`TooFew`), never worn; then `plan`. Both sides call it; the server again before each run.
- Paste (`ItemKind::Paste`, `ItemForm::Paste(brewing::OintmentForm)`, counted, 40 g) and Powder (`ItemKind::Powder`, `ItemForm::Powder`, counted, 20 g): no way to apply them, a process's stored source (a Powder never old). `new_mixture(form, contents, quality, count, id)`: a Paste, an Ointment or a Powder (a Tincture as an Ointment) at the quality Alchemy settled. `Item::mixture() -> Option<(Form, &Contents)>`.
- `run_seconds(steps, skills)`: each step's own time (Retort 15 s, Alembic and Crucible 20 s, Pelican 30 s, the rest 10 s) less the ∛ of its sub-skill%; `run_ticks`. `STEP_PRACTICE` (8, each practised step's sub-skill, flowing up to Alchemy), `HERB_PRACTICE` (2 Herbalism a herb kind). `learn_sub_skills(carried, skills)`: practises each carried apparatus's sub-skill by 1 when unknown, so it is known; the server calls it with Collect Water's learning when it sends the inventory.
- `preview(planned, known) -> Vec<Seen>`: each product dose `Known` only when every herb that gave to its stat has that effect known. `lesson(planned, known) -> Option<Lesson { herb, effect }>`: the strongest product dose with something unknown, on the unknown herb that gave most towards it.

## Notes

- Dilution: each stat's total in hundredths ÷ (100 × units), rounded once to the nearest whole step, halves away from 0. Whole doses stand for one 100 ml bottle, so an ointment and one water make one bottle at full strength (as Brew did), and more liquid spreads the same total over more bottles.
- Boosts (Moon and Cave Water, oils) are an exact quarter in hundredths, at least a whole step, so a Retort can come out up to half a step stronger than Brew's whole steps did; Plain, Salt and Rain Water match Brew exactly (tested).
- Each 100 ml unit applies its liquid once: two Moon Waters boost twice, then dilute over two units.
- The Alembic and pure alcohol (PR 5, Claude's first guesses for the owner to tune): the fed alcohol divides the volume by 1 + its strength in whole tenths (ale and cider not at all, wine by 2, pure alcohol by 11); what comes over from drinks alone is pure alcohol only when reduced, a bottle per unit left, capped by 10 ml of alcohol a bottle (five 6% ciders fed a wine make 2; three make 1; one nothing). Pure alcohol fed to a potion boils it furthest, to one bottle at full strength.
- Claude's choices: the sub-skills' mean counts each apparatus once; an empty Mixture is `Nothing`; learning picks only among herbs whose effect is still unknown, weighing what each gave towards the dose's way (opposite effects count against).
