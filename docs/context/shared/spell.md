# shared: spell

Designed spells' rules (magic plan PR 2, `docs/context/magic-system.md`): a spell's steps as data, checking a design against the caster's skills, its Mana cost, Void's reversal, the combination table, when it is cast automatically, and its automatic name, and what each where step hits (PR 3). Pure rules both sides use, the spellbook (PR 4) on the sheet, casting's timing, Heal's amount, spells' keys for the action bar and the rule that spells never kill the caster or friends (PR 5), missiles' flight (PR 6), and how wheres look for drawing (PR 7); the casting itself is the server's (`connections/cast.rs`). The Heal ability is gone: the Heal spell took its place.

## Files

- `crates/shared/src/spell.rs`: `mod` lines and re-exports.
- `spell/design.rs`: `Spell` (steps), `Step` (`Where(WhereStep)` or `Effects(EffectStep)`; `merged(parts, filters)`), `WhereStep { place, count, filters, more }` (its first where, and `more: Vec<WherePart>` merged after it, PR 10: `serde(default)`, so older saves load as one where; `parts`, `len`, `last`), `WherePart { place, count }`, `EffectStep { effects: Vec<(Effect, u8)> }`, `MAX_STEPS` (10), `MAX_WHERES` (5 in a step).
- `spell/place.rs`: `Where` (Touch, Missile, Surround, Beam, Point: `ALL` in unlock order, `tier` 1 to 5, `key`, `skill` under `magic/form`).
- `spell/effect.rs`: `Effect` (18, Void among them: `ALL`, `key`, `skill_path`, `skill`).
- `spell/filters.rs`: `Filters { caster, friend, enemy }` (Self is `caster`), `NONE`, `any`.
- `spell/part.rs`: `Part { effect, reversed }` (`key`: a reversed one's own, Fire reversed `cold`), `parts(step)`: Void read and left out.
- `spell/combination.rs`: `Mixture` (Steam), `Combination`, `COMBINATIONS`, `Outcome` (a part or a mixture), `outcomes(step)`.
- `spell/auto_cast.rs`: `Aim` (Helpful, Harmful, Neutral) per `Outcome::aim`, `AutoCast` (WhenLow, AtEnemy, ByHand), `auto_cast(spell)`.
- `spell/check.rs`: `DesignError`, `check(spell, skills)`, `unlocked_wheres(skills)`, `is_unlocked`.
- `spell/cost.rs`: `effect_cost`, `effect_step_cost`, `where_part_cost`, `where_step_cost`, `step_cost`, `spell_cost` (a `vitals::Points`), `EFFECT_BASE`, `LEAST`, `FORM_SAVING`.
- `spell/name.rs`: `auto_name(spell, lang)`, worded by `lang/en/spell.ftl`.
- `spell/hit.rs`: `Side` (Caster, Friend, Enemy, Neutral: `allowed_by(filters)`: Friend and Enemy limit hits to theirs, Self limits nothing, the caster is never a friend; `same_as`), `Creature { body: combat::Body, side }`, `Origin { at, height, heading, creature }` (`from_creature`), `Hit { creature, at, height, heading }` (`origin()`: the next step's), `hit_anyone`.
- `spell/toward.rs`: `Toward` (Creature, Spot, Ahead): what a where is aimed at.
- `spell/line.rs`: crate-private line helpers: `ahead`, `wall_stop` (halving its way to the last clear spot), `entry_along` (where a line first meets a circle).
- `spell/book.rs`: `Spellbook` (`Sheet::spellbook`), `BookSpell { spell, name, favourite }`, `BookRefusal`, `MOST_SPELLS` (100), `LONGEST_NAME` (40), `heal_spell`, `tidy_name`.
- `spell/key.rs`: `SpellKey` (`of(spell)`: FNV-1a over the design's postcard bytes, the same everywhere; a where step of one where written as before merging, so saved bars keep their keys), for the action bar's `BarThing::Spell` and `SpellbookAsk::Cast`.
- `spell/cast.rs`: `CAST_COST` (Heal's old 4200), `CAST_WIND_UP` (half), `STEP_MILLIS` (1000: steps a second apart), `heal_restores(count, skills)` (`magic::heal_amount` each).
- `spell/spare.rs`: `spared_harm(harm, health, side)`: harm to the caster and friends stops at 1 Health (used by every harmful effect since PR 11).
- `spell/harm.rs` (PR 11): `harm_skills`, `does_harm`, `harm_before_resisting(outcome, count, skills)` (Heal's line, 12 to 120 a count, by the effect's own skill: Fire for fire and cold, Healing for harm, Fire and Water averaged for steam, `STEAM_HARM` half again), `spell_power` (`combat::power` by those skills), `resistance(attributes, skills)` (Aura and the Warding skill, `warding_skill`), `resisted(harm, power, resistance)` (as Defence: power ÷ (power + resistance) gets through), `takes_hold(power, resistance, rng)` (the ratio contest, for harm that isn't Health: ice's chill); PR 12: `effect_power(effect, attributes, skills)` (power with one effect's skill, for contests), `will(attributes, skills)` (Will and Warding: what paralysis and illusions are contested by). The server multiplies both resistances by the target's `bonus::Stat::Resistance` (Warding).
- `spell/acts.rs` (PR 11): `Acts` (harms, wets, dries, chills, lights, douses, freezes, clouds; `on_place`; PR 12: `knocks: Option<Knock>` (Away for Air, In for a vacuum), `lasts: Option<Part>`, `ends: Option<Ends>`), PR 13: `identifies` (also `on_place`: secret doors round where it lands), `conceals`, `gives_mana`, `drains_mana`, `glows` (Light on spots, `on_place`); `acts(outcome)`, `acts_on_place(spell)` (its first effect step does something to the place, so a first step hitting nobody lands on its spots instead of fizzling), `frozen_cells` and `cloud_cells` (water and shallows, or open cells, within the count's cells, at most 5), `ICE_SECONDS` (60 a count), `STEAM_SECONDS` (5 a count).
- `spell/condition.rs` (PR 11): timed effects as `bonus::Source::Spell(part)`: `WET` (Water, `WET_SECONDS` 30 a count; fire does `WET_FIRE`, half, and dries) and `CHILLED` (ice, `CHILL_SECONDS` 10 a count, `CHILL_SPEED_PERCENT` 67), `wet`, `chill`, `condition`; `Sheet::under_spell`, `end_spell`, `spell_speed_percent` (in `Sheet::speed`).
- `spell/lasting.rs` (PR 12): `PARALYSED` (Earth), `STONESKIN` (Earth reversed), `POISONED`, `CURSED`, `ILLUSION`, `WARDED` parts; `lasting(part, count, skills, now)` (a `bonus::Effect` with `Source::Spell`: Stoneskin and a Curse `HarmTaken` and `BlowHarm` less by `LESS_PERCENT` a count, up to `MOST_LESS_PERCENT`; an Illusion `Defence` and Warding `Resistance` more by `MORE_PERCENT` a count; both twice at 100% skill; paralysis no bonuses), `poison_per_second(skills)` (Heal's line by Poison over `POISON_SECONDS`), `poison(per_second, count, now)` (a `Drain(Health)`), `seconds_of` (`PARALYSIS_SECONDS` 3, `POISON_SECONDS` 10, `LASTING_SECONDS` 30, a count each).
- `spell/ending.rs` (PR 12): `Ends` (Poison: eaten, a fungus's and a spell's poison; Curse; Illusion; Spells: every `Source::Spell`; PR 13: Levitation, Light, Darkness), `Ends::ends(source)`, `Sheet::end_effects(ends, situation, now)`.
- `spell/knock.rs` (PR 12): `knock_units(count, skills)` (`KNOCK_UNITS` a cell a count, twice at 100% Air, at most `MOST_KNOCK`, 5 cells), `knocked_to(level, size, at, heading, units)` (as far as `clearance::check_segment` allows, halving the way).
- `spell/utility.rs` (PR 13): `HASTED`, `SLOWED`, `BLESSED`, `WEAKENED`, `LIT`, `DARKENED`, `CALMED`, `FRIGHTENED`, `LEVITATING`, `TELEPORTING`, `ANCHORED` parts; `is_utility`, `utility_seconds` (`SHORT_SECONDS` 15 for Calm, Fear, Levitate and the two Teleports, else `LASTING_SECONDS`), `utility_bonuses` (Haste `Stat::Speed` more by `MORE_PERCENT` a count up to `MOST_HASTE_PERCENT`, Slow less by `LESS_PERCENT`, Blessing and Weakness the same on every attribute, Light `LIGHT_CELLS` of `Stat::Light` (twice at 100%, at most `MOST_LIGHT_CELLS`)); `lasting` and `seconds_of` hand these parts on to it.
- `spell/under.rs` (PR 13): `Sheet::has_spell`, `light_reach(carrying)` (a lantern's or a Light's, none under Darkness), `levitating`, `may_teleport` (a Teleport and not held).
- `spell/teleport.rs` (PR 13): `TELEPORT_RANGE` (10 cells), `may_teleport_to(level, size, from, to)` (in range, a clear line, room to stand there).
- `spell/mana.rs` (PR 13): `mana_given(cost, targets, skills)` (`MANA_GIVEN_PERCENT` of the cost at no skill, all at 100%, shared out), `mana_drained(count, skills)` (Heal's line by Mana a count).
- `spell/aim.rs`: crate-private `aim_first`: what a first where with filters is aimed at (owner, 2026-11-23): Self the caster, whatever is targeted; Friend or Enemy the target if theirs, else the nearest of theirs in the where's reach with a clear line, or nothing (a fizzle); Surround stays round its centre.
- `spell/flight.rs` (PR 6): `Path` (a missile's way: from, to, the ground's height, heading, length, count and filters; serde, sent in `MissileNews`), `launch(level, step, origin, toward, creatures)` (the first where's aim by `aim_first`, then the way to the spot aimed at or ahead, stopped by walls; `None` a fizzle), `launch_part(level, step, index, origin, creatures)` (a merged missile, going on ahead, never aimed), `Path::speed`, `millis`, `along_after(millis)`, `at(along)`, `meets((start, end), creatures, except)` (the first allowed creature whose circle that stretch enters), `hit`, `end`: the server flies missiles a stretch a tick by these, so those stepping into or out of the way are hit or missed; `missile` is the same flight all at once.
- `spell/look.rs` (PR 7): `Look` (up to `LOOK_PARTS`, 4, distinct parts, first written first): `from_step(spell, index)` every effect after a where, `of_effects(step)` one step's; sent for clients to colour wheres by.
- `spell/shown.rs` (PR 7): `Shown` (a where as drawn: where it started after its filters' aim, the way it faced, its reach for Touch and Surround, Beam's ends (`beam.rs`'s crate-private `beam_ends`), the spots hit, its look), `shown(level, (step, look), origin, toward, creatures, hits)`, `None` on a filtered fizzle, `shown_part` for a merged where; sent in `protocol::SpellNews`, one a where, so the client draws merged wheres as any.
- `spell/touch.rs`, `missile.rs`, `surround.rs`, `beam.rs`, `point.rs`: one where each, with its first-guess constants; `spell/hits.rs`: `where_hits(level, step, origin, toward, creatures)`, by the step's first where and filters; `part_hits(level, step, index, origin, creatures)` (a merged where, from a hit of the one before, going on ahead: Friend and Enemy still limit it, nothing aims it, Self dropped); `step_hits` (the whole step at once, every where's hits together).

## Public API

- The design types above (serde, for messages and saves later), `Spell::new`, `first_where`, `effect_steps`; `Step::at`, `filtered`, `effects`.
- `check`: refuses, first found, `Empty`, `TooManySteps`, `NoEffect`, `WhereAtEnd`, `EmptyStep`, `TooManyWheres`, `ZeroCount`, `FilterNotFirst`, `Locked(where)` (any where in a step). `unlocked_wheres`: ⌊∛(Magic% × 6)⌋, 0 to 5, Magic being the `magic` node's own value; no skills none.
- Costs in thousandths: an effect 5000 less ∛(its skill %) in thousandths, at least 1000; an effect step its effects × counts plus 1000 per unit after the first (Void costs as any); a where (tier × count + count − 1) × 1000, less up to `FORM_SAVING` (half) by its own Form leaf, at least 1000, and a where step its wheres multiplied (PR 10: Missile + Surround 6 at no skill); filters free; the spell its steps multiplied (capped at `u32::MAX`).
- `parts`: Void flips reversal for everything after it in its step (an odd count flips, even puts back); the same part written twice is added up, in the order first written.
- `outcomes`: each `COMBINATIONS` row takes as many of its pair as both have, mixtures first, then what is left. Only Fire + Water = Steam so far.
- `auto_cast`: every outcome helpful: `WhenLow` (first step's target at or below a quarter, `magic::needs_healing`); every one harmful: `AtEnemy`; anything else `ByHand`.
- Where hits (all integers, creatures by their place in the slice the caller passes, the origin's own never hit; every hit needs a clear line from the origin, `dungeon::spots_are_clear`; nearest first):
  - `touch`: within `combat::reach_between` (no weapon) in 3D and an arc of `touch_half_arc(count)` each side (45°, 22.5° wider a count, all round from ×7); nobody: the spot an arm's reach ahead.
  - `missile`: to the spot aimed at (at most `MISSILE_RANGE`, 8 cells; ahead that far with none), stopped by the first wall and the first creature its filters allow whose circle it meets (passing through the rest): a `Flight { hit, length }`; `missile_speed` (16 cells a second, half again a count), `flight_millis`. In play the server flies it a stretch at a time (`flight.rs`).
  - `surround`: anyone whose circle reaches within `surround_reach` (the centre's radius + a cell a count); nobody: `SURROUND_SPOTS` (8) spots on the ring facing out (Surround → Missile's eight missiles).
  - `beam`: `beam_headings` (the middle, then one either side `BEAM_SPREAD`, 22.5°, apart a count after the first), each as long as the way to the spot aimed at (at most `BEAM_RANGE`, 8 cells), stopped by walls; everyone allowed it meets, once; nobody: each beam's end.
  - `point`: the creature aimed at if allowed and in `POINT_RANGE` (8 cells, 3D), else the nearest allowed; then the nearest to it on its side (`same_as`) up to the count. Self adds the caster ("you and") and picks the count from friends: Self + Point ×2 is you and two friends, Friend + Point ×2 two friends. Unfiltered at the caster, the caster. Nobody: no hit at all.
  - A hit faces away from its origin (the origin's heading when on the same spot); a step that met nobody gives spots instead (`hit_anyone` false), which is a first step's fizzle.
- `Spellbook` (like `recipe::RecipeBook`): spells known by their design, never twice. `Default` (a new character's, and an older save's, as `Sheet::spellbook` is `serde(default)`) holds Heal (`heal_spell`, one Heal on the caster) then Healing Missile (`healing_missile`, Missile → Heal; usable once Missile is unlocked); `empty`. `store(spell, name, skills)` (checked by `check`, then the name, then `Known`, then `Full` at 100: never dropping one; to the front), `rename(spell, name)`, `set_favourite(spell, on)`, `remove(spell) -> BookSpell` (for the design panel to hold), `knows`, `get`, `by_key(SpellKey)`, `iter` (favourites first, then the rest, each latest stored first), `len`, `is_empty`. Names: `tidy_name` trims, empty is `None` (the automatic name, worded by the client with `auto_name`), more than 40 characters or control characters `BadName`. The server's `connections/spellbook.rs` answers `SpellbookAsk`.
- Merged wheres (PR 10, owner): within a step each where leads on from what the one before hit, and the effects after the step land on everything any of them hit (Missile + Surround: the target and the ring round it; Surround + Missile: those round you and what the eight missiles hit); separate steps behave as before. Only the first where of the first step uses the caster's target; filters stay in the first step, Friend and Enemy limiting every where in it.
- `auto_name`: the first effect step's first outcome, shaped by the where just before it (a merged step's last): no where `Heal`, `Healing Missile`, `Ball of Fire`, `Chilling Touch`, `Scalding Beam`; nothing `Spell`.

## Notes

- Identify, Teleport, Light, Haste, Calm, Blessing, Levitate and Mana (PR 13): see the plan's "Claude's choices in PR 13". Light also ends Darkness and Darkness a Light; Levitate reversed only ends Levitate. Spell speed (`Sheet::spell_speed_percent`) is the chill times every spell's `Stat::Speed`.

- Air, Earth, Poison, Curse, Illusion and Warding (PR 12): Air knocks away from where its where came from, reversed pulls in, as far as the target's resistance shares out; Earth paralyses (the contest against Will) and reversed gives Stoneskin; Poison drains over time (resisted as a share), reversed cures; a Curse (the contest against Aura) weakens a creature's blows, reversed lifts it and the curses on what is worn; an Illusion raises Defence, reversed ends Illusions; Warding raises resistance to magic, reversed dispels every spell's effect. Blows read Illusion, Curse and Stoneskin through `combat::Guard`. Removing is never resisted.

- Void, Fire and Water (PR 11, owner's steam: scald and blind): fire harms, dries the wet (half the harm) and lights a fireplace with kindling in its basket; cold (Fire reversed) harms; Water wets players and puts out campfires and fireplaces' and hearths' fires (never one always lit); ice (Water reversed) chills (a contest) and freezes water round where it lands to walk on; steam harms half again, puts fires out and leaves a cloud blocking sight; harm (Heal reversed) harms. Harm is resisted as a share, like Defence, rather than all or nothing (the contest's chance as its expected value); a chill, which can't be shared, is the contest itself. Lanterns have no unlit state yet, so Fire and Water leave them alone. Mobs keep no effects, so they can't be wet. Practice: an outcome's skill (Void's too when reversed; steam Fire's and Water's) when it changed anything.
- Integers only; skills are read as values in hundredths of a percent, so costs fall smoothly between the plan's whole-percent rows.
- A spell ending on a where is refused (`WhereAtEnd`): it would hit things and do nothing. Not in the plan's wording; easy to drop.
- Aims are first guesses (Calm counts as harmful, since it is resisted and aimed at enemies; neutral ones such as Water and Light keep a spell to casting by hand).
- Words in `spell.ftl` are first guesses, for the owner to change; a test checks every part and mixture has a noun and `.adjective`.
- Where hits' ranges, arcs, spreads and speeds are first guesses, to balance by playing. Self on Point meaning "you and" friends is read from the plan's two examples (Self + Point ×2, Friend + Point ×2). Filters choose the first where's aim (owner, 2026-11-23, replacing PR 3's reading that Self limited hits to the caster): Self + Surround is a ring round you whoever is targeted; Friend or Enemy with nobody of theirs in reach fizzles.
- Hits work in 2D along lines and in 3D (`combat::distance`) for reach and rings; spots with nobody on them keep the origin's height, for the caller to put on the ground.
