# shared: dungeon

Dungeons and buildings as rules: a grid of cells (floor, wall, doors, windows, outside ground, water, lava, fields, fences) with the room each belongs to, the thin shape walls take, and what can be seen (or lit) from a cell. No drawing (that is client `dungeon_map`); levels are generated by `shared::site`.

## Files

- `crates/shared/src/dungeon.rs`: `Cell`, `WallShape`, `RoomId`, `Level`, `Sight` and the private line checks (`clear_line`). Golden tests (test room layout, sight from its centre), wall shapes, sight past columns and through openings, mutual sight. In `dungeon/`: `cell.rs`, `wall_shape.rs`, `level.rs` (`RoomId`, `Level`), `sight.rs`, `line.rs` (`line_is_clear`, `spots_are_clear`), `darkness.rs` (`CARRIED_LIGHT`, 0: no one gives light without a lantern (owner), 10 with a lit lantern (`lantern::LANTERN_LIGHT`, `Inventory::light_reach`); `darkvision_reach(step, quality)`: a Darkvision dose's cells, 2 a step + 1 a quality over Poor, at most `MOST_DARKVISION` 24, none for 0 or less; sight itself has no darkness rule yet, so the server needs nothing more), `bars.rs` (`WallBar`), `doors.rs` (`Level`'s doors), `furniture.rs` (`Level`'s furniture), `decor.rs` (`Level`'s decor, `shared::decor`: `decor()`, `decor_at`, `place_decor`, `remove_decor`), `herbs.rs` (`Level`'s herb patches), `fungi.rs` (`Level`'s fungus patches), `felled.rs` (`Level`'s felled trees), `campfires.rs` (`Level`'s campfires), `clouds.rs` (`Level`'s clouds of steam, magic plan PR 11), `tree_kinds.rs` (`Level`'s wild trees' kinds).

## Public API

- `line_is_clear(level, from, to)`: the rule sight goes by, between the middles of two cells (`dungeon_map` spills light with it).
- `spots_are_clear(level, from, to)`: the same rule between two `spot::Spot`s (240 units a cell), for blows and spells between creatures anywhere (`combat::within`).
- `Cell` (`Floor`, `Wall`, `Door`, `SecretDoor`, `Ground`, `Water`, `Lava`, `Window`, roads `DirtTrack`…`DressedStone`, `Garden`, `BuildingDoor`, `BlindWindow`, `Hole`, `Field`, `Fence`, `FieldGate`, `HerbBed`, `CoveredGround`, `WorldGate` (the floor a World Gate stands on in the Hall of Worlds: walkable floor, its model drawn by `hall_view`), `Grass` (walkable outside ground round an oasis's pools), `Tree` (on grass: outside, seen and lit past, not walkable and not jumpable), `Shallows` (water shallow enough to wade: walkable outside, drawn as water; the wilderness's), `Bridge` (a road over water in the wilderness: a road surface, drawn as boards), `Air` (open space outside a building's upper floor: open, not walkable, not jumpable, not outside, drawn as nothing of its own; not a `Hole`, which is a ruin's rough-edged gap; `shared::floor_sight`), `Ice` (water or shallows frozen by a spell for a while, magic plan PR 11: walkable, not outside, drawn as the floor's tile tinted icy blue; only the server sets it, and puts back what was there); serde, sent in `protocol::LevelChunk`; new ones go at the end): `is_walkable()` (floor, door, ground, covered ground, road, garden, field, gate, herb bed, World Gate), `is_road()`, `is_outside()` (ground, road, garden, field, fence, gate, herb bed: weather falls), `is_liquid()` (water, lava), `is_jumpable()` (liquid, holes and fences: jumped over, see `movement`), `is_open()` (sight and light pass the whole cell: those plus water, lava, windows, holes, fences, trees and air), `is_wall()` (wall, secret door, and a town building's door and window, which are only looks on a wall), `is_opening()` (door, window). These are the cell's own rules, as if its door were open: `Level`'s methods take each door's swing into account.
- `WallShape { north, east, south, west }`: which sides a wall runs out to; `reach()` how far it goes from the centre each way, in `CELL_UNITS` (40 a cell): `WALL_HALF_UNITS` (2: it covers 45–55% of the cell), to the edge where joined, `WALL_END_UNITS` (4: stopping 60% across) past the centre if joined on one side only. A column has no sides.
- `RoomId(u16)`: the planned room a cell is in; in a town, the building, on all its cells.
- `Level`: rectangle of cells (`new(min_x, min_y, width, height)` all wall, `set`, `cell`, `is_open`, `is_walkable`, `wall_shape`, `wall_bars` (where a wall stands, in units: what blocks sight; client `dungeon_map` draws the edge of sight from them), `opening_runs_east_west`, `room`, `set_room`, `contains`, `bounds`, `natural_walls` and `set_natural_walls` (walls wider at the foot, as they block in `clearance`: set from `PoiKind::natural_walls` by `site::Site::level` and the client's `CurrentDungeon::enter`; built unless set)). Its doors (`doors.rs`, `shared::door`): `swing`, `set_swing`, `swings`, `is_shut` (a closed door, or a secret door unless open), `is_wall` (a secret door only while closed), `is_opening` (an open secret door too), `door_bar` (where a closed door stands across its cell, along its wall: blocks sight like a wall's bar), `is_locked`, `set_locked` (the doors known to be locked: the truth on the server, what the player has found by trying on a client; locking closes), `with_doors_open` (a copy with every door open but the locked, for routes through doors opened on the way), `with_all_doors_open` (locked ones too). `is_open` and `is_walkable` are false for a closed door. Outside the rectangle counts as wall, in no room. `test_room()`: 15 × 7 floor cells (east–west × north–south) walled round, centred on `(0, 0)`, with a 1 × 3 wall at `x = -2`, `y = -1..=1` (for tests, and shown before any site is entered).
- `Level`'s herb patches (`herbs.rs`, `herb::HerbPatch` by cell): `herb_at`, `herbs()`, `set_herb`, `remove_herb`, `set_herb_picked`. Put there by the server (beds, the wilderness's), sent in `LevelChunk::herbs`; they block nothing.
- `Level`'s fruit plants (`fruit.rs`, `fruit::FruitPlant` by cell): `fruit_at`, `fruit_plants()`, `set_fruit`, `remove_fruit`, `set_fruit_picked`, `has_trunk` (a plant whose family `has_trunk`: its cell isn't walkable (`is_walkable`), as a wild `Tree`'s, and `clearance` blocks its trunk). Put there by the server (orchards, the wilderness's), sent in `LevelChunk::fruit`.
- `Level`'s felled trees (`felled.rs`, `tree::TreeStage` by cell, woodcutting): `felled_at`, `set_felled` (`None` or `Grown` takes it off), `felled_trees()`, `stands_tree` (a `Tree` cell or trunked fruit plant not felled: what blocks, `clearance`, and what can be felled), `move_cost_per_mille` (furniture's times the stage's, `TreeStage::cost_per_mille`: what moves and routes go by). While felled, a `Tree` cell `is_walkable` and a fruit plant `has_trunk` no more. Set by the server from its saves, sent in `LevelChunk::felled`.
- `Level`'s clouds of steam (`clouds.rs`, magic plan PR 11): `cloud_at`, `set_cloud`, `clouds()`. Lines (`clear_line`, so sight, `line_is_clear` and `spots_are_clear`) pass into a cloud's cell but not through it. Set by the server for a few seconds (`sites/spell_marks.rs`); the client draws them from `SpellNews::Clouds` and doesn't set them on its own level.
- `Level`'s campfires (`campfires.rs`, cooking plan PR 7): `campfire_at(x, y)` (the pieces of kindling left in the fire burning there, 0 for embers), `set_campfire` (`None` takes it off), `campfires()`. Only looks: they never block or change sight. Set by the server from its saves (`shared::campfire::Campfire`), sent in `LevelChunk::campfires`.
- `Level`'s wild trees' kinds (`tree_kinds.rs`, `tree::TreeKind` by cell): `tree_kind_at`, `set_tree_kind` (`None` forgets). Set by the server where it makes a level (`Wilderness::tree_kinds` for a block, `site_tree_kind` for a site's), sent in `LevelChunk::trees` for the client's tree models; the server's felling reads them too.
- `Level`'s vegetable plants (`vegetables.rs`, `vegetable::VegetablePlant` by cell): `vegetable_at`, `vegetable_plants()`, `set_vegetable`, `remove_vegetable`, `set_vegetable_picked`. They never block. Put there by the server (farms' fields of crops, the wilderness's), sent in `LevelChunk::vegetables`.
- `Level`'s fungus patches (`fungi.rs`, `fungus::FungusPatch` by cell): `fungus_at`, `fungus_patches()` (growing now), `kept_fungus_at`, `kept_fungi()` (all kept, for copying levels), `set_fungus`, `remove_fungus`, `set_fungus_picked`. They never block. A patch on a `Cell::Tree` grows only while that tree is felled and `Cleared` (a wood-feeder on a felled tree), so both sides agree from the felled stages. Put there by the server, sent in `LevelChunk::fungi`.
- `Level`'s furniture (`furniture.rs`, `shared::furniture::Piece`): `furniture_at(x, y)`, `furniture()` (by north-west cell), `place_furniture(piece)` (replacing any it overlaps), `remove_furniture(at)`, `furniture_cost_per_mille(x, y)` (1000 with none; a table's 3000, a seat's 1500). Pieces standing on furniture (`Furniture::stands_on_furniture`: a spirit rack on a table, tavern furniture plan PR 2) are kept apart, on top: `furniture_on_top_at(x, y)`, `furniture_on_top()`, `can_stand_on_furniture(piece)` (one piece that `holds_furniture` under all its cells), `remove_on_top(at)`; `place_furniture` puts one only where it can stand, replacing only others on top, and a floor piece replaced or removed takes what stood on it. `furniture_at` and move costs go by the floor piece. Tests in `dungeon/furniture_tests.rs`. Furniture never changes `is_walkable`, `is_open` or sight: it only makes moves dearer (`movement::LevelWay`).
- `Sight`: cells seen, sized to a level. `add_view(level, x, y, radius)` adds what is seen from a cell (`radius` in cells, straight-line between centres, or `None`); calls combine (shared sight, several lights). `add_glimpses(...)`: the same, but open cells count when a line reaches near one of their corners too; not symmetric, so for drawing only, never game rules. `add_view_in` / `add_glimpses_in(..., cone)`: only open cells whose middle is in a `facing::Cone`. `is_seen(x, y)`, `insert(x, y)` (count a cell as seen: used as a set of cells, e.g. remembered or known ones), `reveal(level, x, y)` (an open cell and the walls touching it).

## Depends on

- Nothing.

## Used by

- `shared::site` (generators), `shared::floor_sight`, `server` (`sites`: sight and preloading), client `common` (`CurrentDungeon`), `player` (only walkable cells can be stepped on), `dungeon_map` (sight, light reach, cell colours), `site` (ground leads out).

## Notes

- `CoveredGround` (owner's) looks like `Ground`, but `is_outside()` is false, so no weather or outside light reaches it, and it never leads out to the world map.
- Three kinds of cell so far: walkable and see-through (floor, door, ground), not walkable but see-through (water, lava, window, hole), not walkable and blocking sight (walls, in the thin shape only). A door is the one cell that changes: closed, it blocks movement, sight and light across its whole cell (`door_bar`); open or broken it is as floor. A door whose swing isn't set acts as before doors could close: a door open, a secret door closed. Light uses the same `Sight` with a radius, so it is blocked by exactly the same rule.
- A wall blocks movement in its whole cell, but sight and light only where the wall stands: they pass beside a column or a wall's end. Doors and windows don't block sight anywhere in their cell, even at the frame drawn at their edges (that frame and how it looks are the client's).
- Sight is exact straight lines in integers: deterministic, no floats, and mutual, since a line is the same both ways. Open cells are seen when the line to their centre misses every wall; a wall is seen when an open cell touching it (corners too) is. Cost grows with the open cells times the distance a line gets, so the server only looks from cells it hasn't looked from yet.
- Walls of different buildings (both in a room, different rooms) don't join, so sight passes along the gap between them. Walls elsewhere are in no room.
- Walls join walls, and doors and windows in line with them (`opening_runs_east_west`: the way with more walls beside the opening); touching only at corners, they don't join, so sight passes between them.
- Changing the algorithm changes what players can see: update the golden test deliberately.
