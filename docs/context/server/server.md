# server

The authoritative game simulation. Native only, never in the browser. Clients connect over WebSockets (`network`, which also serves the web client's files on the same port, over TLS unless behind a proxy), or in-process for single player. It runs worlds (each from its secret `WorldSeed`), as many at once as its players are in, and answers clients through `shared::protocol` messages: welcoming them, sending the world map chunks (pois) coming into each player's sight, and running their visits to sites (entering, stairs, leaving) while sending each level's cells as they come into sight. It checks every step a player takes (`shared::movement`) and tells the client where it has them. It saves the world, the sites entered and each character (what they know, where they are) in its own data folder, so a player continues where they left off. Mobs stand on the levels in use, and players are told who is in their sight.

## Notes

- Embedded, the server runs on its own thread ("server") as its own Bevy `App`, so it shares no components or resources with the client: everything goes through the `Link`. The `App` is built on that thread (it can't move between threads). It adds no log plugin (the client's is used; only one can exist per process). When the client hangs up (on quitting) it saves and stops, and the client waits for it; if the process is killed, at most `SAVE_EVERY` of play is lost.
- Saves from before `shared::site::FORGET_BEFORE` are ignored when loaded and saved over: characters start again, sites are made new at the current versions. `world.ron` stays, as the world comes from its seed.
- For now every poi is sent to everyone (`world_map::ALL_POIS_KNOWN`, the owner's choice: a TODO until characters can learn of them). With it off, pois are hidden until known (`world_map::known_poi`): towns are seen by all, the rest only with `KnownPoi::known` or `visited`, or after `DevRevealPois` (this connection only). Nothing sets `known` yet. Entering (`enter_poi`) checks the same thing, as a client could otherwise enter one it was never told of.
- What a character knows (world map chunks seen, with or without a poi: all sent again on connecting, for the player's map; cells of each level seen) and where they are is saved; what the client has been sent is per connection. A site's generator version is fixed when it is first entered (one per site, not per level: a site's levels must agree on their stairs). `changes` are applied but nothing makes any yet; `last_visit` is recorded for resets, which come with spawns. Dev tools sites are saved too (`SiteId::Dev`); Dev Areas only while in use (`SiteId::DevArea`, forgotten on leaving). Mobs on a Dev Area come from `DevArea::spawns`, not `mob::spawns` (`entities/population.rs`).
- Lockdown (owner, 2026-11-10): `connections/lockdown.rs`. A networked server (`ServerOptions::lockable`, the standalone binary) can't go offline as an embedded one does, so it can be locked down instead (`ServerOptions::lockdown`): `login.rs`' `logged_in` turns away accounts without `Permission::ServerOptions` (`LOCKED_DOWN`), and `apply_lockdown` (after `handle_messages`) does what `SetLockdown` asked (`Client::asked_lockdown`, refused in `answer` for an embedded server or anyone without the permission), noting it in the audit log by account id, keeping it (`ServerWorlds::keep_lockdown`, in `server.ron`, so it lasts past a restart) and telling everyone with the permission (`Client::send_lockdown`, also after `Welcome`); while locked it sends away anyone logged in who may not (so a role taken away counts too). `HOW_LOCKDOWN=true` (`main.rs`) starts it locked; otherwise `ServerPlugin` takes what was saved (`ServerWorlds::saved_lockdown`). Tests: `connections/lockdown_tests.rs`.
- Roles: `connections/role.rs` (`Client::may(Permission)`: the host, the embedded server's own player, always as its Owner; anyone else by their role, taken on logging in (`login.rs`, where Banned is turned away with its reason and end); `may_debug(tool, options)`: by role, or `ServerOptions::dev_tools` for all but `DebugTool::admin_only`; `debug_tools` for `Welcome`; `dev_audit.rs` (`note_use`, from `answer`: each permitted, `audited` dev tool used by anyone but the host goes in the audit log by account and character id, `Roles::record`); `badge`, `powers` for `Welcome`; `RoleState`: the account's `Held` and the character's `ShownRole`, choices from `Roles::choices` on `take_role`, the character's saved choice put back by `show_saved` if still allowed (`CharacterSave::shown_as`, `Storage::load_shown_as`), changed by `RoleAsk::ShowAs` (`show_as`), `mark` for `entities` (`Someone::mark` into `EntityLook::mark`; `Client::seen_marks` tells others again when it changes)). Checked for dev tools (each by its own `Permission::Debug`), `SetPaused`, `SetDifficulties` (`ServerOptions`), the idle limit and the player limit. `SetOnline` stays the host's own (it opens its computer's ports). The claim code is taken in `new_character::create`: no character is made, and the client is sent `Refused(CLAIMED)` to go back to the menu.
- The Accounts panel's requests: `connections/role_ask.rs` (`answer_role`: `RoleAsk` checked against `Permission`s, `LookUp` by `Accounts::find_character`, `Give` through `Roles::set_role` with the asker's `Powers` (an account holding a role it can't give, its own or a guest's can't be changed), `FollowDiscord` through `Roles::hand_to_discord`, only when the server checks Discord (`Logins::requires_member`, passed in by `answer`); `Found` also says the role's `source`, whether Discord is linked and `may_follow_discord`; `Audit` by `Roles::audit_page` (`AUDIT_PAGE` 50), `Roles` / `Rename` (`roles/panel.rs`: 1 to 24 characters, written and logged); `refresh_roles` after every client's messages (`Roles::take_recheck`: those playing on an account handed back to Discord are asked about at the next `check_discord`, `DiscordGate::ask_soon`; `Roles::take_changed`): anyone playing on a changed account takes the new role and is told (`RoleAnswer::Changed`), or is sent away if banned). `accounts/find.rs`: `find_character(name)`, `owner_of(character)` (loaded accounts, then every account saved). The embedded server's own player shows as Owner in a look-up of itself, as its role comes from being the host. A renamed role's new name reaches those holding it when they next log in or their role changes.
- Tests that need saving use `TestFolder`; everything else uses `ServerWorld::new` (nothing saved). Runs for checking visuals should set `HOW_DATA` to a scratch folder, so they don't move the owner's character.
- Movement is steps on the world map (`ClientMessage::Step`) and moves on levels and in the Hall (`ClientMessage::Move { to }`, a `Spot`, checked from where the server has the player, never further than `protocol::MOST_MOVE`): the server never takes a position it hasn't checked. Positions on levels are `Spot`s (240 units a cell): `Visit::spot` (exactly), `Visit::position` (its cell, what cell rules, sight, mobs and bags go by), `CharacterWorldSave::hall` (the Hall's own units); saved as spots, older saves' cells read as their middles (`storage/spot_compat.rs`; maps by spot, bags on the ground and remembered ones, are `storage::SpotMap`, reading older saves' cell keys as middles, `storage/spot_map.rs`). Stairs are underfoot within half a cell of their middle (`Visit::stairs_underfoot`, `Spot::stands_on`), shops are dealt with within `shop::DOOR_REACH` of the door (`beside_door`); exits, borders and World Gates still go by the cell the player is in. Steps, moves, and entering and leaving, wait in `Client::waiting` until the character has the energy (`connections::act`, each tick after their messages), up to `MOST_WAITING`, past which steps are dropped and `Position` puts the client right. One `UseAbility` waits there at a time; `WithdrawAbility` takes it out again (the player moved or chose another). Network jitter only delays them. Energy isn't saved: a character joins ready to act. On the world map, water stops steps, and the server's clock and the moons set the tide.
- Sizes and bodies (free positions PR 5): a mob's body is a `Spot` (`Mob::spot`, sent to players; `position` is always the cell under it, `Mob::place` / `put` keep them together; saved in `SavedMob::spot`, older saves' cells as middles), its circle by its kind's `Size` (`Mob::size`). It thinks in cells as before, stepping its body to the next cell's middle by `movement::mob_step_sized` (bigger than Medium only where it fits, slower by the squeeze; one shoved off its middle goes there straight, or back to its own middle first), with distance maps per goal and size (`Maps`). Players move by their kind's size (`Client::size`). Mobs never step where another mob or a player is (`run_mobs`'s `Taken`). A player's move goes past the mobs about by `clearance::crowd_per_mille` (`ServerWorld::crowd_round`: enemies block, the rest are squeezed past slowly; nobody in god mode), the slower of that and any squeeze through a gap charged on the move's energy (in the Hall too); mobs are no longer swapped. One on a cell someone arrives on otherwise moves aside (`move_mob_aside`). Players pass through each other.
- Level chunks carry the furniture, herb patches, fruit plants and vegetable plants on their cells (`sites/cells.rs`); a wilderness window gets each cutout's furniture, bed herbs, orchards and fields of crops moved by its turn round the planet (`sites/wild/window.rs`), and the wilderness's own herbs, vegetables and fruit plants from its blocks.
- Secret doors are sent as walls, unless `Client::truesight` (`level_chunks`); changing it sends a level with any again (`sites::truesight_changed`).
- Learning by carrying: each time `Client::send_inventory` runs it asks `Learned::learn_from` (Collect Water on a first empty bottle, Fell Tree and Cut Kindling on an axe held) and `alchemy::learn_sub_skills` (an apparatus's Alchemy sub-skill on first carrying it), sending the sheet first when anything is new.
- Items' secrets (`shared::item`): everything sent about items goes through `Client::send_inventory` or `Client::as_seen`, which hide what hasn't been found out unless `Client::truesight` (from `DevTruesight`; per connection, not saved). Saves keep the truth. The player's own removals ask `Client::may_take_off` first: moving, binning, giving and dropping. It refuses a curse, marking it found out, but not in god mode. Putting on spends `Swapped::cost` (`connections/wearing.rs`), taking off or binning `Inventory::taking_off_cost`, so what is covered takes longer.
- Territorial mobs (by `Temper`): one that sees a stranger guards (`Doing::Guarding`), standing between them and its home at its territory's edge, or nearer if they are, and stays neutral. Anyone not friendly it sees inside its territory is remembered as trespassing (`Event::Trespassed`), which turns it unfriendly for a while: it then attacks, but only while they are within `LEASH` of its home, and guards again beyond. The leash is from home, not the mob, as someone no faster could never get further from it. Swapping places with a guard is allowed, and puts the player inside, so it counts as a trespass. Its chase ends only by the leash, outlasting the trespass's memory (`TRESPASS_FORGOTTEN_AFTER`), so it may still be going after someone while neutral: `feeling_shown` tells it as `Unfriendly` to them meanwhile (and `crowd_round` blocks with it), back to neutral when it gives up.
- Scent (owner, 2026-11-05): everything that walks on a level leaves a mark on each cell it steps off (who, which way, the action tick, how strong: `shared::mob::scent_strength`), players by their position changing between ticks (`Population::last_at`), mobs after acting; taking stairs marks the stairs with where they lead (`saw_stairs_taken`). Nobody in god mode leaves any. Each creature keeps only its latest mark per cell; `run_mobs` sweeps marks past `SCENT_KEPT_FOR` every 10 s. A chasing mob that loses someone where it last saw them starts `Tracking` if its kind can smell: it goes the way their mark on its cell says, or to the freshest mark beside it newer than the last it followed (so never back), back to `Going` on seeing them, `Following` at stairs if its kind uses them, and settles when the trail is gone or too old for its nose. Marks are saved with the level (`LevelSave::scents`, swept on loading) whenever they have changed at a save, so active levels' saves change with every step; marks left after the last save of a level that is let go are lost.
- God mode (`Client::god_mode`, from `DevGodMode`; per connection, not saved): left out of `run_mobs`' `spots`, so no mob notices them and one already after them gives up, and mobs don't follow them on stairs. They still count as standing there and seeing (`Taken`, a mob going home unseen). Enemies swap with them like anyone else.
- Mobs and players: each player's `Visit` still has its own copy of the level, as only players' changes differ, and only who is there is shared. A player's id for others (`Client::entity_id`) is their character's, so it is the same when they come back. Other players on the world map are seen within `WORLD_MAP_SIGHT`. A level stays in use while any mob on it isn't at rest, so a chasing mob wanders off and goes home rather than waiting at the stairs. Mobs go by the `ActionClock`, so pausing stops them. A mob after someone who takes stairs in its sight follows if its kind `uses_stairs` (`saw_stairs_taken`, `Doing::Following`, `cross`), carrying the way back, and never onto the world map. A level stays while any of its mobs is away, so they can return. Those not at rest are noted in `LevelSave::unsettled` with each save (`save_mobs`) and carry on when the level is next used; one on another level starts again from home. A level let go of at rest starts them at home, with the same ids.
- A character's saved looks are fitted to its gender as they load (`Storage::character`: a female's beard from before 2026-11-13 dropped). A joining character's `Sheet` is what was saved (with attributes worked out from a centred build for a save from before the nine, `Sheet::settled`), what `CreateCharacter` asked for (its `Build` checked, the attributes worked out here), or plain for their kind (`start_sheet`). Attributes never reach anyone else. A mob's speed goes by its rolled attributes.
- A character without `Inventory::outfitted` gets their first clothes on joining (`give_first_clothes`), if human, and first a full lantern, two bottles of Neutral Oil and a gold coin (`starting_kit.rs`, `give_starting_kit`; owner: new characters only, those made before never get them; the gold is for testing trades, to be lowered: trading plan).
- Other levels: on coming into a site (`enter_site`, also on resuming), `send_other_levels` sends each other level the character remembers as a `RememberedLevel`, generating it with its changes. Taking stairs sends none: the client keeps what it had. In a building, what is seen of other floors from the player's (`other_floors.rs`) follows as more `RememberedLevel`s, after the level's own cells. Each `RememberedLevel` carries its level's `walls` (`SiteLevel::walls`), so the client draws the ground floor's ramparts from other floors.
- Towns: the whole level is sent on entering, marked `remembered`, after `Buildings`; nothing goes into the character's memory, and `update_sight` only sends cells that have changed since (no sight is worked out).
- A saved world from world map version 5 carries on (6 only turned some desert pois into oases); older ones were wiped with `OLDEST_SAVE_FORMAT` 4 (2026-11-06) and are refused.
- Sites and generator changes: a site keeps the version it was first made with, so a bump alone changes nothing while that version can run. It changes when the site is forgotten (`ServerWorld::forget_site`, `forget_sites`, from `DevForgetMapOnServer`, which delete the files) or its version can't run any more (`make_site` makes it anew). `CharacterWorldSave::site_versions` records the version each character knew: entering one that differs drops their memory of it, and coming back inside one (`resume`) puts them out at the poi (`OnWorldMap`) to go in again. Anyone else inside when a site is forgotten is put out the same way by `handle_messages`; whoever asked for it stays, arriving on the new map on the same level and cell if they can. A world map model always shows the current generator, so it can differ from an older site until that is forgotten.
- Levels are generated again on every visit (and the other level of each staircase, to work out its way down); cheap so far.
- Saves out of reach (`LoadError::CantReach`, say a locked file): `Client::refuse_for_saves` logs it, sends `Refused` and disconnects, whether logging in, joining or going into a site. Nothing is put aside or started again, and a client refused while joining is never `Playing`, so nothing blank is saved over the real character. Only `Broken` starts again. The world itself, either way, stops the server.
- Writing happens on the save thread, so a tick only pays for cloning the save. Stopping: `stop_when_asked` calls `Storage::finish_writing`, and the final clock save goes through the queue as `ServerWorld` is dropped, which the last handle's drop waits for. Tests that look at files must `finish_writing` (or drop) first.
- A plan for Postgres, not built: `docs/context/postgres-migration.md`.
- Uses the filesystem (saves), one reason the web client must not contain it. Its data folder is its own, never shared with a client's (a client's data will go beside it, in `Hall of Worlds/client`).
- Pausing (single player, while the system menu is open): only the clock stops. Ticks, saves and pings carry on, and saving while paused saves the stopped time. Anything added later that goes by game time, such as mobs, must check it. A server opened to other players must turn `pausable` off and carry on.
- Latency is only known on the network thread and only logged, for now. A client can look slower than it is by answering late, so it must never earn anything. Handing it to the game (say, to scale `LEVEL_PRELOAD_STEPS`) is for when something needs it.
- In Docker it is the only process: it serves the web client and the game on port 24691, also on 24690 with `HOW_BEHIND_PROXY=true`, and only there with `HOW_DISABLE_SSL=true` as well (`Dockerfile`).
- A browser checks certificates itself and warns about a self-signed one, which a page can't change. The embedded server never uses TLS: it has no network.
- Characters over the network belong to accounts: guests', and those logged in through a provider (`oidc`) or Discord. No email address is kept, only its `EmailKey` (Discord's id is kept, with its `DiscordKey`, and Discord is never asked for an address); it reaches the client once, in `LoggedIn` ("<name> on Discord" for Discord), for showing in its menus. `HOW_DISCORD_ONLY` (`Logins::discord_only`): `main.rs` lets no guests in, and `login.rs` takes a session only for an account with a Discord id. Membership (`HOW_DISCORD_SERVER_ID`, `connections/login/member.rs`): `let_in` replaces `logged_in` plus `tell_discord` for provider and session logins; for an account following Discord with a Discord id, the login waits on `Client::discord` (`DiscordGate { user, waiting, asked, must_link }`) for the bot's answer, taken by the `check_discord` system (after `finish_logins`), which also asks again about everyone in every `CHECK_EVERY` (5 minutes). An answer sets the Discord role (`Roles::follow_discord`; `refresh_roles` then tells or sends away anyone playing); not a member: turned or sent away (`NOT_A_MEMBER`) unless `in_without_discord`; `Unknown` (Discord out of reach): let in as they are, playing ones left alone; `Refused` (the bot refused): waiting logins turned away (`CANT_CHECK`), playing ones left alone. Without a Discord id: `DiscordLink::MustLink` sent before `Characters` and `ChooseCharacter` / `CreateCharacter` refused (`LINK_FIRST`) until linked (a client without the screens is refused at once); a finished link starts the checks (`check_linked`). No guests while required (`main.rs`). Tests in `connections/login/member_tests.rs` against the fake provider's bot route. Link Discord (`connections/login/link_discord.rs`): after any login while the server has Discord, a non-guest account is told `DiscordLink::CanLink` or `Linked` (`tell_discord`); `ClientMessage::LinkDiscord` (from Character Select, or any time logged in) begins Discord's login tied to the account (`Logins::begin_link`, the state on `Client::login_state`, used once), and coming back (`finish_logins`, for the client logged in to that account in any phase) adds the Discord login to the account and notes its id (`finish_link`), or says `Failed` if that Discord user already leads to another account, or the account has another Discord user (no merging). Either login then opens the same account. Tests in `connections/login/link_discord_tests.rs`: linking then both logins, refusal, a failed link tried again, guests and servers without Discord. Logs name accounts by id and characters by id (`Client::in_log`), never by name or address; anything that crosses between the two (such as future chat) logs both. When an account connects again, the older connection is refused ("playing somewhere else") and saved first, then the new one loads the character: `handle_messages` welcomes a client only once its character is decided (`join`), stopping its messages there for that tick. So an account plays one character at a time. The embedded server's own client has no login: it is logged in to the account of `LoginKey::SinglePlayer`, whose characters it chooses between like anyone's.
- A character belongs to the server, not a world: its file says which world they are in and holds what they know of each one. Joining (`connections::join`) opens that world (`open_saved`); someone new, or whose world has no save it can run, is put in the home world (logged), and one whose world's save can't be reached is refused. `Client::world` is the world they are in (in the Hall, the one they came from). The systems over every connection go world by world (`connections::systems::for_each_world`, which sorts the clients by world), so players only see, and are told the time of, their own world; everyone in the Hall sees each other whatever world they came from (`entities::in_hall`).
- When embedded in the client, the client's full Bevy features are active too (Cargo unifies features); that's fine.
- Field of view: `entities::sync` tells a player only who is in their `Cone` (and in the line of sight it already checked), and `tell` sends `Turned` for turns a stretch doesn't explain (`Client::seen_headings`); `sites::update_sight` remembers cells by `add_glimpses_in` (preloading stays all round), and Observation's `further` adds to the reach where there is one (`Visit::further`). `notice` needs the player in the mob's cone. Abilities and `mob_in_sight` still go by the line alone (the ability turns the player first). The world map has no cone.
