# Items

The things you can find, buy, wear and carry: armour, clothes and shoes worn in layers, jewellery, herbs, fruit and nuts, vegetables, pulses and grains, mushrooms, fungi and lichens, weapons, casks, vessels to drink from and pour, yeast, and logs and kindling. Back to [Gameplay](gameplay).

## Quality

Most items have a quality, from worst to best: Poor, Common, Uncommon, Rare, Epic, Legendary and Artifact. Its name is shown in its quality's colour.

Weapons, armour, clothes, shoes and jewellery get one bonus for each step of quality above Common, for as long as you wear or hold them: one on an Uncommon item, up to five on an Artifact. Each bonus is to something different, and suits the item:

- **Weapons:** Strength, Agility, Perception, speed, defending, how hard your blows hit, light, and levels in the skills for that weapon (a sword's own skill, swords, defending with a sword, or Melee).
- **Armour and clothes:** what suits the place they are worn, such as Toughness, Health and taking less harm on the body, Perception, Darkvision and Mana on the head, and Agility and speed on the legs and feet. Armour can give levels in Defence, and anything on the feet levels in Kick.
- **Rings and necklaces:** the mind and spirit, such as Intellect, Will, Aura, Mana, Darkvision and resisting magic.

A skill level is one percentage point of the skill. Now and then one bonus on an item with two or more is a penalty instead, and another bonus on it is twice as strong to make up for it. Artifacts and items that belong to a set never have a penalty, and their bonuses are twice as strong.

An item's popup lists its bonuses, each with what it does: "+5% Strength: carrying and how hard you hit".
