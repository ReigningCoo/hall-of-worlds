# Yeast

Yeast is live starter in a small stoneware crock, for fermenting drinks. Herbalists sell it, nearly always of Common quality. Each crock weighs about 250 g and takes up one cell. Back to [Items](items).

## Keeping it

Yeast has to be fed with flour. Fed between 12 and 24 hours after its last feed, it stays as it is; left unfed, it loses a step of quality every 24 hours, down to Poor, and a Poor yeast stays Poor. Its clock runs while you are playing and it is in your pack, and stops while you are logged out; on the ground it runs all the time.

Several crocks stack, each keeping its own clock. The popup lists them, the one that needs feeding soonest first: "Feed in 7 h", or once a feed is due, "Feed now: weakens in 5 h".

Between feeds, a yeast can start one thing without being used up; the popup says when it has been used since it was fed. Feeding, and fermenting with it, are coming.

## Fermenting

Keeping yeast and fermenting drinks practise the **Fermenting** skill, under Crafting.
