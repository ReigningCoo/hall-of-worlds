# Yeast

Yeast is live starter in a small stoneware crock, for fermenting drinks. Herbalists sell it, nearly always of Common quality. Each crock weighs about 250 g and takes up one cell. Back to [Items](items).

## Keeping it

Yeast has to be fed with flour and fresh water. To feed it, let go of a stack of flour on it while you carry fresh water, in bottles or in a vessel such as a jug or waterskin (not salt water). Each feed takes a sack of flour and a bottle's worth (100 ml) of water. Feeding a stack feeds every yeast in it that can be fed, the one that needs it most first, until the flour or water runs out.

When you feed it matters:

- **Under 12 hours** after its last feed, it splits into two yeasts just alike. That takes flour and water for both, and an empty bottle for the second. Splitting uses it up until its next feed on time, so it can't split again until it has gone 12 hours and been fed on time.
- **Between 12 and 24 hours**, it is fed on time, and slowly gets better: each feed on time adds to its progress, more the better your **Fermenting** skill, and the more progress it has, the more likely a feed is to raise its quality a step. Better yeasts take longer.
- **Later than 24 hours**, it has already lost a step of quality for every 24 hours unfed, down to Poor, and a Poor yeast stays Poor until it is fed back up.

Its clock runs while you are playing and it is in your pack, and stops while you are logged out; on the ground it runs all the time.

Several crocks stack, each keeping its own clock. The popup lists them, the one that needs feeding soonest first: "Feed in 7 h", or once a feed is due, "Feed now: weakens in 5 h".

Between feeds, a yeast can start one thing without being used up; the popup says when it has been used since it was fed, and any feed makes it ready again. See [Drinks](drinks) for fermenting with it.

## Starting your own

Let go of flour on a bottle of fresh water to begin a starter of your own. The bottle becomes a crock of Poor yeast, which takes 24 hours on its clock to be ready ("Starter: ready in 9 h"); after that, feed it on time to make it better.

## Fermenting

Feeding yeast, starting starters and fermenting drinks practise the **Fermenting** skill, under Crafting.
