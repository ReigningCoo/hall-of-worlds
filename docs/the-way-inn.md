# The Way Inn

The Way Inn is where every new character begins, and a place to meet for everyone who travels the Hall of Worlds. Back to [Gameplay](gameplay).

## The legend

Nobody remembers the inn being built. It has always been here, somewhere off the Hall of Worlds, between the worlds and belonging to none of them. Travellers from every world stop here to rest, drink and meet. A magic in its walls stops all fighting inside: you keep your weapons, but no blow can be struck.

## Starting here

A new character starts in the inn's main room, just inside the archway from the entrance. The inn's door opens onto the Hall of Worlds: a marble walkway hanging in a void, with a gate to a different world every few steps along both sides. The first time you leave, you come out somewhere along the Hall, a place of its own for each character.

## Finding it again

The way in is hidden. Step off either edge of the Hall's walkway halfway between two gates on that side, or click or tap there, and you walk into the inn's entrance. Stepping off anywhere else does nothing. The marble looks a little worn in front of each way in, if you look closely.

Every way in leads to the same inn, whichever world you came from. Leaving through the entrance door takes you back to the place in the Hall you came in from.

## Inside

- **The main room**: tables, booths along one side and private rooms along the other, fireplaces, and the staircase up in the middle.
- **The bar**: at the counter, a drinking vessel you carry is filled from the wine, spirits and casks stored behind it. Drinks are free, and the racks fill up again by themselves.
- **The kitchen, brewing and ageing rooms**: through the doors at either end of the back wall, with hearths, an alchemy bench and casks to cook, brew and ferment with.
- **Your own room**: upstairs, a bedroom that is yours alone, with a chest and a wardrobe. What you leave in it is kept, and it is the same room whichever world you came from. Nobody else sees you in it.

The inn is shared with up to 30 other travellers at a time; when it is full, a new copy of it opens. Anything left lying in its public rooms is cleared away after a couple of minutes, so don't leave things on the floor.
