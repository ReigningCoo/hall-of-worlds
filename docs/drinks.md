# Drinks

Ale, cider, perry and wines are fermented in [casks](casks) with [yeast](yeast), using the **Ferment** ability. A game day is one real hour, so a cask of cider takes about a day and a half of real time.

## What ferments

| Made from | Drink |
| --------- | ----- |
| Barley | Ale |
| Any other grain | Grain ale, such as Wheat Ale |
| Apples | Cider |
| Pears | Perry |
| Grapes | Wine |
| Any other fruit | Fruit wine, such as Plum Wine |

A cask can hold juice or cordial (see [Vessels and pouring](vessels)), which ferments as it is, or water, with fruit or grain mashed into it. Whole fruit makes a drink a step worse than its juice would, and so does cordial. One apple in a cask of water does nothing: it takes plenty of fruit or grain for the drink to reach even 1%.

## Fermenting a cask

Use **Ferment** and choose:

- **Cask**: one you carry, or one lying beside you, holding juice, cordial or fresh water. Sea water won't do.
- **Add**: for a cask of water, the fruit or grain you carry. All of that stack goes in.
- **Yeast**: a stack of yeast you carry, and how many from it. Each yeast is used once until it is fed again, but isn't used up.

The panel says what it will make, how strong, how sweet, how good and how long it will take. The cask is then sealed until it is ready, and nothing can be poured into or out of it.

- **Strength** comes from the sugar in it, up to what the yeast stands: about 9% for a Common yeast, more for better ones. Sugar the yeast can't turn into alcohol leaves the drink sweet.
- **Time**: about 24 game days for ale, 36 for cider and perry, 90 for wines, in a Pin with one Common yeast. Bigger casks take longer; better yeast, or more of it, is quicker.
- **Quality** follows the yeast's. A pine cask spoils it a little.

Its popup says "Fermenting: ready in 12 days". Fermenting practises the **Fermenting** skill.

## Clocks

A cask on the ground goes by the world's time, whether or not anyone is near. One in your pack goes by the time you spend playing, and stops while you are logged out.

## Ageing

A sealed cask keeps for ever. A drink left sealed in oak gets better with time, up to a point: a step for ale, cider and perry, two for fruit wines, three for wine. Cherry wood helps a little, slowly; pine makes it worse. Chestnut and acacia change nothing.

## Vinegar

Once a cask is opened, its drink starts to turn to vinegar. Stronger drinks keep longer: about 12 game days at 5%, about 42 at 12%, and anything of 20% or more keeps for ever. The popup says "Turns to vinegar in 9 days", and you are told when it is close. Pouring vinegar into a drink makes it turn sooner.

Opened juice or cordial doesn't go off: left 2 game days, it ferments by itself into a Poor cider or fruit wine.

## Drinking

Drink from a tankard, cup or other vessel a serving (250 ml) at a time, or a bottle all at once, as you would juice. A drink restores 2 Mana a second for 30 seconds a serving, twice what juice does, but it also has alcohol in it, counted in units (10 ml of pure alcohol each): a serving of 5% ale is about one and a quarter.

Units wear off at about one every 10 minutes for a person of 70 kg, by real time, while you are logged out too. Bigger, heavier bodies wear them off faster and take more to feel them.

| Stage | Units (70 kg) | What it does |
| ----- | ------------- | ------------ |
| Tipsy | 3 | A third slower |
| Drunk | 7 | Two thirds slower; Intellect, Perception and Will lower; you stumble now and then |
| Very drunk | 12 | As drunk but worse, Agility lower too, stumbling more; on getting there you collapse for a minute and can't do anything |
| Alcohol poisoning | 20 | As very drunk, losing Health (never your last point); you collapse on getting there |
| Hangover | | After being drunk, once you are no longer tipsy, until half an hour after the last unit wears off: Intellect, Perception and Will a little lower |

Being slowed by drink adds to being slowed by what you carry. A stumble sends a step off to one side and stops you there, so you have to set off again. The stage you are at shows at the top of the window, with how long it lasts.

Drinking while tipsy or worse practises the **Tolerance** skill, which raises every stage's threshold, up to twice as high.

## Distilling

Drinks distilled in an Alembic become pure alcohol, the solvent for tinctures: see [Alchemy](alchemy#pure-alcohol). Pure alcohol can't be drunk.
