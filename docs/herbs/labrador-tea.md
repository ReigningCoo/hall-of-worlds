# Labrador tea

![Labrador tea]({{ '/assets/herbs/labrador_tea.png' | relative_url }})

*Rhododendron groenlandicum*. Knee-high shrub; narrow leathery leaves with rusty wool beneath, round clusters of white flowers. Back to [Herbs](../herbs).
