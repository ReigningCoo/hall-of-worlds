# Parsley

![Parsley]({{ '/assets/herbs/parsley.png' | relative_url }})

*Petroselinum crispum*. Low clump of bright green, deeply cut leaves on long stalks; flat umbels of greenish-yellow flowers in its second year. Back to [Herbs](../herbs).
