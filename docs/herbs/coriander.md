# Coriander

![Coriander]({{ '/assets/herbs/coriander.png' | relative_url }})

*Coriandrum sativum*. Soft plant with broad lobed lower leaves, feathery upper ones and small umbels of white to pale pink flowers. Back to [Herbs](../herbs).
