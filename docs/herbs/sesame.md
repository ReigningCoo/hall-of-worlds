# Sesame

![Sesame]({{ '/assets/herbs/sesame.png' | relative_url }})

*Sesamum indicum*. Upright stem of long leaves with pale bell flowers in the leaf joints, then upright ribbed pods of white seeds. Back to [Herbs](../herbs).
