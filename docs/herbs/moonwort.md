# Moonwort

![Moonwort]({{ '/assets/herbs/moonwort.png' | relative_url }})

*Botrychium lunaria*. A single fleshy frond of half-moon leaflets beside a spike of spore clusters; exaggerate it. Back to [Herbs](../herbs).
