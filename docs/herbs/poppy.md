# Poppy

![Poppy]({{ '/assets/herbs/poppy.png' | relative_url }})

*Papaver somniferum*. Tall grey-green stems of clasping leaves; large papery pale mauve flowers, then round heads of tiny blue seeds. Back to [Herbs](../herbs).
